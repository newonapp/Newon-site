Who it is for

Key features
Work automation · shared schedule · operations report
How it is adopted
Being prepared as a subscription bundle.

Seller solution

A bundle that cuts repeated online-selling work.

Who it is for
Online sellers and small brands
Problem it solves
Time spent on orders, questions and stock
Key features
Order operations · customer replies · sales summary

Planned as a shared product for sellers. Actual trading will happen in apps such as SHAREON.
StatusIn planning

Company operations solution

A company set-up that reduces department-level repetition.

Who it is for
Companies with recurring reports, approvals and handoffs
Problem it solves
Manual links between departments and recurring reports
Key features
Workflow rules · data reports · AI work support
How it is adopted
Being prepared as a company configuration and licence of the shared products.
StatusIn preparation · adoption talks open
