PETLOG

Every moment with your pet,
in one connection.

PETLOG aims to be an all-in-one pet life platform that connects pets' daily life, health, growth and care. Starting from records, it connects shopping, secondhand trading, group buying, booking, community, education, travel and partnerships with businesses and institutions.

Explore PETLOG → Partnership inquiry

Today the PETLOG app focuses on records and health. Shopping, secondhand trading, group buying and booking are in preparation or review; this website only introduces them.

OUR VISION

Starting from records,
connecting all of pet life.

  1. 01

    Life with a pet is scattered across records, health, shopping, care and community.

  2. 02

    PETLOG puts the records you keep every day at the centre and aims to connect the information and services you need in one flow.

  3. 03

    We start with dogs and cats and will expand to other pets.

  4. 04

    Owners' records and personal data belong to owners. They are never shared with sellers or businesses without consent.

02

Shopping and trading

Booking and care

Community and learning

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES

Business across the pet industry

PETLOG is designed as a platform for both individual users and businesses.

01

Personal subscription

Core records stay free; a subscription for advanced features is under review.

02

Trading and group buying

Discovery happens in PETLOG; trading, payment and settlement are planned to link with SHAREON.

03

Booking and services

Booking links to pet service providers will be reviewed step by step after legal and safety review.

04

Businesses and institutions

Provider SaaS, automation, employee benefits and institutional licences are under review.
