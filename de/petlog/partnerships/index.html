PRINCIPLES

Partnership principles

  • Animal welfare and respect for life.
  • Owners' records and personal data are never given to partners without consent.
  • Care, hotel and grooming providers are checked before being connected.
  • Veterinary and insurance law is followed; insurance partnerships only with properly licensed businesses.
  • Partnerships are shown accurately; no one is shown as a partner before a written agreement.
  • Public-interest information from official bodies is kept distinct from commercial partnerships.
  • Terms are set transparently, by written agreement after discussion.
  • Sellers are checked before selling, and regulated items such as animal drugs are not handled.

PROCESS

How it works

The areas shown are the kinds of partners PETLOG wants to work with, not signed partnerships. These steps are for orientation; nothing is signed automatically.

  1. 01

    Proposal

    Send your proposal through the form. Including a company introduction and the scope you have in mind speeds up review.

  2. 02

    Fit review

    We review fit, legal and safety requirements, and business or seller eligibility.

  3. 03

    Scope

    Agree scope, roles, timing and how owners' data is protected.

  4. 04

    Terms and agreement

    Review the terms and sign what was agreed in a written agreement.

  5. 05

    Start

    Start on the agreed terms and review together as it runs.
