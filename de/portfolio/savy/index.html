SAVY

Ausgaben · Einnahmen · Abonnements · KI-Analyse

A smart finance app that brings scattered spending, income, and subscriptions together—and helps you trim waste.

Projekt ansehen App Store Google Play

Idee

SAVY is

a spending tracker that helps you record and review your money flow quickly—without a heavy household ledger.

Log daily expenses and income in seconds, and keep recurring subscriptions visible in one place.

SAVY is more than a ledger: it organizes your spending patterns and supports healthier money habits.

Wichtige Funktionen

Spesenprotokollierung

Enter what you spent today in a few taps Lightweight inputs make it easy to stay consistent

Einkommensprotokollierung

Track salary, side income, allowance, and more See the full in/out picture more accurately

Abonnementverwaltung

Organize recurring charges and subscriptions Catch easy-to-miss repeating expenses

Ausgabenanalyse

Category totals, monthly trends, and patterns Spot where money leaks out

Diagramme und Statistiken

Visualize income, spending, and balance flow Make the numbers intuitive at a glance

Notifications

Renewal dates, budget alerts, logging nudges Stay on top of important money moments

Einblicke in die KI-Ausgaben

Analyze patterns from your logs Get ideas on where you can save
