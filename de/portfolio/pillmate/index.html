Pillmate

Med-Erinnerungen · Gesundheitsprotokolle · Familienfreigabe · Tagesablauf

Record and track daily medications, supplements, and health routines in one place.

Projekt ansehen App Store Google Play

Idee

Pillmate helps you manage daily pills and supplements by time of day and check them off quickly.

Split doses into morning, lunch, evening, and bedtime, and use reminders so important times are not missed.

Wichtige Funktionen

Der heutige Dosischeck

See today's pills at a glance Log doses quickly as you take them

Tageszeitpläne

Morgens · Mittagessen · Abends · Schlafenszeit Keep routines easy to scan

Dose reminders

Reminders at set times Guidance so important doses are not missed

Dosisverlauf

Review by date See consistency at a glance

Medication notes

Name, how to take, amount Keep what you need in one place

Family care

Parents, kids, household routines Support the people you look after

Home-Widget
