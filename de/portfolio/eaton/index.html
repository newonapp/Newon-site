EatOn

Cooking · Dining out · Delivery · AI recipes

From popular recipes to notable restaurants, delivery picks, and ingredient shopping. Explore YouTube, Instagram, blogs, and restaurant mentions in one place—and use AI to review food info faster.

App Store Google Play

Idee

EatOn is

not just another recipe or restaurant app.

When you know what you want to eat, you shouldn’t have to hop between apps to cook it, dine out, or order delivery.

In EatOn you can explore popular recipes, notable places, delivery options, and ingredients together.

Wichtige Funktionen

Popular recipes

Browse popular dishes and explore multiple recipes for the menu you want. Find what you want to cook today, faster.

Unified recipe browse

Compare cooking content from YouTube, Instagram, and blogs in one place. See one dish prepared many ways.

AI recipe summary

Paste a recipe link and AI summarizes the essentials. Name · ingredients · time · steps · difficulty · tips

Ingredient shopping

Review ingredients for a recipe and browse products across linked shopping channels. Explore reasonably priced options—no lowest-price guarantee.

Dining out

Explore restaurants mentioned across TV, YouTube, SNS, and Blue Ribbon–style sources. Source examples: TV · YouTube · SNS · Blue Ribbon

Delivery discovery

Explore popular delivery menus and places by area and category. Quickly check menus people are looking for today.

Unified food search

Search one dish and connect related cooking, recipes, dining, delivery, and shopping. e.g. kimchi jjigae → recipes · YouTube · Instagram · blogs · ingredients · shopping · places · delivery
