SHAREON Overview — Nicht besitzen müssen.
Genau dann, wenn man es braucht.

A way to use what you need sensibly, and to add new value to assets you are not using. SHAREON aims to be an open marketplace that connects people and businesses to share and rent physical assets.

WHAT IS SHAREON

Trading the right to use,
not ownership.

SHAREON is a sharing and rental platform for people and businesses. Goods, equipment and spaces are not bought and sold — they are used for the period needed, without transfer of ownership. Local person-to-person trades and nationwide business rentals sit on one platform.

  • 01

    Things you rarely use still had to be bought

    Camping gear, filming kits, event supplies and baby gear are usually bought even when needed only briefly, and then just take up space. SHAREON eases this with a flow for borrowing for the period needed and giving it back.

  • 02

    Lending between people leaves promises and responsibility scattered

    With noticeboard posts or lending between friends, the schedule, the condition at handover and return, and what happens when something goes wrong are all left to the people involved. SHAREON ties booking requests, handover and return checks, trade records, reviews, and reporting and disputes into one flow.

  • 03

    Business equipment and spaces often sit idle

    Rental stock, company equipment, meeting rooms, studios and event spaces spend long periods unused. SHAREON gives businesses a way to meet new customers while keeping how they already operate.

The six steps of one rental

  1. 01 Explore

    Explore goods and spaces

    Find goods, equipment and spaces by field, area and period.

  2. 02 Details

    Check the details

    Check components, condition, facilities and house rules.

  3. 03 Terms

    Check schedule and terms

    Check the dates, handover method and terms such as a deposit.

  4. 04 Request

    Book or request

    Request your dates and get the provider’s confirmation.

  5. 05 Handover

    Handover, use and return

    Both sides check the condition at handover and at return.

  6. 06 Review

    Record and review

    The trade is recorded and closed with reviews.

SHAREON connects exploring, booking requests, handover, use and return, and trade records and reviews in one flow. Services are used through the mobile app.

CORE VALUES

Three values
SHAREON keeps.

Before the number of fields, we think first about trades that people and businesses meeting for the first time can lend and return with confidence.

  1. Only as much as you need

    Instead of buying, you borrow the right to use something for the period needed. Lenders set their own terms and available dates; borrowers check them before requesting.

  2. From local to nationwide

    Hand-to-hand trades between people nearby and business rentals that reach customers across the country share one platform. Exact addresses and contact details are handled only as far as a trade needs.

  3. A rental ends when it comes back

    A rental is not over until the item is back. Handover and return checks, trade records, reviews, and reporting and dispute handling are the basics of every trade.

TRADE STRUCTURES

Four trade types where
people and businesses meet.

C2C, B2C, C2B and B2B are trade types running from the lender to the borrower. SHAREON handles all four with the same booking, handover and return flow.

Lender ↓Borrower →

Person

Business

Person

Business

  • C2C

    Person → person

    One person borrows goods or equipment another person owns, for the period needed — from hand-to-hand trades nearby to rentals across the country, in one flow.

  • C2B

    Person → business

    A business borrows an individual’s goods, such as a camera kit, for work, an event or a project — a rental with an individual as supplier.

  • B2C

    Business → person

    Rental businesses, space operators and companies with equipment lend goods, equipment and spaces to individual customers. Businesses take part after business verification.

  • B2B

    Business → business

    A business lends equipment, assets or spaces to another business. Bulk and long-term rentals follow terms agreed between the businesses.

  • Space Sharing

    Space sharing

    Lend and borrow idle spaces — meeting rooms, studios, rehearsal rooms, pop-up stores, party rooms — by the hour or the day, with facilities, capacity, opening hours and house rules checked.

  • Free Sharing

    Free lending

    Lending without a rental fee. Trust tools such as handover and return checks and reviews follow the same flow as paid rentals.

OUR ROLE

What SHAREON does,
and does not do.

Clear roles are the first condition for rentals between people and businesses meeting for the first time.

What we do

  • Connect goods, equipment and spaces listed by people and businesses with those who need them

  • A trade structure tying booking requests, handover and return checks, trade records, reviews, reporting and disputes into one flow

  • Participation after identity verification for individuals and business verification for businesses

  • A listing structure that respects businesses’ existing products and ways of running rentals

What we do not do

  • Run a rental company that buys everything to rent out

  • Trades centred on second-hand sales or general shopping, where ownership changes hands

  • Treat deposits as platform revenue or deduct them without grounds

  • Decide damage liability automatically from handover and return photos alone

LONG-TERM VISION

Goods and spaces,
and business assets too.

SHAREON aims for a sharing and rental ecosystem where the assets people and businesses own are used more often by those who need them.

  1. 01

    Sharing goods and equipment

    From home, digital, filming, camping and baby gear to industrial equipment — widening the choice to borrow instead of buying things needed now and then.

  2. 02

    Sharing spaces

    Connecting the idle hours of work, shoot, rehearsal and gathering spaces with the people and businesses who need them.

  3. 03

    Using business assets

    Widening business-to-business rentals and partnerships so individuals and other businesses can use the equipment and spaces companies and institutions own.

GLOBAL VISION

A rental structure built at home,
taken to wider markets.

SHAREON’s long-term direction is a global sharing and rental platform that connects the goods, equipment and spaces people and businesses own with those who need them. No countries or dates have been set.

See the global direction →

  1. The same trade structure

    Whatever the country, the basics of a rental stay the same: explore, request, hand over and return, record and review.

  2. Different lives and rules in each country

    Fields suited to local housing and rental demand, and trading that fits local payment methods, currencies and consumer-protection rules, are designed country by country.

  3. With local partners

    Operating models for each country are built with local rental businesses, space operators and logistics and payment providers.
