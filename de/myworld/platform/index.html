PRIVACY & TRUST

Privacy, security
and trust principles.

Travel records and locations describe a person’s life. These principles come first whenever features expand.

  1. 01

    Only what is needed

    Travel records and location data are processed only as far as a feature needs, and the rules are published in the privacy policy.

  2. 02

    Export & delete

    Countries and trips you record should be exportable and restorable, and how to delete your account is public.

  3. 03

    No public real-time location

    Location sharing during a trip goes only to group members who explicitly agree, and is never shown on public profiles.

  4. 04

    Minimal sensitive documents

    Passport and ID copies are not collected by default; any storage would come with encryption, access control and deletion.

  5. 05

    Nothing passed to partners

    Individual routes, passports and bookings are never provided to or sold to partners without permission.

  6. 06

    No automatic sharing between apps

    Travel, family, health and location information is never shared automatically with other NEWON apps without consent.

  7. 07

    Check AI information

    Visa and entry rules, prices and opening hours shown by AI are not confirmed; we point users to official sources.

  8. 08

    Ads and reviews kept apart

    Ads and partnerships are labelled, booked reviews are separated from general stories, and fake reviews can be reported.

Frequently asked questions
about the service.

  1. Q1Are all 25 areas in the app?

    No. The 25 areas are a long-term plan. Today the app centres on the world map, travel records and stats; itinerary, expenses, the AI report, subscription and multiple languages are partly available. The rest is planned or under review.

  2. Q2Will world-map colouring stay free?

    Yes. Keeping world-map colouring and basic visit records free is a MY WORLD principle.

  3. Q3How is the AI travel report made?

    It shows your travel style and a summary based on the records you leave. Today it analyses record data using defined rules; conversational itinerary generation is under review.

  4. Q4Can I book flights or stays in the app?

    Not yet. After partner contracts and legal review, we will first consider linking to partners’ official booking pages.

  5. Q5Can I share records with other travelers?

    A community and group trips shared with other users are planned after server integration. Until then we do not present any feature as shared.

  6. Q6Are my travel records passed to partners?

    No. Individual routes, passports and bookings are never provided or sold to partners. Even in future institutional work, only aggregated statistics that cannot identify individuals are considered.

  7. Q7Does it work on both iPhone and Android?

    Yes. You can download it from Google Play and the App Store.

  8. Q8How is it different from SHAREON?

    MY WORLD focuses on travel records, planning and travel-related links; SHAREON on sharing and renting items, equipment and spaces. Travel gear rental is planned to work with SHAREON; the two services are not linked today.

This page introduces the MY WORLD business. The website does not offer booking, ordering, payment, sign-in, seller registration or record entry — the actual features live in the MY WORLD app.

“Planned” and “Under review” items are long-term ideas from the final integrated business plan. They are not all built today; timing and scope depend on supplier contracts, applicable law and service validation.
