BUSINESS MODEL

People gather around a free map;
revenue comes from preparation and connection.

The business is designed in order: free record habit → subscription → booking and trade links → business and institutional services. The basic world map is never charged.

  1. Record platform

    The world map and records stay free; added value such as advanced maps, stats, AI and family management is offered by subscription. Records building up on the map are the reason to reopen the app before, during and after a trip.

  2. Travel platform

    Flights, stays, experiences, transport and packages are linked through eligible partners, earning affiliate revenue or brokerage fees under each contract. For external booking partnerships MY WORLD does not set fees itself; the contract’s payment terms apply.

  3. Trade platform

    Fees on trade directly related to travel — goods, second-hand, group buying, gear rental and creator content — are under review. Not a general shopping mall: the focus is on goods directly about travel.

  4. Business platform

    A long-term goal: recurring revenue from operator SaaS subscriptions and tourism-body campaign and solution contracts. Tourism bodies would receive only aggregated statistics that cannot identify individuals.

BOOKING & BROKERAGE

Principles for booking,
trade and brokerage revenue.

  • For partnerships that link to external booking sites, MY WORLD does not set its own fee; it follows the payment terms in that partner contract.

  • The full transaction value that belongs to sellers is never counted as MY WORLD revenue. Transaction value and actual platform revenue are kept apart.

  • In the base design NEWON does not hold users’ transaction funds. Payment for physical or on-site services would use payment-gateway and payout-agent structures.

  • MY WORLD and SHAREON never both charge a platform fee on the same transaction.

  • Travel products are never sold directly without travel-agency registration. Registered operators’ products are linked only through partnership or brokerage.

  • Affiliate and ad links are clearly labelled, and paid placement is never made to look like verified excellence.

MARKET OPPORTUNITY

Market problems
and the opportunity.

The opportunity MY WORLD sees is less the booking itself than the empty time around it.

  1. Travel abroad is everyday now, yet…

    Demand is large, but planning, recording and sharing around a booking are still handled by travelers with many separate tools.

  2. Booking apps fade after the trip

    Booking-centred services have little reason to be opened after travel. A growing map and records give people a reason to return.

  3. Small suppliers lack channels

    Small local tour, experience and stay operators lack ways to reach travelers and tools to run their business. Verified brokerage and operator tools are the opening.

  4. Travel is global from day one

    Maps and records work in any country, which suits multilingual growth — though trade must expand country by country under local law.

DIFFERENTIATION HYPOTHESES

Differentiation hypotheses
versus other services.

These are hypotheses to be validated with user metrics and interviews.

Comparisons with other services use only public facts, and features not yet built are never claimed as an advantage.

  • The map is the identity

    A map of visited countries becomes more meaningful the more it grows — which is exactly why export and deletion must be guaranteed.

  • Before, during and after

    Not a single booking moment but the flow from planning to travel, records and the next plan, in one app.

  • Free core plus added value

    A free core experience lowers the barrier; subscription is added value for heavy users.

  • Links to life platforms

    Over time, situations that connect with other NEWON platforms — travelling with children, pets or older relatives.

GO-TO-MARKET

Initial go-to-market
strategy.

We do not target every traveler at once; we start with the people who value records most.

  • A free record habit

    Offer world-map colouring and travel records for free so that organising past trips becomes a habit first.

  • Spreading through map shares

    Sharing your world map as an image is planned. We will check whether shares bring new users.

  • Subscription experiments

    Test subscription bundles built on planning features and advanced stats. Prices are not final.

  • Affiliate links and operator interviews

    Check booking demand on a small scale with labelled affiliate links, and confirm the need for operator tools through stay and tour interviews.

OPERATIONS & LEGAL

Operating and legal
principles.

These items are reviewed before each business starts. Results may change the scope of a service.

  • Travel products & packages

    No travel product is sold before travel-agency registration and liability under tourism law are confirmed.

  • Flights

    Ticketing, refund and booking responsibilities are set by contract and shown as the supplier’s before booking.

  • Stays

    Booking, cancellation and refund rules and consumer protection apply; unregistered accommodation is never linked.

  • Tours & experiences

    Local operators’ qualifications, safety, insurance and liability are checked.

  • Travel insurance

    Until insurance sales and brokerage rules are reviewed, we stay at information only.

  • Market & rental

    E-commerce and second-hand trading rules, and deposit and dispute handling (with SHAREON), apply.

  • Passports & documents

    Sensitive identity information is minimised and protected.

  • Location

    Public real-time location is restricted; sharing only with groups that agree.

  • Community

    Reporting, blocking and moderation policies against fraud and false information.

  • AI information

    Visa, entry and safety information is checked against official sources.

  • Global

    Each country’s privacy, payment and tourism laws, and map naming and border policies, are confirmed.

  • Business SaaS

    Offered only with operator verification and access-permission management.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions
about the business.

  • How does MY WORLD earn revenue today?

    The app currently has an existing premium subscription and ads served through an ad network. Paying-user numbers and revenue are not published; the other revenue models are reviewed in order after contracts, legal review and service validation.

  • Why finish records before booking?

    Booking-centred services give little reason to come back after a trip, while a world map and records grow more meaningful the more they build up. Travel is highly competitive and legal responsibility and supplier contracts matter, so MY WORLD completes its own strength first and expands in a validated order.

  • When will prices and fees be set?

    New subscription plans, booking and sales fees and settlement terms are not final. The business plan contains several proposals, so details will be announced separately once contracts and policies are settled.

  • Does NEWON hold users’ transaction funds?

    Not in the base design. For physical goods and on-site services, payment and payout agency structures are under review; external booking partnerships follow the payment terms of that contract.

  • Is transaction volume the same as MY WORLD revenue?

    No. The full amount that goes to sellers is never counted as MY WORLD revenue. For brokered transactions, transaction value and actual platform revenue are managed separately.
