02 · About BABYLOG

Twenty years of one child,
one family's record.

BABYLOG is NEWON's family platform that brings together a child's growth, health, education, family life and memories from pregnancy to high-school graduation. It does not stop at the baby years: the goal is to connect the records, information and services a family needs at each stage as the child grows.

Today the iOS and Android app offers parenting records such as feeding, sleep, diapers and meals, growth and health records such as height, weight and vaccinations, hospital and education schedules, family sharing with parents, grandparents and guardians, and a reference-only AI record summary.

The integrated business plan designs 27 long-term areas, from education, community and booking to shopping, used goods, group buying, books, institution services and giving for children and teens. They will be opened step by step after validation; not all of them are available yet.

Public-interest areas such as finding missing children, child protection, and foster care and adoption information are kept separate from commercial services and are prepared around links to official agencies.

03 · Our Mission

For a better everyday life
for children and families.

The six core values BABYLOG keeps as it builds and expands its services.

Family

Parenting together as a family. Parents and guardians see the same records and carry them on together.

Growth

A child's growth and change. Small records add up to a child's growth story.

Connection

Connecting families, information and services. Scattered records and information become one experience.

Care

Consideration for children and guardians. Easing the load on guardians so they can focus on the child.

Trust

Trustworthy information and experience. Official sources come first, and children's and families' data is handled with care.

Accessibility

A service anyone can use easily, including families who are just starting out.

Problem → Solution

From scattered parenting notes
to one family timeline.

The problems families actually face and how BABYLOG proposes to solve them.

ProblemSolution

  • Records are scattered across apps, messengers and paper

    Feeding, sleep, growth and vaccination records come together in one profile per child. This is a core feature of the app today.

  • Several people care for the child but cannot see the same information

    Parents, grandparents and guardians check the same records and schedules. The principle is that the family decides who sees what.

  • Tools stop after the baby years

    The plan is to extend use to school schedules, learning and careers, and to hand records over to the child's own account at adulthood.

  • It is hard to choose goods, services and institutions with confidence

    Explore, booking and market areas are being prepared around verified-use reviews, a clear line between ads and information, and official sources first.

  • Children who need protection struggle to reach official information

    A separate public-interest area is being prepared to guide missing-child reporting routes, foster care, adoption and independence support for free and link to official agencies.

Who It's For

The people who use BABYLOG,
and when they use it.

The initial core customers are families raising a child aged 0 to 2 together. Institutions and companies are met separately as business and partnership customers.

Expecting and new parents

BABYLOG's central users, who log pregnancy weeks and checkups and a newborn's feeding, sleep and diapers every day.

Grandparents and caring family

Grandparents and family who look after the child during the day in dual-income households. They see the same records and keep adding to them.

School-age children and teens

The stage that widens into school schedules, learning and careers, planned around protecting teens' independence and privacy.

Institutions, companies, public bodies

Daycare centers, kindergartens, academies, parenting brands, companies with family benefits, child welfare institutions and public bodies are business and partnership customers.

Typical moments of use

  • Logging a night feed and handing over in the morning

    A partner checks the feeding and diaper records left overnight and carries on. Possible in the app today.

  • Keeping track of vaccinations and checkups

    Hospital visits and vaccinations are added to the schedule and checked by the whole family. Possible in the app today.

  • Sharing the day with grandparents

    Grandparents caring for the child after a parent returns to work leave records, and the parent sees the whole day in the evening.

  • Preparing for school

    Managing timetables, supplies and school calendars together after starting elementary school is an area still to come.

06 · For Families

Making shared parenting
more convenient.

BABYLOG aims for an experience in which family members understand each other's day and share a child's important moments. We are building a platform that keeps records, schedules and everyday information connected instead of scattered.

Shared parenting

In the app, parents, grandparents and guardians check and continue a child's records and schedules together.

Family schedules and roles

A child's personal data

08 · Trust & Responsibility

Trust matters
for children and families.

Six principles BABYLOG keeps as a platform that handles children's and families' records.

  • Protecting personal and child-related data

    Children's and families' information is handled with care, under the Privacy Policy.

  • Guardian permissions and family sharing scope

    The principle is that the family decides how far records are shared.

  • Official sources first

    Health, support-program and safety information comes first from official bodies and trusted sources.

  • Child safety and respect for children's rights

    No features that monitor or track a child; children's rights come first.

  • Responsible guidance in specialist fields

    In medical, legal and adoption matters, BABYLOG connects to official bodies and professionals without diagnosis or brokerage.

  • Public-interest information kept separate from commerce

    Child protection and public support information is free and kept separate from commercial transactions.

We have also decided
what we will not do.

Because we handle children's and families' information, we decided what we will not do before building features. These are the standards written in the integrated business plan and our operating principles.

CHILD SAFETY & SOCIAL IMPACT

So that tomorrow is safe
for every child.

BABYLOG is preparing a future that goes beyond helping families raise children: connecting official information and institutions for children who need protection and support. Public-interest information on child protection, missing children, abuse prevention, adoption and foster care is kept separate from commercial transactions and centers on free access to information and links to official bodies.

Explore public welfare and child safety → Partnership inquiry
  1. 01
    Child safety and missing-child prevention

    A direction to guide everyday safety, accident and missing-child prevention information and the reporting channels official bodies publish.

  2. 02
    Child protection and abuse prevention

    A direction to connect abuse prevention education, official reporting systems and child protection agency information.

  3. 03
    Official foster care and adoption information

    A direction to guide official systems, procedures and responsible bodies accurately, with no private matching or transactions.

  4. 04
    Independence support and verified giving

    A direction to connect support systems for youth leaving care with verified public-interest bodies and corporate social contribution.

  5. 05
    Giving for children and teens (planned)

    A direction beyond growth records and family services: supporting the growth of children and teens who need help through users' voluntary giving and cooperation with trusted child welfare institutions. Children's identities are never disclosed.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership, and it does not replace professional judgment, official reporting or legal procedures.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

Global Vision

Family records proven in Korea,
for families in every country.

BABYLOG's long-term direction is to grow into a global family platform that connects the whole journey of a family and child, from pregnancy and birth to parenting, education and the teenage years. We first prove the service in Korea, then review each country step by step against its own parenting systems, culture and child-related laws.

Localised to each country's parenting context

Beyond language, the direction is to reflect what differs by country, such as pregnancy and birth support, vaccination schedules and school systems, based on official local information.

Child protection before expansion

Children's personal data protection, guardian consent and compliance with each country's child-related laws are the first criteria in any overseas review.

A family ecosystem built with local institutions

We review possible cooperation with local parenting and education services and child welfare institutions, growing as a family platform within the NEWON ecosystem.

Global expansion is a future plan under review. No countries or dates have been set, and there are no contracted overseas partners.

NEWON Connection

Built the NEWON way,
with standard products.

BABYLOG is a core NEWON platform and runs under the same principles as NEWON's other platforms.

  1. In-house products first

    Standardized in-house SaaS, platforms, licenses and automation products — not per-client outsourcing.

  2. Roles divided with SHAREON

    Baby goods shopping, used goods and group buying stay in BABYLOG, while rental links to SHAREON, the sharing and rental platform. The principle is that a single transaction is never charged fees twice.

  3. Roles divided with LIVON and ONGIL

    Guidance on life-stage systems such as childbirth support and parental leave is handled by LIVON, and links to life stages after childhood are a long-term direction.

  4. Roles of the web and the app

    The website introduces the brand, services, business and partnerships and routes inquiries; the app handles actual recording and management.
