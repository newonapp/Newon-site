BabyLog

Every moment of growing up together,
in one place.

From pregnancy planning to birth, parenting, growth records and family life. BABYLOG is a family life platform that connects the precious everyday life of children and families and builds a more convenient, warmer parenting experience.

02 · About BABYLOG

As a child grows,
a family's everyday life carries on.

  1. 01

    From the moment you wait for your child to the day you first meet, recording small changes and growing together. Parenting is full of moments and choices.

  2. 02

    BABYLOG aims for an experience that makes the records and information a child's growth and a family's daily life need easier to manage and connect.

  3. 03

    Today, the iOS and Android app offers parenting records, growth and health records, schedules and family sharing.

  4. 04

    Beyond simple records, BABYLOG aims to grow into a platform that extends to family life, education, care and a wide range of everyday services.

03 · Our Mission

For a better everyday life
for children and families.

The six core values BABYLOG keeps as it builds and expands its services.

01

Family

Parenting together as a family. Parents and guardians see the same records and carry them on together.

02

Growth

A child's growth and change. Small records add up to a child's growth story.

03

Connection

Connecting families, information and services. Scattered records and information become one experience.

04

Care

Consideration for children and guardians. Easing the load on guardians so they can focus on the child.

05

Trust

Trustworthy information and experience. Official sources come first, and children's and families' data is handled with care.

06

Accessibility

A service anyone can use easily, including families who are just starting out.

06 · For Families

Making shared parenting
more convenient.

BABYLOG aims for an experience in which family members understand each other's day and share a child's important moments. We are building a platform that keeps records, schedules and everyday information connected instead of scattered.

Shared parenting

08 · Trust & Responsibility

Trust matters
for children and families.

Six principles BABYLOG keeps as a platform that handles children's and families' records.

  1. 01
    Protecting personal and child-related data

    Children's and families' information is handled with care, under the Privacy Policy.

  2. 02
    Guardian permissions and family sharing scope

    The principle is that the family decides how far records are shared.

  3. 03
    Official sources first

    Health, support-program and safety information comes first from official bodies and trusted sources.

  4. 04
    Child safety and respect for children's rights

    No features that monitor or track a child; children's rights come first.

  5. 05
    Responsible guidance in specialist fields

    In medical, legal and adoption matters, BABYLOG connects to official bodies and professionals without diagnosis or brokerage.

  6. 06
    Public-interest information kept separate from commerce

    Child protection and public support information is free and kept separate from commercial transactions.

CHILD SAFETY & SOCIAL IMPACT

So that tomorrow is safe
for every child.

BABYLOG is preparing a future that goes beyond helping families raise children: connecting official information and institutions for children who need protection and support. Public-interest information on child protection, missing children, abuse prevention, adoption and foster care is kept separate from commercial transactions and centers on free access to information and links to official bodies.

Explore public welfare and child safety → Partnership inquiry
  1. 01
    Child safety and missing-child prevention

    A direction to guide everyday safety, accident and missing-child prevention information and the reporting channels official bodies publish.

  2. 02
    Child protection and abuse prevention

    A direction to connect abuse prevention education, official reporting systems and child protection agency information.

  3. 03
    Official foster care and adoption information

    A direction to guide official systems, procedures and responsible bodies accurately, with no private matching or transactions.

  4. 04
    Independence support and verified giving

    A direction to connect support systems for youth leaving care with verified public-interest bodies and corporate social contribution.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership, and it does not replace professional judgment, official reporting or legal procedures.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

NEWON Connection

Built the NEWON way,
with standard products.

BABYLOG is a core NEWON platform and runs under the same principles as NEWON's other platforms.

  1. 01
    In-house products first

    Standardized in-house SaaS, platforms, licenses and automation products — not per-client outsourcing.

  2. 02
    Roles divided with SHAREON

    Sharing and renting baby goods and person-to-person trading link to SHAREON with roles kept separate, and the principle is that a single transaction is never charged fees twice.

  3. 03
    Roles divided with LIVON and ONGIL

    Guidance on life-stage systems such as childbirth support and parental leave is handled by LIVON, and links to life stages after childhood are a long-term direction.

  4. 04
    Roles of the web and the app

    The website introduces the brand, services, business and partnerships and routes inquiries; the app handles actual recording and management.
