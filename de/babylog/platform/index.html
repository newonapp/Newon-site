BabyLog

From pregnancy planning to a child's growth,
connecting every family moment.

BABYLOG aims to be a platform that connects the records, information, communication and wide range of services that parenting and family life need into one experience.

21 Services

Twenty-one services,
five areas.

From pregnancy and birth to baby care and growth, family and community, parenting lifestyle, and public information and child safety. Features in the app are described in the present tense; areas we are expanding into are described as direction.

Building on the parenting and family services it offers today, BABYLOG is expanding into everyday life, education, care, business and public-sector areas. Some of the businesses and features introduced here are part of future development or partnership plans.

A

A · Pregnancy & Birth

01

Pregnancy Planning

Pregnancy Planning

Expanding into an area that helps families look through the schedules and information needed to prepare for a new family. Key areas: planning checklist, fertility window information, healthy habits, checkup preparation, specialist institutions.

02

Pregnancy Management

Pregnancy Management

Manage weekly changes, checkup schedules and birth preparation together in the app. It does not diagnose the fetus. Key areas: pregnancy week information, prenatal tests, pregnancy records, birth essentials, family schedule.

03

Birth & Recovery

Birth & Recovery

BABYLOG aims to connect information for parents' recovery and a new daily life after birth. Key areas: birth records, recovery records, postpartum care information, postpartum support programs, specialist institutions.

04

Birth Planning

Birth Planning

Widening into an experience in which families check and plan what is needed around birth. Key areas: hospital bag, birth schedule, baby goods, family role sharing, early parenting checklist.

B

B · Baby Care & Child Growth

05

Feeding, Sleep & Diapers

Feeding, Sleep & Diapers

Log and check repeated parenting moments with ease in the app. Key areas: feeding, sleep and diaper records, daily summary, daily patterns.

06

Growth Records

Growth Records

Record height, weight and month-by-month growth in the app and look back on it with family. Organizing photos and growth memories is an expansion area.

07

Health & Vaccination

Health & Vaccination

Keep checkups and vaccinations from being missed in the app. It does not replace medical diagnosis or prescription. Key areas: vaccination schedule, checkup and health records, hospital visits, official health information.

08

Development Information

Development Information

BABYLOG aims to connect age-based information and observation records that help families understand development. Key areas: development information, play by month, growth checklist, specialist institutions.

09

Baby Food & Nutrition

Baby Food & Nutrition

Record changes in a child's eating in the app. Meal planning is an expansion area, and there are no individual nutrition prescriptions. Key areas: baby food records, meal records, meal plans, ingredients, allergy information.

10

Play, Learning & Education

Play, Learning & Education

BABYLOG aims to introduce play and learning that fit a child's age and interests. Key areas: play by age, reading, sensory activities, education content, activities by developmental stage.

C

C · Family & Community

11

Shared Parenting

Shared Parenting

In the app, parents and guardians manage a child's daily life and key information together. Key areas: family members, shared records, shared schedules, family communication.

12

Family Schedule

Family Schedule

Organize a child's schedule and the family's everyday preparation in the app. Key areas: hospital, education and family schedules, things to bring, to-dos, notifications.

13

Parent Community

Parent Community

Building a space for parents at similar stages to share information, with policies protecting children's photos and personal data in place first. Key areas: communities by age, local information, experiences, Q&A.

14

Parent Wellness & Education

Parent Wellness & Education

BABYLOG aims to connect information and education that support parents' wellbeing and parenting skills. Key areas: parent education, postpartum emotional health, parenting stress, counseling bodies.

D · Parenting Lifestyle

15

Shopping

Shopping

Expanding into commerce for exploring the goods and brands children and families need. Key areas: baby goods, children's furniture, clothing, food, household goods.

16

Sharing & Rental

Sharing & Rental

Helping families use short-lived baby goods sensibly. Sharing and rental link to SHAREON with roles kept separate, and safety items follow separate standards.

17

Parenting Services

Parenting Services

BABYLOG aims to connect information on specialist services and qualified providers for birth and parenting. Key areas: postpartum care, postpartum helpers, parenting support, childcare, specialist institutions.

18

Childcare & Education

Childcare & Education

Widening into an area that helps families understand official education and care institution information and schedules; no applications or booking. Key areas: daycare, kindergarten, childcare information, admission guidance, programs.

Parenting Support & Welfare

BABYLOG aims to guide public parenting support programs and official application channels. Key areas: birth support, childrearing support, childcare fees, family welfare, local support programs.

Child Safety & Protection

Child Safety & Protection

A public-interest area connecting official information and education content for children's safety and protection. Key areas: everyday safety, accident prevention, missing-child prevention, abuse prevention, protection bodies.

02 · Growth Journey

From pregnancy and birth
to every stage of growth.

It starts from one child profile and continues into the records and information each stage needs.

  1. 01

    Pregnancy & birth

    Start recording from pregnancy in the app and manage weekly changes and checkup schedules together.

  2. 02

    Child profile

    Manage growth stage, health information and basic schedule for each child in the app.

  3. 03

    Expanding by growth stage

    Widening the records and management a child needs into play, education and family-life stages as the child grows.

03 · Parenting & Records

Feeding, sleep, meals, growth —
records every day.

Repeated parenting moments are logged with ease, and small records add up to a child's growth story.

  • Feeding, sleep, diaper, meal and play records

    Easily log everyday moments in the app and check them in a daily summary.

  • Growth & health records

    Keep height, weight, vaccinations and health history in one timeline.

  • Growth statistics

    See growth trends and daily patterns in charts.

04 · Family Connection

Family sees and manages
together.

Parenting is something a family does together. Guardians and family check and continue the same records.

Shared family management

In the app, parents and guardians look at the same child's records and schedules together.

Permissions per guardian

Step by step, we are designing permissions for each guardian and ways to widen what a growing child manages on their own.

05 · Learning & Education

Play, learning,
and links to education.

Starting from schedules and widening into play and learning information and links to education institutions.

Hospital and education schedules

Gather hospital visits, vaccinations, daycare, kindergarten and education schedules and family plans in the app.

Play, reading and learning information

BABYLOG aims to provide activity information matched to each developmental stage, such as play by age, reading and sensory activities.

Education content and institutions

Expanding into connecting education content with programs of institutions such as daycare centers and kindergartens.

06 · Memories & Milestones

Keeping passing time
as memories.

BABYLOG aims for an experience that lets families look back together on the fast-changing early years.

  • Photos, diary and growth memories

    Building an experience that gathers photos, diary entries and month-by-month growth records into one archive.

  • Growth albums and reports

    Expanding into an experience that turns growth records into albums and reports.

07 · Smart Assistance

Reference-only help
to organize records.

AI is a reference tool that organizes and presents records.

AI record summary and general information

In the app, record patterns are organized for reference and general information suited to age and situation is provided. It does not replace medical diagnosis or professional advice.

08 · Explore & Discover

Toward the products and services
a family needs.

Expanding into an experience that makes the products and services families need easier to find.

Explore products and services

BABYLOG aims for an experience for exploring baby goods, education products and family service information such as postpartum care and childcare.

Sharing and rental with SHAREON

Sharing and renting baby goods and person-to-person trading link to SHAREON with roles kept separate, designed so that one transaction is never charged fees twice.

09 · Our Commitment

A child's safety and a family's trust
come first.

The standards BABYLOG puts first as a service that handles children's and families' records.

  • 01

    Protecting children's and families' personal data

    We handle children's and families' personal data with care; how it is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

  • 02

    Trustworthy parenting and health information

    AI-organized content is for reference and does not replace medical, legal or educational professionals.

  • 03

    Data management that respects guardian permissions

    The principle is that the family decides how far records are shared.

  • 04

    An accessible service

    Used through the iOS and Android apps, with accessibility continuously improved so anyone can use it easily.

  • 05

    Responsible child safety and protection information

    We guide official information and reporting channels, and do not accept reports or identify a child's location or identity.

  • 06

    Public-interest access to information

    BABYLOG aims to let children and families in need reach official systems and institution information free of charge.

PUBLIC WELFARE & CHILD SAFETY

We put a child's safety
and rights first.

Eight public-interest areas, from public parenting support to child safety, protection, independence and verified giving, connecting children and families in need with official information and institutions.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership, and it does not replace professional judgment, official reporting or legal procedures.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

25 · Government Parenting Support

The support programs you need,
accurately, through official channels.

A public-information area connecting central and local government programs for birth, parenting, childcare and family support, and their official application channels.

Key areas

  • Birth support
  • Childrearing support
  • Childcare fees
  • Family welfare
  • Local support programs
  • Official application channels
Official information sources
The health and welfare ministry, local governments, official government welfare and civil-service portals
What we do not do · conditions
BABYLOG does not decide eligibility or apply on anyone's behalf; it points to official application channels. Guidance on life-stage systems is divided with LIVON.

26 · Child Safety & Prevention

A child's safety
begins with small preparations.

An information and content area supporting children's everyday safety, accident prevention, missing-child prevention and guardian education.

Key areas

  • Everyday safety
  • Accident prevention
  • Outing safety
  • Missing-child prevention education
  • Guardian safety checklist
  • Official reporting and support bodies
Official information sources
National police, the health and welfare ministry, local governments, public and expert child safety education bodies
What we do not do · conditions
Only material provided by official bodies and experts is used. This is not a feature to monitor or track a child.

27 · Missing Children Information

So that more people keep
caring about finding lost children.

A public-interest area connecting official reporting bodies for missing children, prevention information and the official guidance channels of public bodies.

Key areas

  • Guide to official missing-child search services
  • Prevention rules
  • Guardian response guide
  • Official reporting channels
  • Related institution information
Official information sources
Official bodies for missing children, such as the National Police Agency and the child rights agency
What we do not do · conditions
BABYLOG will not build its own missing-child reporting, tip, location tracking or identity determination, and will not collect or publish personal data of missing children.

28 · Child Protection & Abuse Prevention

So that danger is not overlooked
and leads to protection.

A public-interest information area connecting child abuse prevention, guardian education, official reporting systems and child protection agency information.

Key areas

  • Child abuse prevention education
  • Official information on warning signs
  • How to report
  • Child protection agencies
  • Guardian education
  • Emergency support guidance
Official information sources
Health and welfare ministry, police, local governments, child protection agencies
What we do not do · conditions
BABYLOG does not judge whether abuse occurred or accept cases. Emergencies are directed to official systems such as 112.

29 · Child Welfare Facilities & Protection

Lasting attention and connection
for children who need protection.

An area guiding child care facilities, the child protection system, related public support programs and official institution information.

Key areas

  • Child care facility information
  • Group home information
  • Support for protected children
  • Child protection agency guide
  • Official counseling and support channels
Official information sources
Health and welfare ministry, the child rights agency, local governments, qualified child welfare facilities
What we do not do · conditions
Names, photos, locations or stories of children in facilities and of protected children are never published; individual cases are not judged, and official counseling and support channels are pointed to.

30 · Foster Care & Adoption Information

Accurate information and careful preparation
on the way to becoming a family.

A public-interest information area providing official systems, procedures and responsible bodies for family foster care and adoption. No private matching or transactions involving children.

Key areas

  • Family foster care information
  • Public adoption procedures
  • Responsible bodies
  • Information for prospective adoptive parents
  • Post-adoption family support
  • Official counseling channels
Official information sources
The health and welfare ministry, the child rights agency, foster care support centers and other public bodies
What we do not do · conditions
BABYLOG does not connect children with prospective parents or broker adoption, and does not collect or publish information on children in foster care or eligible for adoption.

31 · Youth Independence Support

So that no one has to prepare
for life after care alone.

An area connecting young people leaving care with housing, education, employment, financial and daily-life support programs and official support bodies.

Key areas

  • Support systems for youth leaving care
  • Housing information
  • Education and certifications
  • Employment and career
  • Financial and daily-life support
  • Official counseling and support bodies
Official information sources
Health and welfare ministry, the child rights agency, local government independence support bodies
What we do not do · conditions
Personal data and stories of young people are not collected or published, and eligibility is not judged; official counseling and support bodies are pointed to.

32 · Verified Giving & Social Impact

So that small acts of care
can help a child's daily life.

An area connecting verified public-interest bodies and corporate social contribution, aiming to create social value for children and families.

Key areas

  • Verified public-interest bodies
  • Regular and one-time sponsorship information
  • Goods donation
  • Corporate social contribution
  • Official volunteer programs
  • Sponsorship transparency guidance
Official information sources
Officially registered sponsoring nonprofits, local governments, qualified child welfare facilities
What we do not do · conditions
Donations are not shown as BABYLOG's general revenue, and BABYLOG will not raise funds directly until legal requirements and an operating system are in place.

“Official information sources” are types of official bodies to refer to. They are not partners, and official links are added only after verification.
