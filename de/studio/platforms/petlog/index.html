01 · PURPOSE

Purpose of
PETLOG products.

  1. 01

    Let owners record a pet's daily routine, such as walks, meals and toileting, the same way every day.

  2. 02

    Turn regular care, such as baths, grooming, parasite prevention and vet visits, into a schedule that isn't missed.

  3. 03

    Help pet shops and care businesses manage bookings, care records and stock on the same forms.

05 · USE CASES

How they
will be used.

01

When the family takes turns walking the dog

With walks and toileting on the daily sheet, nobody doubles up or misses one.

02

Leaving the pet with a sitter during a trip

The hand-over card covers food and cautions, so you don't repeat the same explanation each time.

03

Looking back on the year's pet spending

The spending sheet's yearly total shows the share of food and vet bills and helps set next year's budget.

04

A weekend rush of grooming bookings

The booking board assigns staff by time slot and shows each pet's notes in advance.

FAQ

PETLOG products
FAQ.

01How does it work with several pets?

Record sheets and schedules are planned as one per pet. The spending sheet is designed to show each pet separately.

02Can I keep vet visit records?

There is space to note visit dates and what you were told, but the products don't interpret or judge it.

03Does it connect with the PETLOG app?

No link is offered today. The products are planned as files used without the app.

04Can several staff at a pet shop use it?

Several staff in one workplace fall under team or business licences.

05When will they launch?

All are candidates with no launch date yet. Confirmed launches are announced on the Newon Store first.
