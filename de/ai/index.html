Savy AI

AI that understands your spending

Savy AI analyzes income, spending, habits, and financial goals to support personalized budget management and saving plans.

Example

“Why did I spend so much on food this month?”

AI reviews food-spend changes and key purchases, then suggests how to manage next month’s budget.

Key AI features · Feature status

  1. 01 Spending pattern analysis Verfügbar

    Analyzes spending by category—food, shopping, transport, living costs—and highlights month-to-month changes.

  2. 02 Personalized budget tips Verfügbar

    Suggests category budgets from income, fixed costs, saving goals, and past patterns.

  3. 03 Expense forecasting Verfügbar

    Projects month-end spend from current pace and history, and flags budget overrun risk.

  4. 04 Saving suggestions Geplant

    Finds recurring and concentrated spend and suggests practical ways to save.

  5. 05 AI money coach Geplant

    Answers budget and saving questions based on your records and goals.

Sign-in and per-service data linking are separate. We do not claim AI can auto-query or analyze unlinked services.

Example

We envision weekly summaries from goals, activity, and schedule logs in services you choose to connect.

Key AI features · Feature status

  1. 01 Unified AI assistant Geplant

  3. 03 Unified schedule view Geplant

404: HUMAN AI

An AI surveillance world that remembers your choices

404: HUMAN is a choice-driven interactive game where you survive as the last human in an AI-only world. Interrogation, AI memory, and Detection/Humanity pressure lead to multiple endings.

These are narrative systems inside the game—not a generic ML difficulty/hint bot.

Example

“What if I risk survival to save a teammate?”

The AI analyzes your answer and shifts Detection, Humanity, and later interrogations.

Key AI features · Feature status

  1. 01 AI interrogation Verfügbar

    You must pass AI questions and checks with limited choices. Answers shape what comes next.

  2. 02 AI Memory Verfügbar

    AI remembers prior choices, hesitation, contradictions, and patterns—and brings them back later.

  3. 03 Detection · Humanity Verfügbar

    Detection (risk of being exposed) and Humanity push you in different directions.

  4. 04 Surveillance intensity Verfügbar

    Choose surveillance intensity before play to set pressure level.

  5. 05 Multiple endings Verfügbar

    Accumulated choices and metrics change the final escape and ending.

  6. 06 Live choice analysis feedback Verfügbar

    After each choice, system analysis feedback shows how your judgment landed.
