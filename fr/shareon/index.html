SHAREON Overview — Pas besoin de posséder.
Juste quand il le faut.

A way to use what you need sensibly, and to add new value to assets you are not using. SHAREON aims to be an open marketplace that connects people and businesses to share and rent physical assets.

WHAT IS SHAREON

Trading the right to use,
not ownership.

SHAREON is a sharing and rental platform where neighbourhood individuals and businesses lend and borrow things, spaces and equipment for just the period needed. Its first focus is small-item rental between nearby neighbours; buying and selling second-hand goods or running a general shopping mall is not SHAREON’s core business. What changes hands is the right to use something for a time, not ownership.

  • 01

    You have to buy things you use once or twice a year

    Even things needed briefly, such as camping gear, a power drill or baby gear, usually have to be bought, and then they take up storage space. SHAREON aims to ease this with a rental flow for borrowing by the day or week and giving things back.

  • 02

    Lending between neighbours lacks clear promises and responsibility

    With noticeboard posts or lending between friends, the schedule, security and return check are left to the people involved. SHAREON brings booking, pick-up and return records, reviews and disputes into one flow, and prepares to handle money through a licensed payment provider.

  • 03

    Equipment and spaces at companies often sit idle

    Filming kits, meeting rooms and office laptops spend long periods unused. Handling rentals between people, business rentals and internal asset sharing on one platform is the long-term direction.

The five stretches of one rental

  1. 01 List

    List an asset

    People and businesses add items or spaces with their own rental terms.

  2. 02 Explore

    Explore

    Find what you need by area, period and deposit terms.

  3. 03 Book

    Book

    Pick dates and send a request; the lender approves it.

  4. 04 Use

    Pick up & use

    Meet, check the condition and use it for the agreed period.

  5. 05 Return

    Return & settle

    Hand it back, compare the condition and finish with a review.

SHAREON aims to join listing, booking, pick-up, return, reviews and disputes into one flow. The payment, deposit and settlement stretches will not operate until a payment-provider contract and legal review are complete.

CORE VALUES

Three values
SHAREON holds to.

Before adding categories, we build trades in which even strangers in the same neighbourhood can lend and return with confidence.

  1. Only as much as you need

    Instead of buying, you borrow the right to use something for a period. Lenders set the fee, rental unit and availability themselves, and basic listing and renting are meant to work without a subscription.

  2. Close to home

    The starting point is small items handed over within a neighbourhood. Exact addresses are not shown before a trade, and contact and location details are used only as far as a trade needs.

  3. A trade ends when it comes back

    A rental is finished only when the item is back. Condition checks at pick-up and return, reviews, reporting and blocking, and dispute handling are designed before extra screens.

WHO IT IS FOR

Neighbours who borrow, neighbours who lend,
and businesses with assets.

Anyone can be both a borrower and a lender. Trades involving a business open only after business verification.

  • Borrowers

    People who borrow now and then

    Someone going camping for the first time, moving or fixing up a home, or needing something for a child for a short while. They borrow for the period needed instead of buying.

    • First camping trip
    • Home repairs
    • Baby gear

    What they do on SHAREONSearch the Explore tab by area, period and deposit terms, ask about condition in chat, then send a booking request. A reminder arrives before the return.

  • Lenders

    People who lend

    Neighbours with things they rarely use. Their items get used again and earn a rental fee, on terms they set themselves.

    • Idle items
    • Own terms
    • Reviews

    What they do on SHAREONAdd photos, parts, rental fee and availability in the List tab, then approve or decline incoming requests. At return, compare the condition with the pick-up record.

  • Rental businesses

    Rental shops & space operators

    Small businesses whose trade is renting: camera, camping or tool rental shops, meeting-room or rehearsal-room operators. They join directly, without a separate partnership contract.

    • Direct listing
    • Stock management
    • Business check

    What they do on SHAREONAfter business verification, list part of the stock and switch to a business role in the same app to manage bookings across several assets.

  • Organisations

    Companies & institutions with assets

    Companies, schools and institutions whose equipment and rooms are often idle. External rentals and internal sharing are kept apart.

    • Internal assets
    • Idle equipment
    • Meeting rooms

    What they do on SHAREONAn internal-assets menu, shown only to authorised users, would handle bookings and returns between staff and departments. This is a future plan.

EVERYDAY SCENES

Moments when
SHAREON comes to mind.

These are the usage scenes the service is designed around. They are not real cases or reviews.

  • First camping trip

    A first camping trip

    Before buying a tent, chairs and a lantern, borrow them nearby for one night. If you enjoy it, there is still time to buy.

  • One shelf to hang

    The day you hang one shelf

    A power drill and a level are needed for half a day. Borrow them from a neighbour and return them that evening.

  • A few months of baby gear

    The months a baby grows

    Baby gear is outgrown quickly, so borrow it for the months it is used, with safety and hygiene checks attached.

  • Weekend shoot

    A weekend shoot

    A small production team borrows an individual’s camera and lights for the weekend: a C2B rental with a person as supplier.

  • Idle office projector

    Equipment the office isn’t using

    Departments share the office projector and laptops, and long-idle equipment could be offered for external rental. Under review.

What you will

Typical rental categories among the 18 areas. SHAREON does not buy items to rent them out; people and businesses list their own, and SHAREON brokers the trade.

  • Electronics & filming gear

  • Camping & outdoor

TRADE STRUCTURES

From lender to borrower:
four trade structures.

C2C, B2C, C2B and B2B describe who lends to whom. They are not all operating; the starting point is small-item rental between people.

Lender ↓Borrower →

Person

Business

Person

Business

  • C2C

    Person → person

    A neighbour borrows something another neighbour owns. This is the trade SHAREON focuses on first, centred on small items such as camping gear, tools and baby gear.

  • C2B

    Person → business

    A business borrows an individual’s equipment, such as a camera kit, for work or an event. This is a rental with an individual as supplier, not a wanted or request board.

  • B2C

    Business → person

    Small businesses and companies with equipment or spaces, such as camera rental shops or space operators, lend to individuals. Opens after business verification.

  • B2B

    Business → business

    A business lends assets such as event or filming equipment to another business. Bulk and long-term rentals are reviewed with separate contract terms.

  • Internal

    Internal asset sharing

    Employees and departments borrowing laptops, equipment and meeting rooms inside a company. Kept apart from external paid trades and planned as a business SaaS subscription rather than a per-trade fee.

  • Free Sharing

    Free lending

    Lending to neighbours without a rental fee. No platform fee is planned for free lending, and the same trust tools, such as return checks and reviews, apply.

OUR ROLE

What SHAREON does,
and what it does not.

A clearly defined role is, in our view, the first condition for rentals between strangers.

What it does

  • Broker items, equipment and spaces that people and businesses list themselves to those who need them

  • Run one trade system for booking requests and approval, pick-up and return checks, reviews, reports and disputes

  • Prepare to handle money through a licensed payment provider (switched off today)

  • Open trades involving a business only after business verification

What it does not do

  • Run a rental company that buys every item itself

  • Expand into second-hand sales or general shopping, where ownership changes hands

  • Hold funds itself, count deposits as revenue or deduct them without grounds

  • Decide damage liability automatically from pick-up and return photos

LONG-TERM VISION

From neighbourhood items
to company assets.

SHAREON’s medium- to long-term direction is to take a rental flow proven in one neighbourhood into spaces, business assets and partnerships. All of it is at the planning stage.

  1. 01

    Small-item rental nearby

    First confirm that lending and returning actually complete end to end in one neighbourhood and a few categories.

  2. 02

    Business and space rental

    Then add rental shops and space operators, and areas such as meeting or filming rooms that need rules checked first.

  3. 03

    Internal asset SaaS & partners

    Tools for managing assets inside companies, and partnerships for delivery, collection and insurance, come after rentals between people are stable.

GLOBAL VISION

Rental proven in one neighbourhood,
taken to neighbourhoods abroad.

In the long run SHAREON aims to grow into a global sharing and rental platform that connects the things, spaces and resources people and businesses own with those who need them. No service is operating abroad today, and no countries or dates have been decided.

See the global expansion stages →

  1. Every country starts with a neighbourhood

    Abroad, too, the first thing to prove is neighbours lending and returning small items within a city or local community. SHAREON does not turn into a resale or shopping service.

  2. Different lives, different rules

    Categories fitted to local housing and rental demand, and trades designed around local payment methods, currencies and consumer-protection rules, country by country.

  3. Home market first

    Market research abroad and the choice of a first market begin only after booking, return, dispute handling and payments have been proven at home.

NEWON ECOSYSTEM

SHAREON’s place
within NEWON.

In the long run SHAREON is being considered as a sharing and rental engine that could take rental demand from other NEWON services. It does not turn their shopping or selling into rental, and there is no integration or data sharing between services today.
