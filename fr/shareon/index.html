OUR VISION

For the moments when
using matters more than owning.

Many things and spaces keep their value even while they sit unused. SHAREON is building a place to borrow what you need when you need it, and to share what you are not using.

  1. 01

    Borrow things you rarely use but would hesitate to buy, for just as long as you need them.

  2. 02

    People and businesses can both be providers and renters.

  3. 03

    Designing a flow in which every rental, from listing to return, ends safely.

WHO WE CONNECT

People and businesses —
anyone can lend and borrow.

SHAREON’s key feature is that people and businesses can both be providers and renters. The six axes below describe the business structure and how it earns; they do not mean everything is built.

  • 01

    C2C

    Person to person

    People lend their things or equipment to other people.

  • 02

    B2C

    Business to person

    Businesses rent equipment, spaces and items to people.

  • 03

    C2B

    Person to business

    Businesses rent equipment or items owned by people.

  • 04

    B2B

    Business to business

    Companies rent equipment, spaces and resources to each other.

  • 05

    Marketplace

    One marketplace

    Listing, search, booking, payment, deposits, settlement, reviews.

  • 06

    SaaS

    Operating software

    Inventory, booking, rental, returns, customers and operations.

TRUST & SAFETY

When things change hands,
trust comes first.

A rental only ends when the item comes back. SHAREON designs responsibilities and procedures before screens.

  1. 01

    Condition checks

    Condition and parts are recorded at pickup and return.

  2. 02

    Deposit principle

    Deposits are kept apart from revenue and operated only after payment-provider agreements and legal review.

  3. 03

    User and business checks

    Identity, business registration and item-specific qualifications.

  4. 04

    Dispute handling

    Rules and procedures for damage, loss and late returns.

  5. 05

    Privacy

    Exact addresses are not shown before a rental; location data is removed from photos.

Deposit holding and compensation are not offered today, and no protection or compensation is promised.
