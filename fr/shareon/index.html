SHAREON

Pas besoin de posséder.
Juste quand il le faut.

SHAREON is a sharing and rental marketplace where people and businesses list their things, spaces, mobility and services directly, and others borrow just what they need. NEWON is building it as an iOS and Android app.

PilotThe SHAREON app is in pilot development. Confirmed rentals, payment, deposits and settlement are not operating yet; this website only introduces and guides.

WHY SHAREON

Every home has something used once and put away —
and somewhere, someone needs it right now.

SHAREON started to connect the two. It is a place to borrow and lend, for just as long as needed, the things that are too costly to buy for occasional use, the spaces that sit empty most of the time, and the services you only look for now and then.

This is not about owning less. It is about making better use of what already exists, and making it easier to get what you need at the moment you need it.

THE GAP

Why is borrowing
still such a hassle?

The four problems we wrote down when SHAREON began. We wanted to solve these before thinking about individual features.

  1. 01

    Too costly to buy, too scattered to rent

    Even things used a few times a year — camping or filming gear — are usually bought. Places to rent them are spread across different shops and areas, so just finding one takes time.

  2. 02

    Unused resources sit idle

    Equipment at home, empty meeting rooms and workshops, idle machines in company storage all cost money to keep, even while unused.

  3. 03

    Lending to strangers feels risky

    Without clear rules on condition, on-time return and what happens if something breaks, neither lender nor borrower wants to go first.

  4. 04

    Small businesses lack a way to reach customers

    Small rental shops, space operators and independent service providers need an open channel to meet customers without signing a separate partnership.

MISSION & VISION

MISSION

What you need, as long as you need it —
borrowed with confidence.

We are building a rental experience where finding nearby, checking conditions, recording condition and returning on time all feel natural.

VISION

An ecosystem where everyone
can lend and borrow.

We picture an open sharing and rental ecosystem in which people and businesses lend to and borrow from each other, and idle resources are used again.

“A rental only ends when the item comes back. SHAREON designs the responsibilities and steps up to the return before it designs screens.”

USER JOURNEY

Two journeys —
the borrower’s and the lender’s — meet here.

The rental journey we are designing in the SHAREON app. The first launch starts with approval-based booking, where the lender reviews and approves each request. Rentals that need a deposit open only once a deposit method supported by a payment provider (PG) is in place.

BorrowerLender

  1. 01 Need

    Borrower

    A need comes up

    A weekend camping trip, a day of filming, a meeting room for a few hours.

    Lender

    Lists an item

    Adds photos, a description, rental terms and handover options in the List tab.

  2. 02 Find

    Borrower

    Looks around

    Browses Home and Explore by area, category and dates.

    Lender

    Gets found

    The listing shows up for people searching nearby.

  3. 03 Request

    Borrower

    Sends a request

    Chooses dates and terms and asks to rent.

    Lender

    Reviews and approves

    Checks the dates and terms, then approves or declines.

  4. 04 Talk

    Borrower

    Arranges in chat

    Agrees handover time, method and any questions in chat.

    Lender

    Prepares the handover

    Checks parts and condition, then hands over as agreed.

  5. 05 Use

    Borrower

    Uses it

    Checks condition together at pickup, then uses it for the period.

    Lender

    Keeps track

    Sees the rental period and return date in the My tab.

  6. 06 Return

    Borrower

    Returns it

    Returns the item, checks condition and rates the rental.

    Lender

    Checks and closes

    Checks the returned condition and follows the damage-report steps if needed.

WHO IT IS FOR

The people who
will use SHAREON.

The same person can borrow today and lend next week.

  • 01

    Occasional users

    People who need something now and then

    Individuals who would rather borrow for the day than buy something used once or twice a year.

  • 02

    Neighbours who share

    People with things to share

    Individuals who want to lend idle gear and things to people nearby.

  • 03

    Small businesses

    Businesses looking for customers

    Rental shops, space operators and service providers who want to list right away and meet customers, without a separate partnership.

  • 04

    Companies

    Companies using resources wisely

    Companies that want to share idle equipment and spaces, or borrow equipment for a short period.

PRINCIPLES

Eight principles
SHAREON stands by.

Promises that stay the same as features grow.

  1. 01

    Anyone can take part directly

    People and businesses sign up and list in the app themselves. No separate partnership contract is needed to list.

  2. 02

    Basic trading without a subscription

    Basic listing and trading must work without subscribing.

  3. 03

    Approval-based booking first

    At first, the lender reviews and approves each request. Instant booking comes once trust tools are in place.

  4. 04

    Money through proven payment methods

    Rentals that need a deposit open only once a PG-supported deposit method is ready. SHAREON does not build its own escrow to hold money.

  5. 05

    Ads are always labelled as ads

    Paid placements and ads are always marked as advertising.

  6. 06

    No names without a contract

    We never show companies we do not actually work with as partners, by name or logo.

  7. 07

    Condition and responsibility on record

    Condition is recorded at every handover, and damage-report and review steps are set in advance.

  8. 08

    As little personal data as possible

    Exact addresses stay hidden until a rental is agreed, and location data is removed from photos.
