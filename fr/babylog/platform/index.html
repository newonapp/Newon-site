CHILD SAFETY & SOCIAL IMPACT

We put a child's safety
and rights first.

Eight directions for connecting official information and institutions for children who need protection and support. Everything below is a plan and not a released service until real institution cooperation and operation are confirmed.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

25 · Missing Children

So that more people keep
caring about finding lost children.

Aims to guide the missing-child search information and reporting channels published by official bodies, and to connect information needed for prevention.

What we guide

  • Guide to official missing-child search services
  • Prevention rules
  • Guardian response guide
  • Official reporting channels
  • Related institution information
Official information sources
Official bodies for missing children, such as the National Police Agency and the child rights agency
What we do not do · conditions
BABYLOG will not build its own missing-child reporting, tip, location tracking or identity determination, and will not collect or publish personal data of missing children.
Current status
Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed

26 · Child Safety

A child's safety
begins with small preparations.

Aims to organize safety information and educational material for outings and daily life so guardians can prepare ahead.

What we guide

  • Outing safety
  • Missing-child prevention education
  • Guardian safety checklist
  • Child safety education
  • Situation-based response information
  • Official reporting and support bodies
Official information sources
National police, the health and welfare ministry, local governments, public and expert child safety education bodies
What we do not do · conditions
Only material provided by official bodies and experts is used. This is not a feature to monitor or track a child.
Current status
Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed

27 · Child Care Institutions

Lasting attention and connection
for children who need protection.

Aims to connect, through official information, the facilities where children in need of protection live and the sponsorship and volunteer programs that support them.

What we guide

  • Child care facility information
  • Group home information
  • Official sponsorship programs
  • Volunteer programs of qualified institutions
  • Goods donation guidance
  • Corporate social contribution cooperation
Official information sources
Health and welfare ministry, local governments, qualified child welfare facilities, official sponsor organizations
What we do not do · conditions
Names, photos, locations or stories of children living in facilities are never published. Only institutions with confirmed cooperation are shown as partners.
Current status
Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed

28 · Child Protection

Every child has the right
to be kept safe.

Aims to guide support systems for children in need of protection and family foster care information, based on official sources.

What we guide

  • Support systems for protected children
  • Family foster care information
  • Child protection agency guide
  • Family support information
  • Official counseling and support channels
Official information sources
Health and welfare ministry, the child rights agency, local governments, child protection agencies, foster care support centers
What we do not do · conditions
Personal data of protected children is not collected or published. Individual cases are not judged or counseled; official counseling and support channels are pointed to.
Current status
Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed

29 · Adoption Information

Accurate information and careful preparation
on the way to becoming a family.

Aims to accurately guide the public procedures, official bodies and post-adoption family support information that adoption requires.

What we guide

  • Public adoption procedures
  • Official adoption bodies
  • Information for prospective adoptive parents
  • Post-adoption family support
  • Official counseling channels
Official information sources
Health and welfare ministry and the child rights agency, and other public adoption bodies
What we do not do · conditions
BABYLOG does not connect children with prospective parents or broker adoption, and has no revenue model tied to adoption brokerage. Information on children eligible for adoption is not collected or published.
Current status
Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed

30 · Donation & Sponsorship

So that small acts of care
can help a child's daily life.

Aims to help people who want to donate, sponsor or volunteer find official sponsor organizations and programs.

What we guide

  • Official sponsor organizations
  • Regular and one-time sponsorship information
  • Goods donation
  • Corporate social contribution
  • Official volunteer programs
  • Sponsorship transparency guidance
Official information sources
Officially registered sponsoring nonprofits, local governments, qualified child welfare facilities

Donations are not shown as BABYLOG's general revenue, and BABYLOG will not raise funds directly until legal requirements and an operating system are in place.
Current status
Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed
