REVENUE MODEL

Six revenue streams.

The revenue model is being designed. The base design does not have Newon hold transaction funds; prices and fees will be decided after confirming supplier contracts, travel-business registration, cancellation and refund responsibility, and payment and settlement structure.

  1. 01

    Subscription

    CustomersIndividual travelers & businesses

    A personal subscription (premium records, AI and planning) and a business subscription are being designed. Prices are not final.

  2. 02

    Booking & packages

    CustomersTravelers & travel businesses

    Flights, stays, tours and packages are to be connected through external booking affiliates. No products are shown before partnership contracts.

  3. 03

    Travel commerce

    CustomersConsumers & sellers

    For shopping and group buying we are reviewing direct-listing commissions and external affiliates. No double commission on the same transaction.

  4. 04

    Ads & content

    CustomersBrands & creators

    Ads, content and promotions are under review; ads and partnerships are always clearly labelled.

  5. 05

    B2B SaaS

    CustomersAgencies, stays & experience providers

    A business subscription or licence for travel-business operations tools, in concept.

  6. 06

    Public & global

    CustomersInstitutions & overseas companies

EXECUTION ROADMAP

Not everything at once —
we expand step by step.

Travel is highly competitive, and legal responsibility and supplier contracts matter. So we expand in a validated order.

We are in stage 1 today. Stage 2 onwards are future plans and timing is not fixed.

  1. 01

    Stage 1 Current stage

    Complete the travel-record app

    Stabilise the world map, visited countries, records, photos and stats, and grow the user base. Prepare the V2.0 structure around the world-map home screen.

  2. 02

    Stage 2

    Planning & AI

    Expand itineraries, place discovery, budgets and planning assistance, and prepare shared itineraries.

  3. 03

    Stage 3

    Booking & package partnerships

    Validate real booking demand through partnerships with qualified stay, tour, activity and package providers. No products are shown before contracts.

  4. 04

    Stage 4

    Commerce & community

    Prepare travel-gear shopping, secondhand, group buying and a shared community built on server integration.

  5. 05

    Stage 5

    Business SaaS, public & global

    Expand to booking and operations SaaS for travel businesses, corporate travel, tourism-body cooperation and global licensing.

COMMERCE PRINCIPLES

Discovery in MY WORLD,
transactions in a responsible structure.

  1. 01

    MY WORLD is where travel gear, secondhand items and group-buying products are explored and discovered in a travel context.

  2. 02

    For general products, we are reviewing using SHAREON — Newon’s shared commerce infrastructure — for orders, payments, settlement and refunds. It is not connected today.

  3. 03

    We do not charge double commission on the same transaction.

  4. 04

    Flights, stays, packages and tours are reviewed separately, in line with supplier contracts and applicable law.

  5. 05

    In the base design, Newon does not hold travelers’ transaction funds. We make clear that payment and refund responsibility lies with the supplier or external affiliate.

  6. 06

    Seller and supplier information and delivery, cancellation and refund rules are shown with each product; products whose rules cannot be confirmed are not presented.

TRUST & COMPLIANCE

Principles we review to build
a travel platform people can trust.

These principles are reviewed and followed before any expansion. Service scope may change depending on the outcome.

  1. 01

    Travel-business registration

    We review the registration and contract structure required to sell or broker travel products, and sell nothing before those requirements are met.

  2. 02

    Ticketing & booking responsibility

    We make clear whether the supplier or the platform is responsible for ticketing and booking.

  3. 03

    Cancellation & refunds

    Cancellation and refund rules for stays, tours and packages are shown with supplier policies before booking.

  4. 04

    Insurance regulations

    Insurance guidance is offered only after review of solicitation and brokerage rules, and stays at information and official channels.

  5. 05

    Personal & location data

    Travel records and location data are handled only as far as needed, and protected. How to delete your account is published.

  6. 06

    Companion & community safety

    Companion and community features come with reporting, blocking and moderation policies, and are not presented as shared before server integration.

  7. 07

    Reviews, ads & partner labels

    No fake reviews or unlabelled ads; partners and ads are clearly labelled.

  8. 08

    Available vs planned

    We always distinguish features available today from planned features, and update the labels when status changes.

Items marked “In preparation” or “Under review” are long-term plans. Timing and scope depend on supplier contracts, applicable law and service validation.
