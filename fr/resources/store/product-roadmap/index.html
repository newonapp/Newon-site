OVERVIEW

A roadmap is not
a promise list.

Instead of pinning features to dates,
hold tension across Now, Next, and Later.

Themes and initiatives group goals,
metrics define success,
and a decision log keeps why you deferred.

The template aims at a shareable direction doc
and a common language for the build team.

HOW TO USE

How to use it.

  1. 01

    One Vision sentence

    Before moving feature lists,
    lock the long-term problem on Vision.

  2. 02

    Sort backlog into Now / Next / Later

    Place every item in one of three columns.
    Keep only this cycle’s capacity in Now.

  3. 03

    Bind with themes

    Connect initiatives on Theme Map
    so you can say why these items are together now.

  4. 04

    Attach success metrics

    Add Metrics to major Now and Next items.
    Separate checkbox-done from success.

  5. 05

    Habitual Decision Log

    When you decline or defer, write why on Decisions
    and refresh the shareable summary.
