Today the PETLOG app focuses on records, health and family care. Shopping, booking and care are business plans prepared step by step; this website only introduces them.

OUR VISION

Better pet life starts by
connecting scattered moments.

  1. 01

    Life with a pet is scattered across records, vets, shopping, care and community.

  2. 02

    PETLOG keeps the records you add every day at the centre and connects goods, services, experts and businesses in one flow.

  3. 03

    We start with dogs and cats and expand to other pets later.

  4. 04

    Owners' records belong to owners. PETLOG does not diagnose and never shares records with businesses without consent.
