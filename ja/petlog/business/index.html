PETLOG

A pet life business
connecting owners and providers.

PETLOG starts as an owner's record app. Its long-term direction is an integrated pet platform shared with vets, groomers, hotels, sitters, trainers and sellers.

BUSINESS DIRECTION

Connecting owners
and pet businesses.

Most of the services pet life needs are made by small local businesses. Owners struggle to find places they can trust, and businesses struggle to reach good customers.

PETLOG puts the record app owners already use at the centre and adds a market, booking and business tools on top, step by step, to connect the two sides.

Trust matters most in that connection. Owners' records never pass to businesses without consent, and businesses join only after checks.

REVENUE OPPORTUNITIES

Revenue follows
once trust is built.

PETLOG has three revenue directions: subscriptions for owners, commissions from the market and booking, and business services for pet businesses.

None of them has a price or rate yet. They will be set after reviewing seller and provider margins, payment and refund costs, legal limits and the value owners actually feel.

Some conditions come before any revenue: a stable record app, security and backup, checked sellers and providers, and a payment structure that has passed legal, PG and security review.

  • No prices or rates set
  • No user numbers published
  • No signed partnerships
  • No payments yet

ORDER OF WORK

In the order we set,
one step at a time.

We don't add everything at once. Each step starts only once the one before it is stable.

  1. Top priority

    Stability, security, backup

    App stability, data security and record backup come first.

  2. Home and Care redesign

    Making the everyday Home and Care screens easier and clearer.

  3. Market

    Opening a market for discovering pet goods.

  4. Booking pilot

    Trying grooming, hotel and training booking on a small scale.

  5. Seller onboarding

    An onboarding process for sellers who pass identity and eligibility checks.

  6. Shared NEWON payments

    Payment infrastructure shared by NEWON apps, prepared with legal, PG and security review.

  7. Business center

    Booking, customer and operations tools for pet businesses.

  8. Short-form video

    Short videos for sharing pets' everyday moments.

  9. AI and global

    The AI care assistant and multilingual expansion abroad.

This is the planned order; no dates are set. Scope and order may change after review.

TRUST & COMPLIANCE

Standards that stay
as the business grows.

  • PETLOG does not diagnose disease or replace a vet's judgement.
  • Pet records and personal data are never shared automatically with sellers or businesses.
  • Booking and payments open only after legal, payment-gateway (PG) and security review.
  • Sellers join only after identity and eligibility checks.
  • Regulated items such as animal medicines are not handled until legal review.
  • No trade in live animals, and no illegal breeding or sales.
  • No insurance solicitation, brokerage or comparison without proper licences and contracts.
  • Showing official information is not the same as being an official partner.
