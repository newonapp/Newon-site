AFTER YOU SEND

What happens
after you write.

  1. 01

    Received

    We check what arrives through the form or at newon@newon.app.

  2. 02

    Reviewed

    We look at it by inquiry type. Partnership proposals are reviewed for fit, legal and safety requirements, and business or seller eligibility.

  3. 03

    Reply

    After review we reply to the email you left. Sending an inquiry does not create a partnership.

BEFORE YOU WRITE

Please check before writing.

  • For app issues, include your device, app version and the screen where it happened.

  • We only ask for your name, email and message, and use them only to reply.

  • Please do not include personal or sensitive data the inquiry does not need, such as original vet records, national ID numbers, bank or card numbers, or your exact home address.

  • For partnership or seller inquiries, include a company introduction and proposed scope. Sending an inquiry does not create a partnership.

  • For insurance sign-up, claims or adoption procedures, please use each insurer's or official shelter's own channel.

  • Booking, payment, seller applications and sales are not offered on this website.

  • PETLOG cannot diagnose health problems; please contact a nearby vet, or an emergency clinic right away in an emergency.

CONTACT FAQ

Common questions before writing

I'm not sure which type to choose.

Pick the closest type, or send it as 'General and other proposals'. We review it based on what you write.

How will I get an answer?

After review we reply to the email you left in the form.

My pet is unwell — can I ask here?

PETLOG cannot diagnose. Please contact a nearby vet, or an emergency clinic right away in an emergency.

Can I book or apply as a seller on the website?

No. Booking, payment, seller applications and sales are not offered on this website. Tell us about listing or partnership interest through the form.

Where do investment or press inquiries go?

Both investment inquiries and interview or press requests go through NEWON's contact page; you can also write to newon@newon.app.
