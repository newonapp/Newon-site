CountUp

Count tracking · Goal progress · AI insights

毎日の行動をカウントし、目標達成率・連続記録・成長の流れを一括管理するスマートカウントアプリです。

プロジェクトを見る App Store Google Play

アイデア

CountUp is

a smart counting platform to log repeated actions and goals as numbers—and see consistency visually.

Track workouts, reading, quitting smoking, study time, habit streaks, and every step of growth in one place.

Use AI analysis to spot patterns and build better routines.

主な機能

Count logging

Log today's actions and manage counts and cumulative data easily. Track workouts, reading, study, water intake, and more.

Goal settings

Set target counts and periods, then check progress in real time. Small goals lead to big results.

Streak tracking

See streak records and turn steady practice into habits. A growth system that boosts your sense of achievement.

Milestones

Save achievements at 100, 500, and 1,000 counts— and celebrate moments of growth.

Statistics

Analyze daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly logs. See your growth journey visually through data.

Chart visualization

View cumulative logs and growth trends as graphs. Understand change at a glance.

AI insights

AI analyzes your patterns and suggests improvements and goals. Helps you build more effective habits.
