This page is currently available in English. Translations are in preparation.

WHY MY WORLD

Meet travelers from the moment
they record their trips.

  1. 01

    MY WORLD is with travelers when they record where they have been and prepare for where they will go next.

  2. 02

    We aim for a structure where partners’ products and content are discovered naturally within the flow of records, planning, booking, commerce and community.

  3. 03

    Ads and partnerships are clearly labelled, and travelers’ trust comes first.

PARTNERSHIP MODELS

Six partnership models.

  1. 01

    Booking partnership

    Connect qualified suppliers’ flights, stays, packages and tours to travelers’ itineraries.

  2. 02

    Listing & sales

    Present travel gear, regional tourism products and experiences on MY WORLD.

  3. 03

    Content collaboration

    Create routes, guidebooks and campaigns with creators and guides.

  4. 04

    Institutional cooperation

    Run campaigns and tourism-data cooperation with tourism bodies and local governments.

  5. 05

    Technology & API

    Integrate with booking, operations and payment technology partners and build SaaS together.

  6. 06

    Global licensing

    Work with overseas partners on multilingual and localised business.

PARTNERSHIP PROCESS

From proposal to operation,
in five steps.

  1. 01

    Proposal

    Proposal

    Send your partner type and proposal through our contact channels.

  2. 02

    Review

    Review

    We review it against service direction, applicable law and traveler value.

  3. 03

    Discussion

    Discussion

    We discuss scope, data integration and operations.

  4. 04

    Contract

    Contract

    We set out terms and responsibilities and sign a contract.

  5. 05

    Operation

    Operation

    After signing, we launch and operate it together.
