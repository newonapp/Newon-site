PARTNERSHIP VISION

Meet travelers from the moment
they record their trips.

MY WORLD is there when travelers record where they have been and prepare where to go next. Records on the map — and the countries still blank — become the interest behind the next trip.

  • Rather than partners’ products interrupting like billboards, we aim for them to be found at the moment they are needed, within the flow of planning, travel and records.

  • Ads and partnerships are clearly labelled and travelers’ trust comes first. Individual routes, passports and bookings are never provided to partners.

  • We are at an early stage with no signed partnerships. So we validate together through small PoCs, and never show partners’ products as bookable before a contract.

PARTNERSHIP MODELS

Six partnership models.

  • Booking partnerships

    Link eligible suppliers’ stays, tours, transport and flights to travelers’ itineraries — starting with labelled affiliate links to the partner’s official booking page.

    OTAs & booking platforms · Airlines · Hotels & resorts · Transport operators

  • Listing & sales

    Present travel goods, local tourism products and experiences, with seller details and delivery, cancellation and refund terms.

    Travel brands · Local tourism businesses · Activity operators

  • Content collaboration

    Create itineraries, local guides and official tourism content with content companies, creators and tourism bodies. Ads and sponsorship are labelled.

    Content companies & creators · Local guides · Tourism boards

  • Joint campaigns

    Design campaigns that use the map and records together, such as visit-record missions and seasonal campaigns.

    Tourism boards & organisations · Local tourism offices · Travel brands

  • Technology & data

    Content and product information feeds and non-identifying aggregated reports are under review. Security and data-handling standards come first.

    Travel technology companies · Tourism bodies

  • Global & localisation

    Multilingual content and local product partnerships with overseas partners, respecting local law and privacy rules.

    Global travel partners · Overseas tourism bodies

DATA & API

Data and API links —
possibilities and limits.

There is no public API today. Links are reviewed contract by contract within these principles.

  • Links we can review

    Feeds that show partners’ official content and product information in the app, labelled affiliate deep links, and aggregated reports that cannot identify individuals.

    Content feed · Affiliate links · Non-identifying stats

  • The order we follow

    PoCs start small with links and files; system integration is considered only after a formal contract and security review.

    PoC → Contract → Security review → Integration

  • What we never do

    Provide or sell individual routes, passports or bookings, share between apps without consent, or expose real-time location.

    No selling personal data · Consent first

PARTNERSHIP PROCESS

From proposal to operation,
in six steps.

  1. 01

    Proposal

    Proposal

    Send the partnership type, your proposal and an introduction by email or the official inquiry form. See the checklist below for what to include.

  2. 02

    Review

    Review

    We review traveler value, service direction and relevant law, and may ask for more material. Travel-agency registration, consumer protection and privacy standards are checked too.

  3. 03

    Discussion

    Discussion

    We discuss scope, data handling, operations and responsibilities, making clear who holds booking and refund responsibility.

  4. 04

    Pilot

    PoC / pilot

    Agree scope and metrics, test small and review the results together — for example one region and one season.

  5. 05

    Contract

    Contract

    Terms and responsibilities are set in a contract. Partners are not announced, and their products are not shown as bookable, before signing.

  6. 06

    Operation

    Operation

    Launch and operate together, improving with traveler feedback. Ads and partnerships stay clearly labelled in operation.

PARTNER VALUE

The value partners
can expect.

We do not promise results. Instead we are clear about the value we can verify together.

  • When travel intent forms

    Meet travelers at the moment they choose and prepare their next destination. The blank countries and bucket list on their map are exactly that interest.

  • Context from records

    Within what users agree to, appear with information that fits their plans and records. Individual routes, passports and bookings are never provided to partners.

  • Trust through labels

    Clear ad and partnership labels help build an environment where travelers choose with confidence. Paid placement is never made to look like verified excellence.

  • Small, verifiable steps

    PoCs confirm scope and metrics first, so both sides start with less risk. We decide on a contract after reviewing the results together.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions
about partnerships.

  • Do you have signed partners today?

    No. We are at an early stage with no signed partnerships. That is why we validate together with a small PoC, and before a contract we never announce a partner or show products as bookable.

  • Can small businesses or local guides propose?

    Yes. Small stays such as pensions and guesthouses, local experiences and shops, and local guides are all partner types. Please include your business registration, any required qualifications and safety standards.

  • What are the partnership fees or settlement terms?

    There are no fixed rates. Partnerships that link to external booking sites follow the payment terms in that contract; other terms are set through discussion and contract.

  • Can we receive user data?

    Individual routes, passports and bookings are never provided or sold. Even in institutional work, only aggregated statistics that cannot identify individuals are considered.

  • Can overseas companies and institutions propose?

    Yes. We consider multilingual, localisation and overseas product partnerships with travel companies, platforms and tourism bodies abroad. Please include the target countries and direction.

  • Can we start with an API integration?

    There is no public API today. A PoC starts small with links and files; system integration is considered after a formal contract and a security review.
