WHY MY WORLD

Meet travelers from the moment
they record their trips.

  1. 01

    MY WORLD is with travelers when they record where they have been and prepare for where they will go next. The records on their world map become the interests behind the next trip.

  2. 02

    We aim for a structure where partners’ products and content are discovered naturally within the flow of records, planning, booking, commerce and community.

  3. 03

    Ads and partnerships are clearly labelled, and travelers’ trust comes first.

  4. 04

    Before a partnership contract, partner products are never shown as bookable, and in the base design Newon does not hold transaction funds.

PARTNERSHIP MODELS

Six partnership models.

  1. 01

    Booking partnership

    Connect qualified suppliers’ flights, stays, packages and tours to travelers’ itineraries. Payment and booking responsibility stays with the supplier or external affiliate.

  2. 02

    Listing & sales

    Present travel gear, regional tourism products and experiences on MY WORLD, with seller information and refund rules shown.

  3. 03

    Content collaboration

    Create routes, guidebooks and campaigns with creators and guides. Ads and sponsorships are clearly labelled.

  4. 04

    Institutional cooperation

    Run campaigns and tourism-data cooperation with tourism bodies and local governments, following public-procurement procedures.

  5. 05

    Technology & API

    Integrate with booking, operations and payment technology partners and build SaaS together, aligning on security and data handling first.

  6. 06

    Global licensing

    Work with overseas partners on multilingual and localised business, following local law and privacy standards.

PARTNERSHIP PROCESS

From proposal to operation,
in five steps.

  1. 01

    Proposal

    Proposal

    Send your partner type, proposal and company overview through our contact channels.

  2. 02

    Review

    Review

    We review it against service direction, applicable law and traveler value, and may ask for more material.

  3. 03

    Discussion

    Discussion

    We discuss scope, data integration, operations and responsibilities.

  4. 04

    Contract

    Contract

    We set out terms and responsibilities and sign a contract. Partners are not announced before contract.

  5. 05

    Operation

    Operation

    After signing, we launch and operate it together, improving it with travelers’ feedback.
