MY WORLD

Every moment of travelling the world,
in one place.

From the first idea to planning, booking, experiences, records and sharing. MY WORLD aims to be a global travel platform that connects travelers, travel businesses and local tourism.

NOW

Today MY WORLD is a travel-record app available on Google Play and the App Store. Colour the countries and cities you have visited on the world map, and look back on trips through routes, photos, notes, travel stats and an AI travel report. Booking, a market and a community shared with other users are not available yet.

WHAT IS MY WORLD

From the world you’ve seen
to your next trip.

MY WORLD starts with colouring the countries you have visited on a world map, and aims to connect travel records, planning, booking links, community and travel trade in one flow. When a trip ends, the record stays on your map — and becomes the starting point of the next one.

Explore the services →Go to contact →

  1. Record platform

    World-map colouring, visited countries and cities, travel journal and stats. Where MY WORLD began, and the core of the app today. We also hold to the principle that the countries and trips you record should be easy to export and restore.

    Available in the app

  2. Travel platform

    Itineraries, budgets, checklists and AI planning, then flights, stays, experiences and transport through eligible partners — step by step. We do not sell tickets or travel products ourselves; linking to partners’ official booking pages comes first.

    Partly available · booking links planned

  3. Trade platform

    We are reviewing trade that is directly about travel only: travel goods, second-hand, group buying and equipment rental. This is not a general shopping mall, and gear rental is planned to work with SHAREON, NEWON’s rental platform.

    Under review

  4. Business platform

    Operating tools (SaaS) and partnership models for travel agencies, local operators and tourism bodies are long-term goals. Connecting to external stay or flight booking systems requires a formal contract with that company.

    Under review

PROBLEM & SOLUTION

What travelers struggle with,
and how MY WORLD helps.

Travel demand is large, yet recording, planning and sharing around a booking are still left to each traveler.

  • Problem

    Travel memories end up scattered

    Photos sit in the camera roll, plans in notes, bookings in email. A few years later it is hard even to list which countries you visited and when.

    How MY WORLD helps

    Colour countries and cities on a map, and keep photos, notes and routes together, trip by trip.

  • Problem

    No reason to come back after booking

    Booking-centred services finish their job at checkout. Everything during and after the trip happens elsewhere.

    How MY WORLD helps

    Your map and records keep growing after each trip, giving a reason to use the app before, during and after travel.

  • Problem

    Few planning tools fit the individual

    Itineraries, packing lists, expenses and sharing with companions are often spread across different apps and spreadsheets.

    How MY WORLD helps

    Itinerary, checklist and expense tools sit in the same app as your records, expanding to shared plans and AI drafts.

  • Problem

    Small local suppliers struggle to be found

    Small tour, activity and stay operators find it hard to stand out on large platforms and lack operating tools.

    How MY WORLD helps

    Long term, a brokerage that connects only verified operators, plus tools built for them.

WHO IT IS FOR

Who uses MY WORLD,
and who it will work with.

We start with individuals who travel abroad often, then widen to groups, businesses and institutions in order.

Services for businesses and institutions are planned or under review.

  • Frequent individual travelers

    Our initial core customers: people in their twenties and thirties who travel abroad at least twice a year and enjoy collecting and revisiting the countries they have seen.

  • Travelers organising old trips

    People whose past trips are scattered across photo albums and social media and who want them sorted by country, city and year.

  • Family and friend groups

    Groups sharing plans, packing and costs. Shared itineraries and albums are planned.

  • Travel creators

    People who share experience as content — itineraries, guides, photo-spot maps (under review).

  • Travel businesses

    Agencies, stays and tour or activity operators who want to meet people preparing a trip (after partner contracts).

  • Tourism bodies and local governments

    Institutions that want to present places from a traveler’s point of view and turn interest into real visits (under review).

TRAVEL JOURNEY

From discovery to sharing,
one continuous journey.

The six-stage travel flow MY WORLD aims to connect. Select a stage to see what it covers and what the app can do today.

01 · Discover

The moment you find where to go

Countries and cities still blank on your map, and your bucket list, become the start of the next trip. Reviews, local information and creator content are planned to widen this.

Seeing the countries you have not coloured yet is itself what sparks the next trip. The more records build up, the clearer that starting point becomes.

  • Travel goals
  • Bucket list
  • Destination ideas
  • Reviews
  • Local information

In the app today

You can save target countries and a bucket list. Reviews, local information and creator content are planned.

02 · Plan

The moment excitement becomes a plan

Prepare with an itinerary, checklist and expense records, expanding to shared itineraries, AI drafts, saved places and route planning.

Create a trip by country, city, dates and party size, then organise a day-and-time itinerary and a packing checklist. Co-editing and link sharing with family and friends are planned after server integration.

  • Itinerary & calendar
  • Checklist
  • Expenses
  • AI draft itinerary
  • Co-editing

In the app today

Itinerary and calendar, checklist and expense records are available. Co-editing and AI drafts are in the development plan.

03 · Book

The moment you reach the right booking

We plan to connect flights, stays, experiences and transport through eligible partners, starting with links to partners’ official booking pages.

Before running ticketing or sales ourselves, contracts, travel-agency requirements and refund responsibility are reviewed first. Car rental and vehicle transport follow their own legal and insurance requirements.

  • Flights
  • Stays
  • Tours & activities
  • Transport
  • Packages

In the app today

Not available. No bookable product is shown before a partner contract.

04 · Travel

The moment you experience a new place

Check official safety information, emergency contacts, travel documents and your budget in one place while you are away.

As a principle, location sharing during a trip is offered only to group members who explicitly agree, and real-time location is never shown on public profiles.

  • Safety & emergency
  • Travel documents
  • Budget & spending
  • Official tourism info

In the app today

Expense records work during a trip. Safety information and document tools are planned.

05 · Record

The moment every place goes on your map

Leave visited countries and cities, routes, photos and notes on your own world map. This is the core MY WORLD stage you can use in the app right now.

Quick capture during the trip and, back home, a timeline and travel album are the planned next steps.

  • World-map colouring
  • City & region records
  • Routes
  • Travel stats
  • AI travel report

In the app today

World map, city and region records, routes, photos and notes, travel stats and the AI travel report are available.

06 · Share

The moment you share the memories

Share memories and routes with family, friends and a traveler community. Map share images and keepsakes such as photo books and posters are also considered here.

Turning records themselves into keepsakes — such as a poster of the countries you have visited — is also under review.

  • Map share image
  • Route sharing
  • Community
  • Shared album
  • Photo book & poster

In the app today

A community shared with other users is not available yet; it needs server integration first.

“Record” is the heart of the app today; the other stages are partly available or planned.

GLOBAL VISION

A world map for
travelers everywhere.

MY WORLD aims to grow into a global travel platform where travelers anywhere record the countries and cities they have visited and discover new travel experiences and services. The starting point is always colouring visited countries on the world map.

Today the app on Google Play and the App Store offers the world map and travel records, with app screens in several languages. The directions below are future plans; target countries and timing have not been decided.

  • World map and travel records first

    Core record features — colouring visited countries, city records, photos, statistics and visit history — are refined into one shared experience that works the same way in any country.

  • An app that feels local

    Beyond language, we plan an experience that reflects currency, date formats, place names and travel culture.

  • Travel information and community

    Country and city travel information, trip planning, and travelers sharing reviews and recommended places are reviewed step by step.

  • Connecting local travel services

    Partnerships with tourism, experience, accommodation and transport providers are reviewed contract by contract after checking each country’s law and privacy rules.

LONG-TERM VISION

Not everything at once —
growing in order of priority.

Travel is highly competitive, and legal responsibility and supplier contracts matter. So we first complete the map and records that make MY WORLD distinctive, then expand in a validated order.

We are at priority 1. Later priorities are long-term plans without fixed dates.

  1. Priority 1 Current stage

    Complete the world map & records

    Stabilise the world map, visited countries and cities and travel records, and protect existing users’ records and subscriptions.

  2. Priority 2

    Planning, groups, AI & subscription

    Widen itineraries, group trips and AI planning so the app is used from the moment a trip is prepared, and refine premium subscription.

  3. Priority 3

    Stay, tour & experience partners

    Validate booking demand directly tied to travel, on a small scale, with eligible partners.

  4. Priority 4

    Market, packages & creators

    Expand to travel-related goods and content, and packages from registered agencies.

  5. Priority 5

    Business SaaS, tourism bodies & global

    Build recurring revenue and an overseas base through operator tools, tourism-body partnerships and country-by-country booking.

NEWON PLATFORMS

Travel that connects with
other NEWON platforms.

MY WORLD is an independent service run by NEWON. Each app keeps its own core features; we are considering natural links at the moments a trip is needed.

No data is currently linked or automatically shared with other NEWON services. Travel, family, health and location information is never shared between apps without the user’s consent.

  • MY WORLD

    World-map travel records and planning, booking and trade links. The travel hub that ties before, during and after a trip together.

  • BABYLOG

    Preparing family trips with children. We are considering links to itineraries and packing suited to travelling with kids.

  • PETLOG

    Travelling with pets and pet-friendly information. We are considering links to pet-friendly stays and travel information.

  • ONGIL

    Travelling with older relatives, accessibility information. We are considering links to travel information that accounts for mobility and accessibility.

  • LivOn

    Holidays, life goals and travel budgets. We are considering a link that shows travel goals and budgets within life plans.

  • SHAREON

    Renting travel, camping and camera gear. We are considering a structure where booking, deposits and returns run through SHAREON (SHAREON is in development).

FAQ

Frequently asked questions
about MY WORLD.

  • What kind of app is MY WORLD?

    A travel-record app where you colour the countries and cities you have visited on a world map and keep photos, notes and routes for each trip. Travel stats and the AI travel report help you look back, and it is available on Google Play and the App Store.

  • Is it free to use?

    World-map colouring and basic visit records are free. The app has an existing premium subscription; its benefits and price follow what the app and store show.

  • Can I record trips or book on the website?

    No. The website introduces the business and does not offer records, booking, payment or sign-in. The real features are in the MY WORLD app.

  • Is MY WORLD trying to become a booking app?

    Booking is one stage among several. The centre of MY WORLD is the world map and records that keep building after a trip ends; booking starts, after partner contracts, with links to partners’ official booking pages.

  • Are my records connected to other NEWON apps?

    Not today — nothing is linked or shared automatically. Even if links come later, travel, family, health and location information is never shared between apps without the user’s consent.

  • Can I use it abroad?

    The world map and travel records work regardless of country, and the app screens are available in several languages. Features with country-specific rules, such as booking and payment, are reviewed market by market.

This page introduces the MY WORLD business. The website does not offer booking, ordering, payment, sign-in, seller registration or record entry — the actual features live in the MY WORLD app.

“Planned” and “Under review” items are long-term ideas from the final integrated business plan. They are not all built today; timing and scope depend on supplier contracts, applicable law and service validation.
