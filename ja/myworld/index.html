MY WORLD · GLOBAL TRAVEL & EXPERIENCE PLATFORM

Every moment of experiencing the world,
in one connection.

MY WORLD aims to be a global travel platform that connects inspiration and planning with booking, transport, stays, experiences, shopping, records and sharing — richer journeys for travelers and new business opportunities for partners across the travel industry.

NOWToday the MY WORLD app is a travel-record service that marks the countries and cities you have visited on a world map. Booking, shopping and a shared community are being prepared step by step.

Get it on Google PlayExplore the platformPartnership inquiry

The iOS version is in preparation.

OUR VISION

From the first idea to the last memory,
one connected journey.

  1. 01

    A trip is one journey — inspiration, planning, booking, transport, experiences, records and sharing. Today these steps are scattered across many services.

  2. 02

    Starting from the world map and travel records, MY WORLD aims to connect how travelers discover, prepare for and remember their next trip.

  3. 03

    Together with suppliers, brands, creators, regions and institutions, we are building a structure that helps the travel industry meet travelers more precisely.

TRAVEL ECOSYSTEM

An ecosystem that connects travelers
and the travel industry.

MY WORLD starts from one traveler’s records and aims to connect the many participants of the travel industry.

  1. 01

    Travelers

    Record, plan, and find the products and information they need in one place.

  2. 02

    Travel suppliers

    Travel agencies, flight and transport, stay, tour and activity operators meet travelers.

  3. 03

    Brands & sellers

    Travel-gear brands and sellers present products in a travel context.

  4. 04

    Creators & guides

    Bring travel content and local experiences to travelers.

  5. 05

    Regions & institutions

    Local businesses, tourism boards and governments promote destinations and connect visits.

NEWON ECOSYSTEM

Growing together with
Newon’s services.

MY WORLD is an independent service operated by Newon. Connections with other Newon services are reviewed service by service and introduced step by step.

  1. 01

    Newon+

    We are reviewing a connection to the unified account and membership used across Newon services.

    Under review
  2. 02

    SHAREON

    We are reviewing using Newon’s shared commerce infrastructure for orders, payments, settlement and refunds of travel gear, secondhand and group-buying products. It is not connected today.

    Under review
  3. 03

    Newon AI

    Newon’s AI capabilities power travel reports and recommendations.

    Partly available · expanding
  4. 04

    Newon app ecosystem

    Each service runs independently and connects only where needed, by the user’s choice.

    In preparation

This page introduces the MY WORLD business. The website does not offer booking, ordering, payment, sign-in, seller registration or record entry — the actual features live in the MY WORLD app.

Items marked “In preparation” or “Under review” are long-term plans. Timing and scope depend on supplier contracts, applicable law and service validation.
