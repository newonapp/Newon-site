NEWON LABS · UTILITY · DISTRIBUTION

From a QR that makes links
to a QR that reads response.

QR codes are easy to make.
Business cards, posters, menus, packaging —
it looks finished once they hit the offline world.

But how often they are scanned,
where traffic goes, and in which context people respond
stays hard to see.

Newon QR does not end at generation.
It connects distribute → scan → visit
and turns offline touchpoints into observable digital signals.

THE QUESTION

Can a QR be an intake touchpoint —
not just a link delivery tool?

QRs are easy to create, but hard to know how often they are scanned and where traffic goes. Creation UX alone does not complete the product value.

WHY THIS EXPERIMENT

How we frame the problem.

01

PROBLEM

Even after creating a QR, it is hard to know how much it is used.
Offline touchpoints do not become digital signals.

02

HYPOTHESIS

Connecting create → scan → visit makes offline intake easier to understand.
For small teams, minimal measurement may be enough.

03

EXPERIMENT

CREATE → SCAN → VISIT → SIGNAL
