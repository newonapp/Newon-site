NEWON AI / ENTERPRISE

AI that understands company work
and helps carry it out.

Newon Enterprise AI aims to connect company data and work flows so teams spend less time on repeat tasks, find needed information faster, and work with AI.

02

ASSIST

AI that supports judgment and getting work done

Drafts and sorting help people decide. Final calls stay with the owner.

03

EXECUTE

AI that runs repeat work in an approved range

Carry out repeat steps within set procedures and permissions, then report results.

04

CONNECT

AI that links tools and work flows

Review existing tools and procedures, then design links only where they are needed.

We do not present AI as acting on every task without human approval. Sensitive work is designed to stay under owner review and approval.

Solutions

AI that extends across company work.

Newon plans to develop AI solutions step by step around company size, industry, and how teams work. The areas below are not live products. They are in development.

01 · AI AGENTS

Company-shaped AI agents

Support a single flow for repeat gathering, sorting, drafting, and handing work to an owner.

02 · KNOWLEDGE AI

Internal document and knowledge search

Help staff find what they need without opening many folders themselves.

03 · CUSTOMER AI

Customer support and account AI

Sort repeat questions and leave complex or sensitive cases for a person.

04 · SALES & MARKETING AI

Sales and marketing AI

Help a salesperson organize accounts and prepare follow-ups and proposals.

05 · WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

Work-automation AI

Sort incoming requests, send them to an owner, then continue after approval.

06 · OPERATIONS AI

Operations and admin AI

Help staff find internal procedures, forms, and the right team.

07 · DATA & INSIGHTS

Data and decision-support AI

Help an owner compare sources and see what changed and what needs a closer look.

08 · ENTERPRISE AI PLATFORM

Enterprise AI platform and SaaS

A shared work environment where a company can choose AI features and manage them by team.

Process

From work analysis to operations,
built in stages.

From understanding the work to rollout and improvement.

  1. 01 DISCOVER

    Learn the work and the problem to solve

  2. 02 DESIGN

    Design AI features, data range, permissions, and review steps

  3. 03 VALIDATE

    Check function and accuracy with a limited prototype

  4. 04 BUILD

    Build needed features and links to existing systems

  5. 05 DEPLOY

    Apply in stages after tests and user training

  6. 06 IMPROVE

    Fix errors and improve features from real use

Trust

Control and trust that enterprise AI needs.

Adopting AI is not only about convenience. Access range, user permissions, review of results, and operations all need to be considered.

  • Data access by role
  • Separating company data from user data
  • Using only the information that is needed
  • Separate review for sensitive information
  • Sources and evidence on AI answers
  • Human approval for important actions
  • Work history and error checks
  • Security requirements for the operating environment

These are later product design principles. We do not list security certifications, compliance marks, audit results, or data-protection outcomes we do not hold.

Roadmap

Start with a small type of work,
then grow into a company AI environment.

No fixed launch date. Start by proving AI on a small type of work.

  1. 01

    Prove core AI on real work

  2. 02

    Company-shaped AI solutions

    Build solutions around each company’s data, work flow, and permissions.

  3. 03

    Enterprise AI SaaS

    Turn shared features into a subscription product for more than one company.

    A combined AI work environment

    Connect agents, company knowledge, automation, and operations tools in one platform.
