01 · PURPOSE

Purpose of
MY WORLD products.

  1. 01

    Gather daily plans, transport and booking details in one place, easy to find during the trip.

  2. 02

    Split flights, stays, food and activities into a budget beforehand and compare it with what you spend.

  3. 03

    Help tourism, stay and experience businesses manage bookings and operating checks on the same forms.

05 · USE CASES

How they
will be used.

01

A family of four abroad for a week

The itinerary planner holds daily transport and booking references, printed and on file.

02

Settling up after a trip with friends

Enter what each person paid in the split sheet and it works out who owes whom.

03

A guesthouse before peak season

The booking board shows free rooms by date, and the utilisation tool compares with last year.

FAQ

MY WORLD products
FAQ.

01Can I book travel with them?

No. They are forms for organising plans and budgets, with no booking or payment.

02Can I budget in foreign currencies?

Separate columns for each currency are planned; you enter exchange rates yourself.

03Does it tell me visa or entry rules?

It gives a checklist of things to check, not country-specific rules. Please check official guidance.

04Can a guesthouse use it for rooms?

Business products are planned under commercial and team licences for business use.

05When will they launch?

All are candidates with no launch date yet.
