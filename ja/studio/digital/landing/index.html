OVERVIEW

A landing page is short —
so the strategy must be clearer.

A typical website can explain brand and services across many pages. A Landing Page must persuade inside a limited screen.

Visitors judge fit on the first screen, gather more as they scroll, then decide to act.

A strong Landing Page answers the questions below clearly.

Newon Landing Page designs content order and UI structure so the whole page works as one conversion flow.

These six answers should be clear before design begins.

  • What do you offer?
  • Who is it for?
  • Why does it matter?
  • What is different?
  • Why trust it?
  • What should I do now?

SCOPE DIFFERENCE

Landing Page vs Web Design,
and design vs development, differ.

Landing Page fits one clear goal — launch, campaign, ads, pre-registration. Web Design fits a full site with many menus and content.

Projects may run Design only or Design + Development. Development, server, domain, forms, CMS, and analytics inclusion are separated at quoting.
