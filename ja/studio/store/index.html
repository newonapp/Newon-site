01 · PRODUCT CATEGORIES

Six product
categories.

Products are grouped by how they are used. Products on one topic can sit in several categories.

Planners & checklists

Forms for preparing things with a timeline and steps: birth preparation, travel preparation, moving out. Covers the items that are easy to miss.

Product packages

Templates and tools for one life stage or one job, bundled. Separate packages for individuals and businesses. (In planning)

03 · PLANNERS & TEMPLATES

How planners and
templates are used.

A template product is a form with sections and order already set. Below are examples of what each family contains; the actual contents are on each product page.

Preparation planners
A schedule split by week or month, step-by-step checklists, item lists and budget notes. E.g. birth preparation, travel preparation, moving out
Record sheets
Repeating date / item / note forms with a monthly summary page. E.g. growth records, pet care records, daily schedules
Budget & spending sheets
Spreadsheets with planned and actual spending side by side, monthly totals and remaining-amount formulas. E.g. parenting, travel, pet spending
Workbooks
Pages that ask questions to organise your thinking, plus an action plan. E.g. career exploration, start-up ideas, yearly goals
Business document kits
The documents for one workflow in order. E.g. requirements questionnaire → quotation → scope of work → contract draft → delivery checklist

04 · CALCULATORS & TOOLS

When calculators
and tools help.

Leave short but important calculations to a tool. The tools below are free today.

01

Counting days

Days left to a due date, a departure or a contract end with the D-day counter; the period between two dates with the date calculator.

02

Adding up costs

Total your monthly subscriptions with the subscription cost calculator; see a price change with the percentage calculator.

03

Making something

A QR code for a notice, a password for an account, a UTM link for a campaign, made on the spot.

04

Tidying text and data

Check length with the character counter; tidy working data with the JSON formatter and colour converter.

08 · HOW TO USE

Using downloadable
products and software.

Downloadable products

  1. 01
    Get the files

    Receive the files by the link or e-mail you are given. The product on sale today is delivered by e-mail.

  2. 02
    Make a copy

    Keep the original and work on a copy, so the same form can be reused.

  3. 03
    Edit

    Edit DOCX in a word processor and XLSX in a spreadsheet program. PDF is for viewing and printing.

  4. 04
    Print or send

    Within the licence, print what you have written or send it to the people you work with.

Software and web tools

  1. 01
    Open the page

    Web tools open in the browser without installation. A current browser is recommended.

  2. 02
    Enter values

    Values are calculated on your device. Avoid entering sensitive personal data.

  3. 03
    Save the result

    Copy the result or save it as an image or file; this varies by tool.

  4. 04
    Check the version

    Software products state supported environments and how updates work on their page at launch.

11 · REFUNDS, DOWNLOADS & SUPPORT

Refunds, downloads
and support.

Refunds & cancellation
The Studio pages don't set a separate refund policy. Refund and cancellation conditions follow what is shown on each product page and in the purchase guidance.
Downloads
The product on sale today is delivered by e-mail after the purchase inquiry is confirmed; there is no instant download. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder first.

Help with use
Opening files, how to use a form and web tool errors go through Studio Contact. Filling in documents for you or per-customer changes are not part of support.

FAQ

Digital Store
FAQ.

01Can I buy every product in the store now?

No. The paid product on sale is the NEWON Project Starter Kit, and the 13 free web tools are ready to use. Other families are in planning and are not sold before launch.

02Do the free web tools need an account?

No. They run in the browser without sign-up or log-in.

03What opens the files?

DOCX opens in a word processor, XLSX in a spreadsheet program and PDF in a PDF viewer. Each product page lists its formats.

04Can I reuse a form I bought?

Yes. Within the licence you can make copies and reuse them. You can't pass the original files on or sell them.

05Can I send completed documents to customers?

If the product allows business use, you can send what you filled in (a quotation, for example). Distributing the blank original form is not allowed.

06Can I find out prices of upcoming products?

We don't announce prices for products whose launch isn't confirmed. They are first published on the Newon Store product page.

07Can you make a form I need?

We don't make custom forms. Tell us what you need through Contact and we'll consider it when planning standard products.
