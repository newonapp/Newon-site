01 · Business Models

A business structure that grows
from individuals to companies and the public sector.

Revenue opportunities are individual premium subscriptions, parenting commerce and lawful transaction revenue, childcare and education institution SaaS, corporate family-benefit solutions, parenting brand campaigns, partnerships with qualified service providers, public-sector contracts, and global SaaS and licensing. Child protection, reporting and public support information and donations are kept separate from commercial revenue.

NEWON does not do per-client outsourcing or operations on behalf of clients. It runs its business around standardized in-house SaaS, platforms, licenses and automation products.

The revenue models below are types of business structure, not prices. Prices and fees are published only after an actual launch and approval.

The parenting log app is available today on the App Store and Google Play, and the experience is widening with information and everyday services.

07

Public & Child Safety

Public & Child Safety

4 lines

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership, and it does not replace professional judgment, official reporting or legal procedures.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

Business method
A public-interest area that guides public parenting support and child safety information free of charge and connects to official bodies.
Revenue model
No monetization targets children or protected persons. Public-sector cooperation is kept separate from public-interest information services, under separate contracts.

These criteria apply to every business line in this category.

Structures we do not use

  • Fees for missing-child reports or tips
  • Trading of child location data
  • Selling protected children's personal data

Business Roadmap

Expanding step by step,
in five stages.

The business is designed broadly, but it expands in the order of completing the core recording experience first and confirming user demand.

01

Current platform foundation

Strengthening the core app experience: feeding, sleep, growth and health records, family sharing, usability, data stability, backup and recovery.

02

Wider service experience

Widening the experience with shared family management, parenting schedules, and trusted parenting content and support information.

03

Family life ecosystem

Connecting family life as a whole with parenting services such as postpartum care, childcare and education, and with commerce and group buying.

04

Enterprise & institution solutions

Providing childcare and education institution SaaS, corporate family benefits and parenting brand solutions as standard products.

05

Global expansion

Reaching more families through a multilingual platform, SaaS licensing, and local government and public-sector cooperation.

The roadmap is an order of priorities, not release dates or revenue targets. The public and child welfare areas proceed at the stage where official cooperation and an operating system are confirmed.
