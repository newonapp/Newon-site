Partnership Vision

Trustworthy connections
from an app families open every day.

BABYLOG keeps the family's record at the center and aims to connect, in a verified way, the institutions, services and goods that record calls for. At this stage, cooperation means models and pilot proposals to validate together, not signed partnerships.

Only connections families need

Cooperation goes ahead when the information or service genuinely helps families.

Eligibility and official sources

Partners whose registration, qualifications and safety standards are checked, and official information, come first.

Public interest kept apart from commerce

Child protection and welfare cooperation is run as free links to official agencies, separate from commercial cooperation.

When Partnerships Open

Partnerships open
in development order.

Which partnerships are needed when, mapped to development stages P4–P9. The stages are an order of work, not launch dates or contract schedules.

  1. P4

    Community, institution search, booking

    The stage that needs life-service businesses such as kids' classes, family photo studios and kids cafes, and information from education and care institutions.

  2. P5

    Shopping, second-hand, group buying, Books

    Brand and seller onboarding and publisher partnerships are reviewed. Seller checks and safety standards for infant products come first.

  3. P6

    Toy Club, Family Box, subscriptions

    Starts with verified toy rental companies; Family Box is reviewed as a corporate birth gift and family benefit.

  4. P7

    Institution SaaS, smart devices

    Pilots of institution tools with daycare centres, kindergartens and academies; links with device makers that meet KC certification and security standards.

  5. P8

    Foster care, adoption, giving, independence

    Cooperation with official agencies, child welfare facilities and non-profits. Official information and emergency contacts can be offered from P3.

  6. P9

    Public and corporate partnerships, global

    Public contracts, corporate family benefits and social contribution, and the possibility of work with overseas companies and institutions.

Actual links with institutions and their operation follow only once verified partnerships and child protection safeguards are in place.

Data & API

Data links with consent
and the minimum scope.

BABYLOG does not offer a public API today. Below are the kinds of links that can be reviewed during a partnership and the principles we keep.

Data link principles

  • We never sell children's or family data
  • Records never leave without guardian consent
  • Only the minimum information is exchanged
  • Nothing is shared automatically with other NEWON apps either
  • Links proceed only after a separate contract and security review

  • Public and official information

    Linking officially published information such as vaccination schedules, support programs and missing-child prevention, with source and date last checked.

  • Institution notices and schedules

    Showing an institution's notices and schedules in the family calendar only when the guardian consents.

  • Goods and booking information

    Linking listed businesses' product information and available booking times, reviewed after market and booking features launch.

  • Device links

    Linking smart-device data to records under guardian consent and security standards. Under review.

CHILD SAFETY & SOCIAL IMPACT

Thirteen types of institutions
for child protection and public interest.

We seek institutions to cooperate with in order to convey official information accurately for children who need protection and support. Public-interest work is kept separate from commercial cooperation.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership, and it does not replace professional judgment, official reporting or legal procedures.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

Partner FAQ

Common questions before partnering.

Will we be listed as a partner once we inquire?

No. Only companies and institutions with a confirmed contract are shown as partners. An inquiry does not guarantee cooperation or a contract.

Can a partner receive child or family data?

No. Child and family data are never sold, and records are never sent out without guardian consent. Where a link is needed, only the minimum information is handled, under a separate contract and security review.

Is there a public API?

There is no public API today. Technology and API cooperation is reviewed through an inquiry.

Can we list products or create a seller account on the website?

No. The website only introduces BABYLOG and connects inquiries. The market and onboarding process are being prepared for the next app version.

What are the fees and contract terms?

Prices and fees are unconfirmed proposals and are not published until actual launch and approval. Individual terms are set during scope discussion and contract review.

Can overseas companies and institutions inquire?

Yes, through the global partnership inquiry. There are no contracted overseas partners today; we start by reviewing the possibility together.
