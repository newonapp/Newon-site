OVERVIEW

Plenty of opinions.
Not enough pattern.

User voices always exist when discussing product, marketing, or service improvement. The harder part is organizing them into segment needs and behavior patterns.

Reviews, support tickets, survey answers, interviews, community signals, and usage data each offer a different angle — but rarely in a form teams can decide from.

CONSUMER RESEARCH starts by defining what you need to learn. Then we collect, classify, and structure signals into segments, needs, pains, motivations, and opportunities in one insight brief.
