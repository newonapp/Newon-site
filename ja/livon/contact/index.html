Inquiry Guide

Inquiries by purpose

Choosing the right channel gets you a faster answer. Every link goes to NEWON's existing official contact channels.

Please do not include personal or sensitive information the answer does not need — ID numbers, health history, bank accounts or sensitive family circumstances. Your name, a contact for the reply and your question are enough.

Before you write

Helpful to include

  1. 01

    Inquiry type and your organisation

  2. 02

    The life area you have in mind (with its category number, if known)

  3. 03

    Timing and scope

  4. 04

    Links to any material

  5. 05

    For an error report, the page and the official source

  6. 06

    Please leave out sensitive personal data such as ID numbers or health details

There is no separate LIVON contact system or sign-up. NEWON's official contact page is used as it is. Personal legal, medical or financial advice requests are not answered — we point to the right official body instead. The market is in preparation, so product, booking and payment requests cannot be handled; for personal used-goods trading please use SHAREON. Prices and fees are still proposals, so no firm answers can be given.

Company and institution projects can also use the NEWON Business inquiry. NEWON Business →
