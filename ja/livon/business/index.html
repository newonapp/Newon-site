Principle

Forty categories,
not forty apps.

  1. 01

    One platform built on a shared data structure, life stages, event-led connections and managed official sources — the only way to keep updates manageable and information accurate.

  2. 02

    Standard products and our own platform — not custom client work.

  3. 03

    The free core comes first; business models sit on top of it. We do not make money by shrinking what is free.

Two Tracks

A life super app and
a service & expert market.

Track 1

Life super app

The app you open first at every stage. Information, records and official sources — free, no account.

  • Free core stays
  • Premium is a proposal
  • Standalone iOS & Android apps in preparation
Available (LIVON web)

Track 2

Service & expert market

Where informed users reach trustworthy services and experts. Opens once real sellers are on board.

  • Market tab says ‘in preparation’
  • No fake products, booking or payment
  • Used goods point to SHAREON
In preparation

Revenue Opportunities

Opportunities,
and honest caveats.

LIVON's opportunities sit where the moment of looking something up meets the moment of acting on it. Someone preparing a wedding needs a timeline, a budget and services together; someone preparing to retire needs pension information, a plan and expert advice together. A service market that bridges those moments, enterprise solutions for employees' life events and public solutions for residents are the main opportunities.

All of this is still a proposal. No prices are set for subscriptions, AI, business plans, advertising or commission, and no numbers appear on this page. Performance-based fees in regulated fields such as medical and legal services are on hold until a legal review is complete.

Caveats

  • Prices and rates not set — not published

  • Performance-based fees in regulated fields on hold (pending legal review)

  • No fees on public benefits or statutory payments

  • Free core and account-free use stay

  • No market revenue before real sellers exist

Operating Principles

We keep general information
apart from licensed services.

  • No insurance sales or brokerage.

  • No sale or recommendation of financial products.

  • No investment advice or personal recommendations.

  • No diagnosis, treatment or other medical practice.

  • No legal or tax representation.

  • No fees on public benefits or statutory payments.

  • No personal used-goods trading — that points to SHAREON.

  • Paid ads and partnerships are always labelled.

Medical, legal, insurance, financial and welfare information is general guidance with links to official bodies. It does not replace professional judgement.
