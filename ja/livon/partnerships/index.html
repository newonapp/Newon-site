Collaboration Vision

Checked information and services,
at the moment they are needed.

LIVON wants people to meet accurate information and services they can trust as they pass life's turning points. Companies and institutions gain a route to the people who need them at that moment. Everything below is a proposal; no agreements are signed and no pilots are running today.

  • User value first

    Every collaboration starts with whether it really helps users.

  • Transparent labels

    Sources, ads and partnerships are always shown apart.

  • Start small, then test

    Narrow pilots first, judged by their results.

Collaboration Models

Collaboration models

Employee life benefits

Partners HR and benefits teams

Company and public schemes in one flow when employees join, move, marry, have a baby, care for family or retire.

Value for the partner
Expected to raise use of benefits and reduce repeated questions.
Conditions
Minimal employee data; designed so the company cannot see an individual employee's situation.

Living-service links

Partners Moving, cleaning, tidying and repair providers

Checked providers shown at the right step of a moving or move-in checklist.

Value for the partner
A route to users at the moment they need the service.
Conditions
Starts only after provider checks, price and review display, and payment and refund rules; ads always labelled.

Education and certificates

Partners Education providers, exam and course platforms

Courses and materials shown beside exam dates and study plans.

Value for the partner
Meeting learners just as they start preparing.
Conditions
Official exam dates first; no wording that promises a pass.

Public life information

Partners Local governments and public bodies

Guidance built around residents' life events, such as moving in, a birth or settling as a young adult.

Value for the partner
Schemes explained clearly to the residents who need them.
Conditions
Public procurement and agreements followed; sources shown.

Finance, insurance and life platforms

Partners HR, finance, insurance and life-app businesses

General financial-literacy content, labelled ads and possible technical links, under review.

Value for the partner
An information touchpoint in the context of life events.
Conditions
LIVON never solicits, sells or brokers products; regulatory review comes first.

Data & API

Data and API possibilities

LIVON offers no external API today. Below are the possibilities under review and their principles.

  • LIVON is preparing its app launch to use official portal information and some public data in its guides; some providers are under review.
  • Under review: licensing life-event guidance content to companies and institutions
  • Under review: exchanging scheme and date information with institutions' systems
  • Under review: handing over only the items a user agrees to, to other NEWON apps
  • Principle: consent-based, minimal collection, aggregated information only where needed — nothing that identifies a person is provided.
  • Principle: access rights, security and processing contracts are agreed before any link.

How it works

How a partnership comes together.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    Tell us the type, purpose and life area through NEWON's official contact page.

  2. 02

    Review

    We check accuracy, value to users and regulatory conditions together; regulated fields go through legal review.

  3. 03

    Proposal

    We agree the scope and how it is shown — source, ad or partnership.

  4. 04

    Start

    Once an agreement is signed we start within its scope and keep the source and ad labelling rules.

Partnership FAQ

Questions before working together

Are there signed partners or running pilots?

No. The collaboration models and PoCs on this page are proposals, and no organisation is shown as a partner without a signed agreement.

Does using a public body's open information make it a partner?

No. Government and public bodies are shown only as the source of public information unless an agreement exists.

How are paid placements or sponsored content shown?

Anything paid for is always labelled as an ad or partnership and kept apart from general information and real reviews.

Can partners receive user data?

As a rule, no information that identifies a person is passed to partners. Where data is needed at all, it is consent-based and aggregated.

Can finance, insurance, medical or legal providers work with LIVON?

Within general information and official-channel guidance only. LIVON does not solicit, sell or broker products, diagnose or represent anyone legally, and regulatory review comes first.

Is a fee charged twice when two NEWON platforms are involved?

No. Two NEWON platforms never both charge a fee on the same transaction.
