Our Vision

Not just collecting information —
connecting it to the life you live now.

LIVON's strength is connection, not volume. It links information across fields around your life stage and what you are going through.

  1. 01

    Stage Start from where you are, from the teens to 70+.

  2. 02

    Event One life event — enlisting, a first job, marriage, retirement — shows every field it touches.

  3. 03

    Source Public schemes always show the official source, the date checked and the region covered.
