SHAREON PLATFORM

From things to spaces,
connected when you need them.

From everyday things to professional equipment and spaces. SHAREON aims for a sharing and rental experience that helps all kinds of resources get used more efficiently.

BusinessContact us

The SHAREON app is in pilot development. Real rentals, booking confirmation, payment and settlement are not operating yet; this page introduces the business direction and where it stands.

01PERSONAL ITEM RENTAL

What you need, for as long as you need it.

A sharing model where people lend things they own or borrow the things they need.

  • Household goods
  • Tools and repair gear
  • Cameras and lenses
  • Camping gear
  • Sports equipment
  • Travel gear
  • Hobby gear
  • Event supplies

StatusIn development in the app

02CAMERA & CREATIVE EQUIPMENT

Professional gear, used more flexibly.

A model for using shooting and production equipment that is costly to buy, only for as long as it is needed.

  • Cameras
  • Lenses
  • Lighting
  • Tripods
  • Video equipment
  • Audio equipment
  • Shooting accessories
  • Production gear

StatusIn development in the app

03OUTDOOR & LEISURE RENTAL

You don’t have to own every piece of gear for a new experience.

A model for borrowing outdoor and leisure gear you use only now and then, and sharing the gear you own.

  • Camping
  • Hiking
  • Sports
  • Leisure
  • Travel
  • Events
  • Hobbies
  • Outdoor activities

StatusIn development in the app

04BUSINESS EQUIPMENT RENTAL

Making better use of company equipment.

A model where rental companies and businesses offer equipment, and companies and individuals use it for the period they need.

  • Office equipment
  • Event equipment
  • Exhibition equipment
  • Shooting equipment
  • Training equipment
  • Commercial fixtures
  • Specialist equipment
  • Short-project equipment

StatusIn development in the app

Participation for registered businesses is being built in the app.

05SPACE SHARING

Turning empty space into new possibilities.

A model that connects spaces in their idle hours with the people and businesses who need them.

  • Photo studios
  • Meeting rooms
  • Seminar rooms
  • Practice rooms
  • Workshops
  • Shared offices
  • Event spaces
  • Pop-up and exhibition spaces

StatusIn development in the app

Listing and browsing spaces is being built in the app; space booking is in preparation. Areas that need separate licences, such as lodging or parking, will not be offered until lawful operating conditions are confirmed.

06LONG-TERM RENTAL

From a single use to long-term rental.

A model that widens the rental period from a day to monthly and recurring use.

  • Daily rental
  • Weekly rental
  • Monthly rental
  • Recurring use
  • Long-term business rental
  • Long-term space use
  • Extensions
  • Repeat bookings

StatusIn preparation

07LOCAL SHARING

Sharing that starts close to home.

A model for finding nearby resources first, and borrowing and returning them within the neighbourhood.

  • Local item rental
  • Nearby resource search
  • Local rental businesses
  • Neighbourhood resources
  • Rental information by area
  • Area-based reviews

StatusIn development in the app

The default is to show an area rather than an exact location, and location data is removed from photos when they are added. In-person safety guidance is being prepared too.

08BUSINESS-TO-BUSINESS SHARING

Turning a company’s idle assets into new resources.

A model for companies to share their equipment, spaces and assets with other companies.

  • Equipment sharing between companies
  • Idle office assets
  • Event and exhibition assets
  • Space use between companies
  • Short-term asset use
  • Long-term rental
  • Business supplier management
  • Business renter management

StatusLong-term direction

09RENTAL SCHEDULING

From available dates to the return date.

Managing availability and booking status in one place so that rentals don’t overlap.

  • Available periods
  • Rental schedules
  • Booking status
  • Changing the rental period
  • Expected return date
  • Preventing double booking
  • Recurring bookings
  • Schedule reminders

StatusIn development in the app

Schedule screens and approval-based booking requests are being built in the app; booking confirmation is not operating yet.

10TRUST & SAFETY

For a rental experience you can feel safe about.

Preparing clear terms and responsibilities before and after a rental, and a way to respond when something goes wrong.

  • User verification
  • Business verification
  • Terms of use
  • Deposit policy
  • Damage and loss handling
  • Reviews
  • Reporting and blocking
  • Dispute handling

StatusIn preparation

Deposit holding, payment and compensation will not be offered until operations and legal review are complete, and no protection or compensation is promised.

11DELIVERY & RETURNS

From the moment you borrow to the moment you return.

A direction for connecting how rented items travel — from pickup in person to delivery and collection.

  • Pickup in person
  • Parcel delivery
  • Home collection
  • Equipment recovery
  • Return schedules
  • Delivery tracking links
  • Return condition checks
  • Logistics partner links

StatusLong-term direction

There are no logistics partner agreements yet.

12RENTAL BUSINESS TOOLS

Operating tools for rental businesses.

Aiming for tools that let rental businesses manage assets, bookings, customers and returns in one place.

  • Asset and inventory management
  • Booking management
  • Rental schedules
  • Customer management
  • Return management
  • Settlement reports
  • Operations automation
  • Business analytics

StatusLong-term direction

A seller centre inside the app is being built; no separate SaaS product has been released.

13APP & ACCESS

Sharing and renting, in one app.

SHAREON is being prepared as a mobile app for iOS and Android. Real rentals and booking will be offered in the app only; the website does not offer listing, search, booking or payment.

  • iOS
  • Android

StatusIn development in the app

The app is not in the app stores yet, so there is no download link. Launch news will be shared through official channels.
