WHO WE ARE

A company that builds and runs its own platforms.

New + On

TURN POSSIBILITIES ON

NEWON is a digital platform company that connects many areas of everyday life. We are not a studio that builds to order for clients: our business is the platforms and digital products we plan and build ourselves.

Parenting, pet life, travel, sharing, life stages and senior life are different areas, but they share the same needs — keeping records, finding information you can trust and reaching the right services. NEWON answers those shared needs with six platforms, each designed for its own area.

The name NEWON starts from New + On: switching new everyday experiences on and making them work. We keep improving each platform's experience and aim to build a sustainable digital ecosystem that people and companies can take part in.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

A platform built for each area of life.

Rather than putting everything into one app, we design the records, information and services each area needs for the people in that area. That is why NEWON runs six separate platforms.

01

FOCUS

Parenting and senior care, travel and renting things have different users and different needs. Each platform focuses on one area and goes deep into its problems.

02

OWN & OPERATE

We plan, build and run the platforms ourselves, and keep improving them after launch based on how people use them and what they tell us.

03

OPEN ECOSYSTEM

People use the services; companies and institutions take part through listings, partnerships and collaboration. Each platform becomes a point where people and businesses meet.

OUR SIX PLATFORMS

NEWON · 6

Six core platforms.

PLATFORMS BABYLOG · PETLOG · MY WORLD · SHAREON · LIVON · ONGIL
01 ↗

BABYLOG — Parenting & Family

From preparing for pregnancy to birth, parenting, growth records and family life, BABYLOG brings a child's and a family's scattered records and information together. It is built for parents-to-be, families raising children and the carers who share the work. Today the iOS and Android app offers parenting records, growth and health records, schedules and family sharing. Families keep the same records together, and companies and institutions in parenting, education and care are invited to take part through partnerships.

  • Parenting records
  • Growth & health
  • Family sharing
  • Education & care
02 ↗

PETLOG — Pet Life

PETLOG reduces the problem of a pet's daily life, health history and vet dates being split across different owners. It is built for pet owners and their families. Today the PETLOG app offers profiles, daily and health records, vaccination and vet schedules, and shared family care. Pet life information and a community are areas in preparation, and pet brands and care providers taking part is a future partnership direction.

  • Daily & health records
  • Vaccination & vet dates
  • Shared family care
  • Pet life services
03 ↗

MY WORLD — Travel & Experience

MY WORLD starts from the problem that discovery, planning, booking, records and sharing are spread across many services. It is built for travellers. Today the MY WORLD app is a travel-record service: mark the countries and cities you have visited on the world map on its home screen, and look back on trips through routes, photos, stats and an AI travel report. Booking, commerce and a shared community are being prepared in stages, with travel agencies, stays, tour operators, brands, regions and institutions meeting travellers.

  • World-map travel records
  • Trip planning
  • Booking & experiences
  • Traveller community
04 ↗

SHAREON — Sharing & Rental

SHAREON deals with things that are used rarely but cost too much to buy, and equipment and spaces that sit idle. It aims to be an open sharing and rental marketplace connecting people who want to borrow for just the time they need with individuals and businesses that own items, equipment and spaces. The SHAREON app has not launched yet: no real rentals, confirmed bookings, payments, deposits or settlements run today. The first launch is being prepared around rentals and spaces with approval-based booking.

  • Things
  • Equipment
  • Spaces
  • Mobility
05 ↗

LIVON — Life Stage

LIVON deals with information scattered across many places at every stage of life — education, careers, jobs, military service, money, housing, marriage, birth, family, health, welfare and retirement. It is an integrated life platform that starts from your current life stage, from the teens to 70 and over. The LIVON app is being prepared for launch. Public programmes are shown with their official source and reference date, and LIVON aims to work with companies and public bodies in education, recruitment and everyday information.

  • Life-stage information
  • Public programmes
  • Life records
  • Education, careers & welfare
06 ↗

ONGIL — Senior Life

ONGIL connects everyday information, family connection and care and welfare information so that seniors can carry on their lives at their own pace and in their own way. It is built for seniors and their families. The iOS and Android app is being prepared for its first release in Korea. Collaboration with care providers, welfare bodies, companies and local governments is under review, and no institution has a confirmed official partnership yet.

  • Daily life
  • Health records
  • Family connection
  • Care & welfare guidance

A business in three areas.

NEWON's business has three areas: running its own platforms, partnerships with companies and institutions built on those platforms, and its own digital products. All three rest on services and products NEWON owns and runs itself — we do not build to order for clients.

HOW WE BUILD

Start small. Improve while we run it.

  1. 01

    LISTEN

    Find the real problems and needs of people in each area.

  2. 02

    DESIGN

    Shape records, information and services into a flow that fits the area.

  3. 03

    BUILD

    Build the features that matter most ourselves, and ship them.

  4. 04

    OPERATE

    Run the service and learn from how it is used and what people say.

  5. 05

    IMPROVE

    Widen what is proven, and say clearly what is still in preparation.

PRINCIPLES

How we hold the work.

01

USEFUL FIRST

Build what people actually use first. Recording, finding and connecting should be easy.

02

HONEST STATUS

Separate what is available from what is in preparation. We never call an unreleased feature available.

03

TRUST & PRIVACY

Handle information about children, pets, health and families with care, and put official sources first for public information.

04

EASY FOR EVERYONE

Design so first-time users, and people who need large text, can use it without difficulty.

05

BUILT TO LAST

We don't build once and walk away — we keep improving, version after version.

OUR VISION

A platform company for every stage of life.

NEWON aims to be a company that connects the services people need across many stages of life — from waiting for a child to daily life with a pet, travel, borrowing what you need, big life decisions and growing older. Each platform earns trust in its own area, and on that base we want to build a sustainable digital ecosystem that people and companies take part in together.

LONG-TERM DIRECTION

Where we are heading.

01 LIFE

CONNECTED EVERYDAY

Six platforms that grow deeper in their own areas and lead naturally to the services people need as their life stage changes. Today data is not linked or automatically shared between the platforms.

02 PARTNERS

OPEN ECOSYSTEM

Step by step, a structure in which individual suppliers and small businesses, brands and companies, institutions and local governments take part on each platform's terms.

03 9 LANGUAGES

GLOBAL READY

The NEWON website is available in 9 language versions, with some pages shown in English. That does not mean overseas users or country-by-country operations; overseas expansion is reviewed platform by platform as each service is validated and ready.

04 TRUST

LASTING TRUST

Services people can trust and use for a long time come before fast growth. NEWON describes itself by verified facts and what is actually available.
