NEWON LABS · AI · PRODUCT INTELLIGENCE

Not an AI that reads reviews.
An AI that supports product decisions.

Reviews are the most direct user voice a product team gets.

But as volume grows,
the cost of reading, sorting, and prioritizing grows with it.
Stars and sentences pile up —
the signals for decisions stay hard to see.

Review AI does not stop at summaries.
It structures repeated problems, requests, sentiment, and patterns
into signals teams can use in product decisions.

THE QUESTION

Can user reviews become decision signals —
not just star ratings and sentences?

Reviews are the most direct user voice, but as volume grows, reading and prioritizing get expensive. Summary alone is not a decision.

WHY THIS EXPERIMENT

How we frame the problem.

01

PROBLEM

As review volume grows, it is hard to read everything
and spot repeated issues.
Teams feel the pain — but struggle to use it as evidence.

02

HYPOTHESIS

If AI goes beyond summarizing and structures repeated problems, requests, sentiment, and patterns, teams can use reviews more directly in product decisions.

03
