What is LIVON

Services matched
to life's moments.

LIVON is a life-event platform that connects life's big changes with everyday life. When something changes — moving, a first job, marriage, pregnancy and birth, military service, retirement — it brings official information, plans and checklists into one flow, and later connects checked living services too. The aim is simple: not to miss the moments that matter.

The problemWhat people do todayHow LIVON does it
Information for each life event is scatteredSearch portals, blogs and agency sites one by one.Groups every related field around your life stage and event.
Knowing does not lead to doingWrite things down in notes and calendars.Turns it into to-dos, dates and checklists, with applications linked to official channels.
Hard to tell what applies to youCompare age and region conditions by hand.Shows audience, region and date checked, and puts your own choices first.
Hard to choose a service you can trustSearch results mixing ads and reviews.Later, links only checked partner services, with ads always labelled. (Planned)

Our Vision

Not just collecting information —
connecting it to the life you live now.

LIVON is an All-in-One Life Platform. Its strength is connection, not volume. It links information across fields around your life stage and what you are going through.

  1. 01

    Stage Start from where you are, from the teens to 70+.

  2. 02

    Event One life event — enlisting, a first job, marriage, retirement — shows every field it touches.

  3. 03

    Source Public schemes always show the official source, the date checked and the region covered.

  4. 04

    Free LIVON on the web is free and works without signing up or logging in. Personal records stay on your device.

  5. 05

    Classification The 40 categories are a content classification. Each one shows what is actually available.

Life Journey

From growing up to later life,
one continuous journey.

Five chapters of life and the areas people open most in each, with key topics and the linked areas (A–E). Ages are typical examples — everyone's pace differs.

  3. 03 20s–30s

    Standing alone

    A first home, moving, driving, managing money — pre-contract checks, registration, budgets and credit basics.

  4. 04 30s–50s

Who is LIVON for?

Individuals and families facing a life event come first; companies and institutions follow as customers who help them.

  1. 01

    Individuals facing a life event

    People about to start work, move out, marry, have a baby, enlist or retire. The first focus is planned to be people in their 20s and 30s in the capital region preparing for a birth or a move.

  2. 02

    Families

    Couples planning together, families expecting a baby, adult children caring for parents — households that share dates and paperwork.

  3. 03

    HR and benefits teams

    Companies that want to explain company and public schemes when employees join, move, have a baby, care for family or retire (concept).

  4. 04

    Institutions and service partners

    Local governments and public bodies informing residents, and moving, cleaning or education providers who want to be found at the right moment (concept).

Why LIVON

How is it different?

These are differentiation hypotheses based on how public services work, not yet tested against real usage data.

  1. 01

    Public portals such as Gov24 Strong at finding services and benefits. LIVON places those schemes inside the plans and checklists of a life event, and sends applications back to the official channel.

  2. 02

    Search portals and blogs Plenty of information, but source, freshness and ads are hard to tell apart. LIVON adds the source and date checked, and always labels ads.

  3. 03

    Calendar and notes apps Good for recording, silent on what to do when. LIVON shows what to check for each event first.

How the NEWON platforms divide the work

Each app focuses on its own job and LIVON points to them. There is no data link between the apps today.

Check birth support and parental leave in LIVON, then keep feeding, sleep and growth records in BABYLOG.

LIVON explains retirement planning, pensions and senior welfare; ONGIL handles day-to-day care and support for older adults and their families.

When moving out means buying or selling things, LIVON does not run personal used-goods trading itself — it points to SHAREON.
