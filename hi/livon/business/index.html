LIVON

A business that connects people's lives
with information and services.

LIVON earns through information, user experience and enterprise solutions rather than selling products. It builds standard products and its own platform, not custom client work.

The LIVON app is in preparation. You can use LIVON on the web for free today.

Principle

Forty categories, not forty apps.

  • One platform built on a shared data structure, life stages, event-led connections and managed official sources — the only way to keep updates manageable and information accurate.

  • Standard products and our own platform — not custom client work.

  • No paid product is on sale today. Prices and rates for subscription, AI, enterprise, advertising and referral revenue are all proposals and not applied. No fees are charged on public benefits or statutory payments, and paid ads are always kept apart from general information.

Business Model

Who uses it, and who pays?

LIVON's core life information stays free. Revenue is planned to come from individuals and families who want advanced features, companies running employee benefits, checked living-service partners and institutions. No paid product is on sale today.

B2C · Individuals
People facing a life event — free basics, advanced management and AI subscription (proposal)
B2C · Families
Couples and households caring for parents — shared family plan (proposal)
C2C · Person to person
Not run in LIVON. Sharing, rental and personal used-goods trading go to SHAREON.
B2B · Companies
Companies running employee benefits — employee life benefits, enterprise SaaS (proposal)
B2B2C · Service partners
Moving, cleaning and education providers — referral and transaction fees (proposal, after provider checks and payments)
B2G · Public bodies
Resident information — SaaS and information-service contracts for institutions (proposal)

Everything is free today. The tiers below are proposals in the business plan and are not on sale. Prices will be set only after AI, payment, server and support costs and real demand are known, and are not shown before then.

  1. Now · LIVON web

    Free

  2. Proposal

  3. Proposal

  4. Proposal

    Family

For businesses and sellers, tiered plans — from free listing through small, growth and advanced tiers to custom institutional contracts — are being considered as proposals.

Transactions & Fees

Principles for transaction, booking and referral revenue

Fees for living services, classes, everyday goods and education materials are not set, because payment, tax, refund and dispute costs are not yet verified. The principles by field are:

Kept free
Welfare and benefits, official military service information, basic certificate and exam information, official pregnancy and birth information
Not introduced
Fees proportional to medical bills
On hold
Performance fees for legal referrals
Separate review
Financial and insurance products by regulation and contract; property brokerage after legal review
Decided after cost checks
Referral fees for living services, classes, goods and education materials; safe payment for person-to-person trading
No double fees
Two NEWON platforms never both charge a fee on the same transaction.

Market & Opportunity

The market problem and the opportunity

  • Scattered information

    What each life event needs is split across public portals, agency sites and blogs.

  • A gap between knowing and doing

    Even when people know a scheme, few tools turn it into dates, applications and the services they need.

  • Changes everyone goes through

    Work, moving out, marriage, birth and retirement happen to most adults. We do not present a money market size without evidence.

Differentiation hypotheses

These are differentiation hypotheses based on how public services work, not yet tested against real usage data.

Public portals (Gov24 and others)

Scheme search → LIVON places schemes inside a life event's plans and checklists and links applications to official channels

Search portals and blogs

Source, freshness and ads are hard to tell → source, date checked and ad labels

Calendar and notes apps

Recording → shows what to check for each event first

Other NEWON apps

A hub that hands over at the right moment instead of copying specialist features

Go-to-market

Entering the market (plan)

  1. 01

    A free information habit

    Search traffic for life events and shared checklists build a free-use habit.

  2. 02

    Two events first

    People in their 20s and 30s in the capital region preparing for a birth or a move are the first audience, with deep content for them.

  3. 03

    Testing subscription value

    After the app launches, price and conversion of individual and family plans are tested.

  4. 04

    Employee-benefit pilots

    Small pilots are proposed to HR and benefits teams.

Operating Principles

We keep general information apart from licensed services.

  • No insurance sales or brokerage.

  • No sale or recommendation of financial products.

  • No investment advice or personal recommendations.

  • No diagnosis, treatment or other medical practice.

  • No legal or tax representation.

  • No fees on public benefits.

  • Performance-based fees in regulated fields such as medical and legal are on hold pending legal review.

  • The market is in preparation: no products, booking or payment are shown until real sellers exist.

  • Personal used-goods trading is not run in LIVON; it points to SHAREON.

  • Core life information stays free and usable without logging in.

  • Two NEWON platforms never both charge a fee on the same transaction.

  • As a principle, nothing that identifies a person is handed to partners.

  • Before any subscription, automatic payment and cancellation terms are explained clearly first.
