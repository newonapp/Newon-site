PARTNERSHIPS · 15 PARTNER TYPES

So that more people can experience the world,
we are looking for partners.

We want to build better travel experiences with partners across the travel industry. Check how each partner type can work with us, and send us a proposal.

Partnership inquiryGo to contact

15 PARTNER TYPES

Fifteen partner types

  1. 01

    Travel agencies

    Who
    Domestic and overseas agencies, package operators
    What we do together
    Presenting packages and themed trips, connected to booking
    Value
    Reach travelers through their records and taste
    Revenue model
    Commission per contract
    Conditions
    Required registration and licences; product information and cancellation/refund policies

    Partnership inquiry →

  2. 02

    Flight & transport suppliers

    Who
    Airlines, rail, bus and ferry operators, booking platforms
    What we do together
    Flight and transport search connected to booking
    Value
    Visibility inside travelers’ itineraries
    Revenue model
    Commission per contract
    Conditions
    Agree booking and ticketing responsibility and data integration

    Partnership inquiry →

  3. 03

    Hotels & accommodation

    Who
    Hotels, resorts, guesthouses, accommodation platforms
    What we do together
    Presenting stays, connected to booking
    Value
    Stays discovered by destination and itinerary
    Revenue model
    Commission per contract
    Conditions
    Room and rate information; cancellation/refund policies

    Partnership inquiry →

  4. 04

    Tour & activity operators

    Who
    Local tours, activities, ticket operators
    What we do together
    Presenting experiences, connected to booking
    Value
    Reach travelers looking for things to do
    Revenue model
    Sales commission (rate to be agreed)
    Conditions
    Safety and insurance standards; operating information

    Partnership inquiry →

  5. 05

    Travel-gear brands

    Who
    Luggage, apparel, electronics and other travel-gear brands
    What we do together
    Curation in a travel context; group-buying campaigns

    Revenue model
    Sales commission (rate to be agreed)
    Conditions
    Product information and authenticity; order, delivery and refund policies

    Partnership inquiry →

    Who

    What we do together
    Secondhand trading and sharing
    Value
    Reuse of travel gear
    Revenue model

    Conditions
    Follow safe-trading rules and operating policies

    Partnership inquiry →

    What we do together
    Travel content, recommended routes, digital travel products
    Value

    Revenue model

    Conditions
    Copyright clearance; ad and sponsorship disclosure

    Partnership inquiry →

    Local guides and interpreters
    What we do together

    Business information and operating standards

PARTNERSHIP PROCESS

From proposal to operation,
in five steps.

  1. 01

    Proposal

    Proposal

    Send your partnership type and idea through our contact channels.

  2. 02

    Review

    Review

    We review it against service direction, applicable law and traveler value.

  3. 03

    Discussion

    Discussion

    We discuss scope, data integration and operations.

  4. 04

    Contract

    Contract

    We set out terms and responsibilities and sign a contract.

  5. 05

    Operation

    Operation

    After signing, we launch and operate it together.

We are looking for partners to write
the next chapter of travel with us.
