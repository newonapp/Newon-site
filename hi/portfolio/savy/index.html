Overview

SAVY is

a spending tracker that helps you record and review your money flow quickly—without a heavy household ledger.

Problem

Subscriptions and recurring spend scattered across apps and statements

Solution

One place to organize spending, income, and subscriptions

Product

Bring money flows into one view so spending patterns are easier to see.

iOS/Android app, subscription and spend tracking, alerts, categories, i18n

मुख्य फीचर

व्यय लॉगिंग

Enter what you spent today in a few taps Lightweight inputs make it easy to stay consistent

आय लॉगिंग

Track salary, side income, allowance, and more See the full in/out picture more accurately

सदस्यता प्रबंधन

व्यय विश्लेषण
