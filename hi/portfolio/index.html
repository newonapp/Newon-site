SELECTED CASE STUDIES

Decisions behind the products.

Not what the product is — how problems were approached and what was learned.

CASE 01 TESTING

Review AI

CHALLENGE
How quickly can we surface improvement signals from app and service reviews?
DECISION
Structured summaries and theme clustering can prioritize issues faster than manual sorting.
BUILD
Building a prototype: review intake → theme classification → summary board.
LEARN
Even keyword/sentiment heuristics surface repeated complaints and requests quickly. Accuracy needs a real model connection.
View case study →
CASE 02 TESTING

Newon QR

CHALLENGE
What does a small team actually need to create and track branded QR codes?
DECISION
Create, destination edits, and basic scan tracking is enough to test early demand.
BUILD
Assembling a QR generator with a dashboard prototype.
LEARN
Creation UX should stay minimal. Tracking value is hard to prove without a backend.
View case study →
CASE 03 RESEARCH

Character Lab

CHALLENGE
Can we publish only validated IP — never fake characters?
DECISION
Validating a small character system first keeps brand consistency.
BUILD
Drafting character guidelines, tone, and usage rules. No downloadable assets yet.
LEARN
Validate one internal character concept, then decide on a public release.
View case study →

BUILD PRINCIPLES

How I build products.

  1. 01
    SHIP REAL PRODUCTS
