You see the problems.
Not where to start.

We review core flows and UI — signup, onboarding, purchase, settings, and more. Usability issues, information structure, and conversion or drop-off points are structured into an audit report, issue list, and prioritized action items your team can execute.

OVERVIEW

Improvements are needed.
Priority is not clear.

Conversion, churn, support tickets, review complaints — signals that UX problems exist are often there. What is harder is seeing which flow, UI, or information structure causes the friction.

Teams share opinions that something is awkward, but without classifying issues by core flow and priority, improvement discussions repeat or stall.

UX AUDIT starts by defining scope and core flows. Then we review screens, flows, and information structure, classify issues, and prioritize action items by impact and effort.

PROCESS

From scope to delivery.

  1. 01

    DISCOVERY

    Confirm audit purpose and business context. Understand conversion, churn, and support concerns.

  2. 02

    SCOPE

    Define core flows, screen range, and access method. Agree staging, accounts, and data scope.

  3. 03

    AUDIT

    Review flows, UI, and information structure. Record issues by screen and step.

  4. 04

    ANALYSIS

    Classify, dedupe, and prioritize issues. Connect impact and implementation effort.

  5. 05

    DRAFT

    Draft audit report and action items. Build the skeleton of key findings.

  6. 06

    REVIEW

    Review key issues and priority together. Adjust gaps, overload, and feasibility.

  7. 07

    DELIVER

    Deliver final documents and action items. Connect to design, dev, and experiment next steps.
