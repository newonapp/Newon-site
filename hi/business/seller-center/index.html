NEWON · SELLER CENTER

Guidance and partnerships
for sellers.

A guide for sellers and businesses that want to introduce products and services in the Newon app ecosystem.

Overview

Newon builds everyday apps for parenting, pets, travel, daily life and senior care. This page explains how sellers with fitting products and services can work with us.

  • 01

    Partnership first

    It starts with a partnership inquiry, not a seller sign-up. Scope and approach are agreed after the inquiry.

  • 02

    Introduced in the apps

    How things are introduced and used is agreed per app. The website only provides guidance.

  • 03

    Agreed individually

    Fees, prices and terms are not fixed; they are discussed individually after an inquiry.

Who it is for

  • 01

    Individual and small sellers

    Sellers who want to introduce products or services to app users.

  • 02

    Brands and businesses

    Brands and businesses that want to collaborate in parenting, pets, daily life and seniors.

  • 03

    Service providers

    Businesses that run helpful services such as care and living services.

Related apps

Each app page has a partnership section describing how we work in that field.

01→ BabyLog Parenting products and services 02→ PetLog Pet products and services 03→ ShareOn Sharing items, spaces and services (coming soon) 04→ LivOn Life-stage information and services 05→ Ongil Senior living and care services

This website is for information only

Service features such as sign-in, using services, booking, trading, payment and seller management are not provided on the website. It only offers app guidance, partnership info and inquiries.

The website does not currently provide seller accounts, product listing, trading, payment or settlement.

CONTACT

Partnership and adoption inquiries are welcome

Send partnership, adoption and collaboration inquiries from businesses, sellers and institutions through the contact page. Terms are agreed individually after an inquiry.
