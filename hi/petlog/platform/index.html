PLATFORM OVERVIEW

26 areas:
what is here now, and what comes next.

PETLOG grows inside one app into 26 areas in five groups. The status below is what the current app and published material confirm; anything unconfirmed is shown as planned or under review.

PETLOG is expanding step by step from the records, health and family-care features in its app today into care services, commerce, safety, public-good and business areas. Some services and businesses on this page are future development or partnership plans, not current operations.

In the app today

The basics are available in the PETLOG app on the App Store and Google Play.

  • 02 Health and medical records
  • 03 Daily life log
  • 04 Calendar and reminders
  • 05 Notes, photos and memories

Being prepared together with operating policies and safety rules.

  • 16 Record-summary and life assistant AI

Planned

APP STRUCTURE · PROPOSAL

26 areas,
gathered into five tabs.

The bottom-tab structure proposed in the business plan. Rather than laying out 26 areas on one screen, the app is meant to show the menus each owner needs, based on the pet's information and the owner's interests. It is a proposal and may differ from today's app screens.

  1. 01

    Home

    Today's condition, schedule and suggested services

  2. 02

    PETLOG

    Health, meals, walks, sleep, diary and calendar

  3. 03

    Discover

    Vets, grooming, care, hotels, training, market, travel, shelters, funerals

  4. 04

    Community

    Information, reviews, local groups, lost pets, protection work

  5. 05

    My

    Pets, family account, bookings, orders, subscription, devices

Records and care core

Gathering one pet's day and health history in one place. The basics of all five areas are in the PETLOG app today; extras such as advanced statistics and albums are planned.

Pet profile and family account

Create a profile per pet with name, breed, birth date, weight and photos, and invite family as co-owners. Every record builds on this profile. Sharing only what a carer needs, and lawful transfer when the owner changes, are planned.

PETLOG AI · IN DEVELOPMENT

Ten jobs for
the AI assistant.

The AI functions outlined in the business plan. The record-summary AI is in development; the rest are reviewed step by step. None of them diagnoses, prescribes or makes emergency decisions, and where an emergency is possible they point to a vet or an emergency clinic.

FunctionWhat it does
Health AISummarises health records so they are easy to read before a vet visit.
Life AIOrganises daily patterns such as meals, walks and sleep.
Calendar AISuggests vaccination, medication and vet dates and to-dos.
Training AIGives general training information and never prescribes risky methods.
Nutrition AIGives general nutrition information.
Travel AIPrepares packing lists and conditions for pet-friendly trips.
Market AISuggests everyday supplies, with ads clearly marked.
Care AIBuilds a checklist to hand over when someone else cares for your pet.
Senior Pet AIOffers tips for daily care of senior pets.
Memorial AIHelps put together memory albums and records.

LOCALISATION

What we check first in each country.

Language and units

not only the interface language but weight units, date formats and breed names follow local conventions.

Pet-keeping culture

indoor and outdoor life, walking habits and the species people keep differ by country and shape the features offered.

Law and privacy

each country's animal-protection, veterinary and data-protection rules are reviewed before launch, and owners' records are handled only within their consent.

Payments and currency

subscriptions, booking and trading open only once local payment methods, currency, tax and consumer-protection standards are in place.

Official information and local partners

vaccination, registration and travel rules follow local official sources, and service links run only with local partners under a written agreement.

BY USER TYPE

How each type
of user takes part.

The same pet record should show different things depending on who is looking. Owners and family use the app today; the other types are planned.

Main owner

Creates the pet profile and manages all records, including family invitations and sharing scope.

Co-owners (family)

Invited family members record and view the same pet's records; change history shows who recorded what, and when.

Carers (planned)

Sitters and hotels are planned to see only what the owner allows — food amounts, cautions, emergency contacts.

Businesses and sellers (planned)

In principle, businesses receive only what a booking needs, and sellers receive no pet records beyond what an order needs.

Shelters and public bodies (planned)

Take part by publishing official notices and guidance, without access to owners' records.

PRIVACY, SECURITY & TRUST

Privacy, security and trust principles

  • Pet records and owners' personal data belong to the owner and are never given to businesses, sellers or partners without consent.
  • Health, family and location data are not shared automatically with other NEWON apps either; links are considered only with explicit consent and lawful authority.
  • Device data such as live location or cameras is handled only with separate consent, permissions and device security.
  • Lost-pet reports never publish an owner's exact address or contact details.
  • PETLOG and its AI features never diagnose, prescribe or make emergency decisions. Vets decide.
  • Ads, sponsorship and partnerships are clearly separated from general information, reviews and verification status.
  • Live animals are never sold or traded secondhand, and regulated items such as animal drugs are not sold pending legal review.
  • Booking and payment do not run before legal, payment-provider (PG) contract and security review, and are never offered on the website.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does PETLOG offer today?

In the app: pet profiles and family sharing, daily life logs such as meals and walks, health records such as weight, vaccinations, medication and visits, schedule reminders and photo records. The 'In the app today' group on this page lists current features.

When can I book grooming or hotels, or shop?

These are planned. Booking and payment come only to the app after legal review, a payment-provider (PG) contract and security checks; no date is fixed yet.

Does PETLOG diagnose my pet?

No. PETLOG and its AI features organise records and give general information; they never diagnose, prescribe or make emergency decisions. If something seems wrong, contact a vet; in an emergency, contact the nearest emergency clinic right away.

Are my pet's records shared with businesses or sellers?

Not without the owner's consent. Even once booking exists, the principle is that only the information the owner chooses reaches that business.

Does PETLOG cover animals other than dogs and cats?

PETLOG is designed around dogs and cats. Other species are planned once their health, husbandry and legal requirements are checked.

Do shelter adoption or lost-pet search cost anything?

Essential shelter, lost-pet, adoption and foster features are planned to be free, with no fee on adoptions. These areas are not yet in the app; they are planned.

Can I record or book on the website?

No. This website introduces PETLOG and takes inquiries; records and every service are used in the app.

Can I keep records without subscribing?

Yes. Basic profile, health and life records are meant to stay free. Price and benefits of the PETLOG Premium subscription listed on the stores are as each store shows them.

When will community and AI be available?

Both are in development. Community is being prepared with operating policies and report and block tools, and AI with safety rules that rule out diagnosis; no date is fixed yet.
