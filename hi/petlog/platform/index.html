PETLOG SERVICES

What you can use today,
and what we build next.

PETLOG is expanding from the pet life services it offers today into health, care, commerce, business and public areas. Some of the services and businesses introduced here are part of future development or partnership plans.

  1. 01
    In the PETLOG app today

    Pet profile · Daily log · Health records · Vaccination and medication · Family sharing · The app on the App Store and Google Play

  2. 02
    Expansion direction

    AI assistant · Community · Training · Shopping · Secondhand · Group buying · Vets · Grooming · Hotels and daycare · Pet sitting · Travel · Insurance, adoption and memorial information

01 · PROFILE, DAILY LIFE & HEALTH

Records and health:
the core of PETLOG.

Pets' information, daily life and health history in one place — the core features you can use in the PETLOG app today.

01

Pet Profile

Pet profile

Name, breed, age, weight and photos in one profile. Every record builds on it.

  • For All owners
  • Basic info
  • Growth and weight
  • Photos
  • Breed and age
Open the app →
02

Daily Life Log

Daily life log

Record meals, treats, walks, sleep, toilet and special moments every day. Over time the log helps you look back on changes in condition.

  • For All owners
  • Meals and treats
  • Walks
  • Sleep and toilet
  • Diary
  • Special moments
Open the app →
03

Health Records

Health records

Keep visits, tests, surgery, allergies and weight changes to check when needed. Essential health records are never over-charged, and PETLOG does not diagnose.

  • For All owners
  • Visit history
  • Test results
  • Allergies
  • Weight trend
  • Surgery history
Open the app →
04

Vaccination & Medication

Vaccination and medication

Manage vaccination schedules, prescriptions and medication logs, with reminders so the next vaccination or vet visit is not missed.

  • For All owners
  • Vaccination schedule
  • Medication log
  • Vet appointments
  • Reminders
Open the app →

02 · FAMILY & AI

Understand records,
care as a family.

Family sharing is in the app today; an AI assistant that makes records easy to understand is being prepared on a no-diagnosis principle.

05

AI Pet Assistant

AI pet assistant

An assistant being prepared to summarise records and explain care information and schedules. It never diagnoses, prescribes or decides treatment; when something seems wrong, it points to a vet.

  • For Owners
  • Record summaries
  • Care information
  • Schedule guidance
  • Vet first
Ask about this →
06

Family Sharing

Family sharing

Manage walks, meals and medication with family. Member roles and change history show who recorded what, and sharing is based on consent.

  • For Families
  • Invite family
  • Member roles
  • Change history
  • Shared schedule
  • Consent
Open the app →

03 · COMMUNITY & LIFESTYLE

Share together,
learn together.

An area connecting owners' experience and education content, prepared step by step with policies for safe conversation.

07

Community

Community

A space being prepared where dog and cat owners share tips, reviews, walk spots, photos and short videos, with safety tools such as report and block.

  • For Owners
  • Q&A
  • Reviews
  • Local info
  • Short video
  • Report and block
Ask about this →
08

Training & Education

Training and education

Toilet training, walk manners, socialisation and behaviour information, growing towards links with experts whose qualifications and experience are checked.

  • For Owners and trainers
  • Basic training
  • Socialisation
  • Behaviour
  • Trainer links
  • Qualification checks
Ask about this →

04 · PET COMMERCE

Discover and share
the goods you need.

Pet-specialist commerce, kept separate from SHAREON's sharing and rental role. Seller checks and regulated-item limits come first, and pet records are never shared with sellers automatically.

09

Pet Shopping

Pet shopping

A commerce area for food, treats, toys, walk gear, carriers and hygiene goods. Direct sales and a seller marketplace are separate stages, and ordering and payment will come only to the app after review.

  • For Owners and brands
  • Food and treats
  • Walk and travel gear
  • Hygiene and home
  • Brand promotions
  • Seller marketplace
Ask about this →
10

Secondhand & Sharing

Secondhand and sharing

An area to trade or give away unused carriers, toys, clothes and houses. Unsafe items are restricted and live animals are never traded.

  • For Owners
  • Person to person
  • Giving away
  • Restricted items
  • Safety policy
Ask about this →
11

Group Buying

Group and repeat buying

Group buys, subscriptions and bundles for repeat items like food, litter and pads. Sellers are checked before selling, and regulated items such as animal drugs are not sold pending legal review.

  • For Owners and sellers
  • Group buys
  • Subscriptions
  • Bundles
  • Seller checks
  • Regulated items blocked
Ask about this →

05 · CARE SERVICES

Find and connect
care you can trust.

An expansion area connecting owners with qualified providers, from vet information to grooming, hotels, daycare and pet sitting. Booking and payment come only to the app after legal, payment-provider and security review.

12

Veterinary Network

Veterinary network

An area for clinic specialties, location, hours and booking routes. Booking integration needs a separate partnership, and clinic advertising and brokerage follow legal review.

  • For Owners and clinics
  • Find clinics
  • Specialties
  • Opening hours
  • Booking links
Ask about this →
13

Grooming & Care

Grooming and care

An area to compare groomers' services, pricing policy, accepted pets and reviews, and connect to booking. Booking and payment connect only after legal, payment-provider and security review.

  • For Owners and groomers
  • Find groomers
  • Service scope
  • Accepted pets
  • Reviews
  • Business checks
Ask about this →
14

Hotel & Daycare

Hotel and daycare

An area to find hotels, boarding and daycare, including cancellation and refund rules. Facility and business eligibility and safety standards are checked first.

  • For Owners and operators
  • Boarding
  • Daycare
  • Cancellation rules
  • Facility info
  • Safety standards
Ask about this →
15

Pet Sitting & Walking

Pet sitting and walking

An area connecting pet sitting, home visits and walks while you're away — with provider checks, liability, safety and insurance built in.

  • For Owners and carers
  • Home visits
  • Walking
  • Provider checks
  • Safety policy
  • Liability
Ask about this →

06 · TRAVEL, LIFE & APP

Travel together,
remember for long.

Travel, insurance, adoption and memorial information, and a PETLOG app everyone can use. Insurance and adoption focus on information and official channels.

16

Travel & Places

Travel and places

An area for pet-friendly stays, cafés, parks, transport and trip prep, with a MY WORLD link under review; PETLOG focuses on pet policies and owners' experience.

  • For Owners and lodgings
  • Pet-friendly stays
  • Cafés and parks
  • Transport
  • Trip prep
  • Pet policies
Ask about this →
17

Insurance, Adoption & Memorial

Insurance, adoption and memorial

An area for insurance basics, official shelters and responsible adoption, lost-pet prevention, memories and licensed funeral information. Insurance is information only; for cover and claims use each insurer's official channel.

  • For Owners and shelters
  • Insurance basics
  • Adoption and registration
  • Lost-pet prevention
  • Memorial space
  • Official channels
Ask about this →
18

App & Accessibility

App and accessibility

PETLOG is on the App Store and Google Play, aiming to be easy for everyone with large text and clear contrast. Records and personal data are never shared without the owner's consent.

  • For All owners
  • iOS and Android
  • Privacy
  • Accessibility
  • Records belong to owners
