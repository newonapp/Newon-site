BEFORE YOU WRITE

Please check before writing.

  • Nothing is transacted on the web

    This website has no sign-in, booking, payment or seller tools. Records live in the PETLOG app; booking and payment are not offered yet.

  • Health concerns go to a vet

    PETLOG does not diagnose. If your pet is unwell or it's an emergency, contact a nearby vet straight away.

  • Describe app issues in detail

    Your device, app version and the screen and steps where it happened help us check faster.

  • Leave out sensitive data

    Please don't include passwords, payment details or original medical files in your message.

  • Be specific about partnerships

    Your company or organisation, the area and how you'd like to work together make review easier.

  • Used only to reply

    Your name, email and message are used only to reply to you.
