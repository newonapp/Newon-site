Everyday rental

Borrow household equipment you would hesitate to buy, just for the days you need it.

  • Cleaning gear
  • Tools
  • Pressure washers
  • Ladders
  • Appliances
  • Moving tools

To checkBasic usage notes and a parts check are needed.

02Electronics

Electronics rental

Status: In preparation

Use costly devices — laptops, tablets, projectors, monitors, consoles — for a short time.

  • Laptops
  • Tablets
  • Projectors
  • Monitors
  • Consoles

To checkBecause of damage, loss and data-leak risks, device reset, condition checks and handover steps are needed.

03Camera & Production

Camera and production gear

Status: In development in the app

Shooting equipment for individual creators, small studios and companies.

  • Cameras
  • Lenses
  • Lighting
  • Tripods
  • Microphones
  • Video gear

To checkEquipment condition disclosure and agreed damage rules.

04Travel & Camping

Travel and camping gear

Status: In development in the app

Borrow gear for a trip or a camp. MY WORLD handles travel planning and booking; SHAREON focuses on gear rental.

  • Suitcases
  • Tents
  • Camping chairs
  • Tables
  • Sleeping bags
  • Travel accessories

To checkSeasonal demand, and cleaning and drying checks.

05Fashion & Events

Fashion and event items

Status: In preparation

Borrow outfits and props for an event or a shoot.

  • Event wear
  • Costumes
  • Bags
  • Accessories
  • Party props

To checkAuthenticity of branded items, hygiene and cleaning, damage responsibility and whether an item may be rented.

    • Skis

    • Industrial equipment

    • Copiers

    • Meeting gear

    • IT equipment

D · RENTAL SYSTEM

The rental system

SHAREON aims to be a platform where rentals complete safely, not a list of products. Payment, deposit, return and dispute features are described as they actually stand.

  1. 01

    13Listings & Discovery

    Listing and discovery

    Status: In development in the app

    People and businesses list items and spaces; renters search by area, category, dates and price.

    • Listing
    • Photos
    • Categories
    • Area search
    • Date and price filters

    To checkLocation data is removed from photos when they are added.

  2. 02

    14Booking & Rental

    Booking and rental

    Status: In development in the app

    Check availability and conditions, then send an approval-based booking request.

    • Available dates
    • Booking requests
    • Approval-based booking
    • Extensions
    • Schedules

    To checkBooking-request screens are being built; booking confirmation is not operating yet.

  3. 03

    15Payments & Deposits

    Payments and deposits

    Status: In preparation

    Preparing to handle rental payments and deposits through a qualified payment provider.

    • Rental payment
    • Deposits
    • Refund rules
    • Settlement

    To checkOperated only after the payment-provider agreement, party responsibilities, refund rules and damages are designed together. Deposits are kept apart from platform revenue.

  4. 04

    16Returns & Disputes

    Returns and disputes

    Status: In preparation

    Inspect the condition at return, and handle damage, loss and late returns by set procedures.

    • Condition inspection
    • Parts check
    • Damage and loss rules
    • Late returns
    • Disputes

    To checkRules will be fixed with the terms of use after legal review.

  5. 05

    17Trust & Reviews

    Trust and reviews

    Status: In development in the app

    Reviews after a rental, plus reporting and blocking, for a trustworthy place to rent.

    • User checks
    • Business checks
    • Reviews
    • Reporting and blocking

    To checkReporting is being built in the app; reviews start once real rentals do.
