PARTNERSHIP VISION

Proving it together,
one neighbourhood at a time.

Collaboration with SHAREON starts with small local pilots rather than large contracts. Rental shops and space operators normally join directly without a partnership contract; the models below are for cases where a separate discussion makes sense, such as transaction infrastructure and company or institution assets.

  1. 01

    Direct listing

    Rental shops and space operators sign up and list without a partnership contract or subscription, going through business verification and item safety rules only.

  2. 02

    Local pilot

    List part of your stock in one neighbourhood and test the booking, pick-up and return flow together. Until payment opens, it runs without real money.

  3. 03

    Transaction infrastructure

    Connecting payment providers, delivery, collection, insurance and guarantees that rentals need, under lawful contract structures.

  4. 04

    Company & institution asset sharing

    Testing internal sharing of equipment and rooms with corporate asset teams, universities and institutions. The business tools have not been released.

  • POC & PILOT PROPOSALS

    Pilot proposals
    worth considering.

    Proposals that start small and check results in numbers. Until payment opens they run without real money, and targets are set together.

  • Neighborhood pilot

    Neighbourhood rental shop pilot

    A local rental shop lists part of its camping, tool or baby gear stock, and we follow the flow from request to return over a set period. Measures: share of requests completed, disputes and repeat rentals.

  • Idle-hours pilot

    Empty-hours space pilot

    Meeting or rehearsal room operators list empty time slots to test local demand. Each space’s permitted use and safety rules are checked first.

  • Internal sharing pilot

    Internal equipment sharing pilot

    One department or campus shares laptops and filming kit through internal booking, then reviews usage and idle assets. No per-trade fee.

  • Delivery pilot

    Delivery & collection pilot

    Test the cost and responsibility per leg when delivery and collection are added to rentals that are hard to hand over in person, alongside small-rental economics.

  • Protection review

    Deposit & insurance structure review

    Design how deposits, insurance and the dispute process split the work when damage or loss happens, together with regulatory review. A structural review, not a product sale.

DATA & API

Data and API
possibilities.

No public API is offered today. The links below could be considered after a pilot and are all at the planning stage.

  1. 01

    Stock and booking calendar sync

    Aligning a rental shop’s stock and booking tools with SHAREON’s available quantities to prevent double bookings.

  2. 02

    Delivery and collection status

    Showing delivery and collection status in the trade screen, once a delivery partnership is agreed.

  3. 03

    Insurance and guarantee confirmation

    Confirming whether a rental carries cover; regulatory review of insurance comes first.

  4. 04

    Company systems and licensing

    Connecting internal asset tools to company systems, and licensing, are the last, long-term items.

  5. 05

    Data principles

    Exchange only the minimum needed, never share contact details or exact addresses, and never use data beyond the consented purpose.
