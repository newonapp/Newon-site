OVERVIEW

Um produto forte começa com um problema claro —
, não com quantos recursos ele tem.

A falha mais comum ao construir algo novo é incluir muitos recursos.

Mesmo uma ideia forte fica confusa sem clareza sobre o que construir primeiro, o que cortar e por onde os usuários devem começar.

O design de produto começa antes da interface de usuário. Definimos de qual problema resolvemos, priorizamos casos de uso e recursos, e então transformamos isso em telas e fluxos.

Newon Design de Produto segue Problema → Estrutura do Produto → Fluxo Principal → MVP → UI → Validação.

Esclareça isso antes das triagens.

MVP · PRIORITY · STRUCTURE

Você não precisa de um produto finalizado
desde o primeiro dia.

Product structure is not a screen list — it defines how features, actions, and information connect.

SCOPE DIFFERENCE

Product Design vs App UI/UX,
and design vs development, differ.

Product Design fits idea-stage work or when prioritization and MVP must be defined first. App UI/UX fits when product structure and features are already clearer. Scopes can combine by project.

Projects may run Product Design only or connect Design + Development. Development inclusion, stack, and build scope are confirmed separately at quoting.

01

PRODUCT DESIGN

Starts with what to build — problem, goal, prioritization, MVP scope, structure, core flow, UX, UI.

02

APP UI/UX

Goes deeper on how to use it — IA, flow, screens, UI, interaction, state, design system.

03

PRODUCT DESIGN (SCOPE)

Design problem, features, product structure, flows, and interface.

04

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Implement so the product runs — front-end / back-end / database / API and related build.

Studio Product Design starting price is design only. Live product build continues through Newon Business MVP / BUILD with a separate quote.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

Scope notice

Exact feature count, screen count, user flows, prototype level, research scope, deliverables, and revision rounds are confirmed before kickoff.

Product Design covers product direction and experience design. Live development, servers, APIs, databases, and store submission may be separate Development scope.

AI APIs, payments, maps, login, push notifications, and other external services can change in implementation method and cost based on technical conditions and vendor policy.

MVP, prototype, or Product Design does not guarantee user counts, revenue, investment, downloads, conversion, retention, or product-market fit.

Final App Store / Google Play approval is also not guaranteed.
