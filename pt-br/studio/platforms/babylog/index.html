01 · PURPOSE

Purpose of
BABYLOG products.

  1. 01

    Help first-time parents prepare for pregnancy and birth stage by stage, without missing anything.

  2. 02

    Let the family record a child's growth and days the same way and find them again later.

  3. 03

    Help childcare-related facilities put their daily records and notices on the same forms.

05 · USE CASES

How they
will be used.

01

Two months before the birth

The planner's checklist separates what you already have from what you need before buying anything.

02

A day with grandma

The newborn record sheet stays on the fridge, and whoever is caring writes feeding and sleep times in the same boxes.

03

End-of-month spending check

Enter the month's spending in the budget sheet to see totals by category and the gap to budget.

04

Running a nursery class

Staff record the day on the daily form and copy the messages box into the parent notice.

FAQ

BABYLOG products
FAQ.

01Can I use them without the BABYLOG app?

Yes. They are planned as print and editable files used separately from the app.

02Does it tell me vaccination dates?

No. It gives you space to note dates; it doesn't set or advise on them. Check with your clinic and official guidance.

03Can growth records show developmental status?

The products collect records. They don't assess development or health.

04Can several classes at a nursery use it?

Several staff in one facility fall under team or business licences. Check the licence terms at launch.

05When can I buy them?

All are candidates with no launch date yet. Confirmed launches appear on the Newon Store first.
