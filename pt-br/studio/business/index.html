01 · INDIVIDUAL VS BUSINESS

Individual products and
business products.

A business product is not a bigger personal product; it is designed separately around work that several people share.

Individual products and business products.
Individual productsBusiness products
Designed aroundOne person's life and recordsRecurring work shared by several staff
Main contentsPlanners, record sheets, personal budgetsOperating logs, schedules and registers, reports, work calculations
UsersThe buyerThe team or workplace set in the licence
ScopePersonal and family lifeInternal work and customer service
SupportDelivery, opening files, how to useAdoption guidance, how to use, version updates
Custom developmentNoneNone — every customer uses the same standard product

02 · THREE PRODUCT TYPES

Three types of
business product.

01

BUSINESS TEMPLATES

Business templates

Documents and sheets that put daily and weekly records and reports on the same form. The way of recording stays the same when staff change.

Examples

  • Daily operating log
  • Booking & rental schedule
  • Inventory & asset register
  • Monthly operating report
  • Customer notice forms
  • Handover document
02

CALCULATION & ANALYSIS

Calculation & analysis tools

Tools that calculate the numbers behind operating decisions the same way every time. Formulas are visible, so results can be checked.

Examples

  • Room & asset utilisation
  • Rental income & cost
  • Staffing & scheduling
  • Monthly spending analysis
  • Price change comparison
03

BUSINESS SOFTWARE

Business software

Products that keep records, schedules and registers in one place for several staff. In planning; feature scope and supported environments are fixed at launch.

Examples

  • Operating records
  • Booking & rental schedules
  • Inventory & asset management
  • Work status by person

04 · PACKAGES

Business product
packages.

An industry package bundles what an industry's recurring work needs. Contents are fixed per industry and not changed per customer.

01

Core operations bundle

The templates used every day: daily logs, schedules, registers. For workplaces putting their forms in order for the first time.

02

Operations & analysis bundle

The core bundle plus utilisation, income and cost tools and a monthly report form.

03

Bundle with software

Business software used with its related templates. Offered once the software has launched.

05 · LICENCES

Individual, team and
business licences.

A licence sets who may use a product. These are general concepts; actual user numbers and conditions are set on each product page and in the licence guidance.

07 · TERMS OF USE

Business templates
Within the licence you can copy, edit and reuse them. Completed documents may be sent to the people you work with, but blank original forms may not be distributed or sold.

Results are for reference. Check inputs and formulas yourself before using them for decisions. The tools may not be built into other products for sale.
Business software
Supported environments, user numbers, data storage and update scope are set on the product page at launch. Terms are not confirmed before launch.
All products
Price, payment and cancellation or refund conditions follow each product page and the purchase guidance.

FAQ

Business customer
FAQ.

01Can you adapt a product for our company?

No. Studio's business products are standard products every customer uses with the same contents. We don't offer per-customer changes, custom development or design work.

02Is there a business product we can adopt now?

Industry products are in planning. What businesses can use today is the NEWON Project Starter Kit and the free web tools. Confirmed launches are announced on the Newon Store first.

03How is pricing decided?

We don't announce prices for unreleased products. After launch, price and per-licence conditions are shown on the product page.

04Can we get a quotation?

Studio doesn't work by individual quotes. Products come with set licence conditions; tell us your users and scope and we'll tell you which licence fits.

05Can several branches use the same forms?

Use across several branches falls under a business licence. Tell us the number of branches and users through a licence inquiry.

06Does it integrate with our systems?

Integration with outside systems isn't offered. Templates use widely used file formats so they open in the tools you already have.

07Do you train our staff?

We don't offer on-site training. Each product comes with usage guidance, and questions are welcome through Contact.

08Can we use contract or report forms as they are, legally?

Forms are practical references. Your business needs to check them against your actual deals and rules, with professional review where needed.

09Can care or health organisations use them?

Yes, for organising records and schedules. The products don't judge anyone's condition or replace professional services, and your organisation's rules take precedence.
