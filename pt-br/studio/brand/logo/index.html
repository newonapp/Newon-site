OVERVIEW

A strong logo lets people
recognize the brand without explanation.

A logo is one of the most repeated visual assets where brand meets people.

It appears across websites, app icons, social profiles, products, ads, and documents — forming memory through repetition.

A strong logo is not only a distinctive shape. It should express brand character, stay memorable, work across sizes and contexts, and remain usable over time.

Newon Logo Design builds the core mark and a practical Logo System from brand strategy and Identity.

PROBLEMS

Logo Design helps in situations like these.

01

NO LOGO

There is no logo to represent the brand.

Business and product are ready, but no core mark identifies the brand visually.

02

GENERIC

The current logo is too ordinary.

It blends with other brands or fails to make distinctive memory.

03

DOESN'T SCALE

It fails when small.

Fine on large screens, but form breaks in app icons, mobile, and tiny digital use.

04

OUTDATED

Logo and brand no longer match.

Business, audience, or direction moved on — the mark still looks like the past.

05

INCONSISTENT

The logo changes every time it is used.

Color, proportion, placement, and versions are undefined.

06

EXPANSION

The brand appears in more places.

App, web, social, ads, documents, and product need a Logo System.
