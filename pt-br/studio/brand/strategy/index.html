OVERVIEW

Before shaping what people see,
set the brand baseline.

Brand strategy is not a short idea dump or a design task.

It defines what problem the brand solves, who needs it, how it differs from competitors, and how it should be remembered.

That strategy becomes the shared baseline for Naming, Identity, Logo, Website, App, Content — every place the brand meets people.
