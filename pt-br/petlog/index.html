WHAT IS PETLOG

From records to memories,
one app for a pet's life.

PETLOG is a pet life-cycle platform that keeps a pet's whole life in one account: from the day it joins the family, through growth, health and daily life, to old age and the memories after farewell. Today it runs as a records, health and family-care app for dog and cat owners; building on those records, it plans to widen step by step into care services, shopping, safety and shelter links.

Five values PETLOG keeps

Care
so small changes are not missed.

Steady meal, walk, weight and medication records help owners notice changes in condition first.

Connection
with family, close to the records.

Families care from the same records, and future information and services stay close to them.

Lifetime
from first meeting to last goodbye.

The changing needs of the early months, adult years and old age, on one life record.

Trust
checked information and clear rules.

No diagnosis, ads kept apart from information, and no record shared without the owner's consent.

Accessibility
easy for everyone.

Large text and simple screens, so even first-time owners find the app easy.

PROBLEM → SOLUTION

Three problems
owners really face.

Caring for one pet takes vet, grooming, food, walk and care information — and most of it is scattered.

Scattered records

Records are scattered everywhere

Vaccination dates sit in a clinic booklet, food notes in a memo app, photos in the phone gallery. At the vet, "since when?" is hard to answer.

PETLOG's approach

One profile per pet, with daily life, health and schedules gathered on it. This is available in the app today.

Information gap

Care services are hard to compare and trust

Groomers, hotels and sitters differ in pricing policy, accepted pets and cancellation rules, and there are few ways to check who to trust. Monthly costs and large treatment bills add up.

PETLOG's approach

Service discovery and booking with business checks and real reviews is planned. Booking and payment come only to the app after legal, payment-provider (PG) and security review.

Disconnected life stages

Adoption, loss and farewell are disconnected

Shelter adoption, reporting a lost pet, funerals and memorials each start from scratch somewhere else — usually at the most stressful moment.

PETLOG's approach

Adoption, lost-pet and foster guidance linked to official shelters and bodies, and memorial records, are planned as later-life areas. Public-good features are meant to run without fees.

OUR APPROACH

How NEWON
proposes to solve it.

Rather than adding many features at once, PETLOG first makes everyday records solid, then connects services on top of them one by one.

  1. 01

    Daily records come first

    Meal, walk, weight and vaccination records are free core features, so owners can build the habit without cost. This stage is in the app today.

  2. 02

    Records lead to services

    Records and schedules are designed to lead naturally to finding and booking grooming, care, hotels and training. Planned.

  3. 03

    A whole life in one account

    Widening into old-age care, lost-pet readiness, shelter links and memorials — the later part of a pet's life. Planned.

WHO IT'S FOR

Who uses
PETLOG.

The first core users are dog and cat owners. Other species follow once their health, husbandry and legal requirements are checked.

  • Dog and cat owners

    From owners living alone to households with their first pet — people who want daily records and health schedules in one place.

  • Families who care together

    Families who split care, like a child walking the dog and a parent giving medicine. Invitations and change history show who recorded what.

  • Owners of senior pets

    People keeping steady weight, meal and medication records to discuss with their vet.

  • Future adopters (planned)

    People looking for official shelter adoption notices and preparation guidance. Shelter links and adoption guidance are planned.

  • Pet businesses and bodies (planned)

    Groomers, hotels, trainers and shelters are the future users of business tools and partnership models. Partnership inquiries are welcome now.

WHAT'S DIFFERENT

How it differs
from the usual options.

This comparison is PETLOG's differentiation hypothesis about types of service. It claims no advantage over any company and does not present unbuilt features as existing strengths.

Compared with record apps

Apps that only store records rarely lead to the next action.

PETLOG's approach

Records connect to schedules and reminders, and are designed to lead to future service discovery and booking on the same profile.

Compared with shopping apps

When selling is the centre, owners' records and care context tend to drop out.

PETLOG's approach

Records and health are the free core; shopping is added on top. Pet records are never shared with sellers automatically.

Compared with single providers and communities

Booking, reviews and information are spread across different places.

PETLOG's approach

A life-cycle structure from adoption to old age and after farewell in one account, including public-good areas such as adoption and lost pets.

TRUST & RESPONSIBILITY

Trust matters,
for pets and their owners.

  • Accurate information and sources

    Pet life information is shared with its evidence and sources checked. Ads and sponsorship are always clearly marked.

  • Protecting owners' privacy

    Owners' records and personal data belong to them and are never shared with businesses or sellers without consent.

  • Responsible health information

    PETLOG does not diagnose disease or replace a vet's judgment. When something seems wrong, we suggest seeing a vet.

  • Animal welfare and respect for life

    No selling or secondhand trading of live animals and no illegal breeding or sales; we support responsible adoption.

  • Qualified service providers

    Professional services such as care, grooming and funerals connect only providers whose qualifications and business requirements are checked.

  • Transparent commerce and partnerships

    Sellers are checked, and partnerships and terms are shown accurately. Essential health records are never over-charged.
