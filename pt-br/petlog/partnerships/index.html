PARTNERSHIP VISION

Pet service partnerships
built on records.

PETLOG puts the records owners keep every day at the centre, and aims for partnerships where those records lead to trusted businesses, brands and bodies at the moment they are needed. These are models and possibilities, not signed partnerships.

POC & PILOT

How we propose
PoCs and pilots.

Rather than a large contract up front, we propose starting with a small pilot with an agreed scope and measures.

  1. STEP 01

    Set the scope

    Keep the area, number of businesses, period and services narrow.

  2. STEP 02

    Agree the measures

    Decide in advance on measures such as completion rate per booking request, intent to return and operating burden.

  3. STEP 03

    Check safeguards

    Confirm legal, payment and privacy requirements and the incident process before the pilot.

  4. STEP 04

    Share results and decide

    Review the results together and decide whether to widen, change or stop.

DATA & API

Data and API
possibilities.

No partner API or data integration is offered today. These are directions open to discussion; in any integration, records never leave without the owner's consent.

  • Booking and schedule sync (planned)

    Connecting a business's booking system with the PETLOG calendar; only the booking details the owner chooses are exchanged.

  • Consent-based record sharing (under review)

    Passing a record summary the owner selects to a clinic or care business.

  • Smart-device data (planned)

    Bringing records from manufacturers' feeders and GPS devices into the app, after certification and security checks.

  • Official notice information (planned)

    Showing official adoption and lost-pet information from shelters and local government, with its source.

PRINCIPLES

Partnership principles

  • Animal welfare and respect for life.
  • Owners' records and personal data are never given to partners without consent.
  • Care, hotel and grooming providers are checked before being connected.
  • Veterinary and insurance law is followed; insurance partnerships only with properly licensed businesses.
  • Partnerships are shown accurately; no one is shown as a partner before a written agreement.
  • Public-interest information from official bodies is kept distinct from commercial partnerships.
  • Terms are set transparently, by written agreement after discussion.
  • Sellers are checked before selling, and regulated items such as animal drugs are not handled.

PROCESS

How it works

The areas shown are the kinds of partners PETLOG wants to work with, not signed partnerships. These steps are for orientation; nothing is signed automatically.

  1. 01

  2. 02

    We review fit, legal and safety requirements, and business or seller eligibility.

  3. 03

    Scope

    Agree scope, roles, timing and how owners' data is protected.

  4. 04

    Terms and agreement

    Review the terms and sign what was agreed in a written agreement.

  5. 05

    Start

    Start on the agreed terms and review together as it runs.
