BEFORE YOU WRITE

Please check before writing.

  1. 01For app issues, include your device, app version and the screen where it happened.
  2. 02PETLOG cannot diagnose health problems; please contact a nearby vet.
  3. 03We only ask for your name, email and message, and use them only to reply.
  4. 04For partnership or seller inquiries, include a company introduction and proposed scope. Sending an inquiry does not create a partnership.
  5. 05For insurance sign-up, claims or adoption procedures, please use each insurer's or official shelter's own channel.
  6. 06Booking, payment, seller onboarding and sales are not offered on this website.
