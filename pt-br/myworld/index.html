MY WORLD

Every place you visit
becomes your own world map.

MY WORLD is NEWON's map-based personal travel record app. It starts by putting one person's travels on a map, and grows toward a global travel platform that connects the whole journey.

NOW

Today the MY WORLD app is a travel record service: fill in the countries and cities you have visited on the home world map, then look back through routes, photos, notes, statistics and an AI travel report. Booking and a shared community are not offered yet.

WHY WE BEGAN

The trip ends.
The map stays.

When a trip is over, the photos sit in a phone album, the plans in a group chat and the receipts in a wallet. A few years later it is hard even to remember exactly where you went.

MY WORLD began as a way to keep those memories in their most intuitive form: a map. As the countries and cities you have visited fill in one by one, travel stops being a pile of scattered files and becomes the world you have walked.

That is why the first screen of MY WORLD is always the world map. The more you record, the more the map takes on a shape that is yours alone.

THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

From scattered trips
to a single flow.

Travelers still move between apps, notes and photo albums to prepare for and remember their trips. Here is the picture MY WORLD wants to change, side by side.

MISSION & VISION

Starting from one person's travels,
connecting the world.

The mission MY WORLD keeps, and what it hopes to become.

  1. Mission

    Help anyone record the world they have travelled, simply and beautifully, and keep it for a long time.

  2. Vision

    Become a global travel platform where discovering, planning, booking, travelling, recording and sharing connect as one.

  3. Promise

    A traveler's records belong to the traveler. Partnerships and ads are always shown apart from records.

  4. Approach

    We do not open everything at once. We make the record app solid first, then expand in a validated order.

PRINCIPLES

Eight things
MY WORLD holds to.

  1. 01

    Map first

    Whatever features are added, the first screen is the world map.

  2. 02

    Records belong to travelers

    Travel records and location data are handled only as far as needed, and how to delete an account is public.

  3. 03

    Available vs planned

    What you can use in the app and what is still planned are always shown separately.

  4. 04

    No selling before contracts

    No bookable products are shown before affiliate contracts are signed.

  5. 05

    No holding of funds

    In the base design, NEWON does not hold transaction funds between travelers and suppliers.

  6. 06

    Ads are labelled as ads

    Partnerships, ads and sponsorships are never mixed into records or reviews and are clearly labelled.

  7. 07

    Share when ready

    The community opens as a shared space only after server integration and operating policies are ready.

  8. 08

    Expand step by step

    We expand in a validated order, checking the responsibilities and regulations of the travel industry.
