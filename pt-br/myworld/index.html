MY WORLD

Every moment of travelling the world,
in one place.

From the first idea to planning, booking, experiences, records and sharing. MY WORLD aims to be a global travel platform that connects travelers, travel businesses and local tourism.

NOW

Today the MY WORLD app is a travel-record service: mark the countries and cities you have visited on the world map on its home screen, and look back on trips through routes, photos, stats and an AI travel report. Booking, commerce and a shared community are being prepared step by step.

OUR VISION

Scattered travel moments,
in one connected flow.

  1. 01

    From the excitement of planning to new places and memories that last — a trip is one connected journey. MY WORLD aims to link its beginning and end inside one app.

  2. 02

    Today discovery, booking, records and sharing are spread across many services. Starting from the home-screen world map and travel records, MY WORLD aims to connect that flow.

  3. 03

    Together with travel agencies, stay and tour operators, brands, creators, regions and institutions, we are building a structure that helps them meet travelers more precisely. Ads and partnerships are always clearly labelled.

  4. 04

    The next version (V2.0) is being prepared around five tabs: World, Travel, Book, Community and My. The world-map home screen stays as it is — it is the signature of MY WORLD.

WORLD MAP & MEMORIES

Everywhere you have been
becomes your own world map.

Where MY WORLD began — and the core features you can use in the app today. The world map on the home screen, the first thing you see when you open the app, is the signature screen of MY WORLD.

Available in the app

  1. 01

    World map

    Fill in the countries and cities you have visited and complete your own world map. The more you travel, the more of the map fills in.

  2. 02

    City & region records

    See visits and completion by city and region on the map — even within one country, see at a glance where you have been.

  3. 03

    Routes

    Record routes and distance by plane, train, car or on foot.

  4. 04

    Photos & notes

    Keep photos, notes and a travel journal for every trip, so the memory of each day stays.

  5. 05

    Stats & AI report

    Look back on your travel patterns with stats on countries, days and distance, and an AI travel report.

  6. 06

    Completion by continent

    See how many countries you have visited on each continent, and find the regions you have not yet explored on the map.

SHAREONMY WORLD focuses on buying, reselling and giving away travel gear; SHAREON focuses on sharing and renting items and equipment. For example, you would buy or resell a suitcase on MY WORLD and rent one for a while on SHAREON. The two services are not connected today.

EXECUTION ROADMAP

Not everything at once —
we expand step by step.

Travel is highly competitive, and legal responsibility and supplier contracts matter. So we expand in a validated order.

We are in stage 1 today. Stage 2 onwards are future plans and timing is not fixed.

  1. 01

    Stage 1 Current stage

    Complete the travel-record app

    Stabilise the world map, visited countries, records, photos and stats, and grow the user base. Prepare the V2.0 structure around the world-map home screen.

  2. 02

    Stage 2

    Planning & AI

    Expand itineraries, place discovery, budgets and planning assistance, and prepare shared itineraries.

  3. 03

    Stage 3

    Booking & package partnerships

    Validate real booking demand through partnerships with qualified stay, tour, activity and package providers. No products are shown before contracts.

  4. 04

    Stage 4

    Commerce & community

    Prepare travel-gear shopping, secondhand, group buying and a shared community built on server integration.

  5. 05

    Stage 5

    Business SaaS, public & global

    Expand to booking and operations SaaS for travel businesses, corporate travel, tourism-body cooperation and global licensing.
