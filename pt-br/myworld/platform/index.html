HOW THE APP TREATS YOUR RECORDS

Five ways we
handle your records.

  1. 01

    Travel records and location data are handled only as far as needed for recording and showing the map.

  2. 02

    How to delete your account and data is published on the account deletion page on the web.

  3. 03

    Until server integration, community features are not presented as shared with other users.

  4. 04

    Stored per-trip colours stay the same when you change how the map is displayed.

  5. 05

    Partner and ad information is never mixed into your records and is always shown separately.

PLATFORM FAQ

Frequently asked questions
about the services.

  1. Q1What can I use in the app right now?

    The home world map, visited countries and cities, routes, photos and notes, travel statistics, the AI travel report, and itinerary, checklist and expense records. Some features may be part of a premium subscription.

  2. Q2Can I book in the Book tab?

    Not yet. Until affiliate contracts are signed, the booking area shows no bookable products and stays “In preparation”.

  3. Q3Will other people see what I post in the community?

    The shared community opens only after server integration and operating policies are ready. Until then it is not presented as a feed shared with other users.

  4. Q4If I change the map colour, will my trip colours be lost?

    No. The map shows MY WORLD blue by default and you can choose per-trip colours; stored trip colours do not change.

  5. Q5Is there an iOS app?

    The website currently links to Google Play. Release plans for other platforms will be announced once set.

This page introduces the MY WORLD business. The website does not offer booking, ordering, payment, sign-in, seller registration or record entry; the actual features are in the MY WORLD app.

Items marked “In preparation” or “Under partnership review” are business plans. Timing and scope may change depending on partnership contracts, regulations and service validation.
