HOW TO CONTACT

How to contact us.

  1. 01

    Choose a type

    Pick an inquiry type above. If none fits, choose the closest one or a general inquiry.

  2. 02

    Contact us

    ‘Contact us’ opens Newon’s contact page with MY WORLD and the inquiry type preselected. You can also write to newon@newon.app.

  3. 03

    Review & reply

    Our team reviews your message and replies. Partnership and institutional inquiries may take longer to review, and our team will ask separately if more material is needed.

BEFORE YOU SEND

What to have ready
before you write.

When the details we need are there from the start, our team can review and reply faster. Please leave out sensitive information.

Please include

  • App features & bugs

    • Device model, operating system, app version, and the screen and situation where the problem happened.

  • Subscription & billing

    • Which store you use (Google Play or App Store) and your question. Cancellations and refunds are handled in the store’s subscription settings.

  • Travel records & account

    • Your question and the device you use. The account deletion guide explains how to delete right away.

  • Travel business partnerships

    • Company introduction and team, partnership type, products or services and regions, registrations and licences such as travel-agency registration, and cancellation and refund policies.

  • Tourism bodies & local governments

    • Introduction of the institution, the project purpose and target area, and the procedure.

  • Technology & APIs

    • The technology area, integration approach, and security and data-handling standards. There is no public API today.

  • Global partnerships

    • Company or institution introduction, target countries and direction.

  • Press & investors

    • Outlet or affiliation, purpose of the inquiry and deadline. We answer with material that can be made public.

  • Privacy note · Please do not include sensitive information such as passport or ID numbers or copies, card or bank details, your real-time location or health information. If anything specific is needed, we will ask for it separately in a safe way.

  • This page introduces the MY WORLD business. The website does not offer booking, ordering, payment, sign-in, seller registration or record entry — the actual features live in the MY WORLD app.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can I book or buy anything on the website?

No. The website introduces the business. Booking, ordering, payment and sign-in are not offered on the web.

Which inquiries can I send by email?

Every type can go to newon@newon.app. You can also use ‘Contact us’ on this page to send it through Newon’s contact page with MY WORLD and the type selected.

What should I include in a bug report?

Device model, operating system, app version and the screen and situation are enough. Do not include sensitive data such as passport, card or location details.

How do I cancel or get a refund for a subscription?

Cancellations and refunds are handled in Google Play or App Store subscription settings. Send other questions as a subscription and billing inquiry.

How much are prices or fees?

New subscription plans and partner or sales fees are not final. For the current app subscription, check the benefits and prices shown in the app and store.

Will my proposal appear in the app right away?

No. It is published only after review, discussion, a PoC if needed, and a contract. Partners’ products are never shown as bookable before a contract.

What material is available for investors?

We reply with business material that can be made public. Prices and revenue figures in the business plan are unverified proposals, and no investment outcome is promised.

How do I delete my account and data?

The account deletion page explains how, and how data is handled.

Where do I ask about other NEWON services?

On the NEWON official contact page you can choose the platform. ‘Contact us’ on this page opens it with MY WORLD already selected.
