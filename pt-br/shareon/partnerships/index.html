OPEN MARKETPLACE

No partnership contract needed —
list and rent directly.

Not a closed model where only contracted partners can join, but an open marketplace where qualified people and businesses list and rent directly. The types below are where a separate collaboration adds value.

  1. 01

    People and businesses sign up directly, without a separate partnership contract, to list things, spaces, mobility and services.

  2. 02

    Qualifications and safety are checked per item. Items with legal requirements, such as vehicles and professional equipment, are handled only by qualified operators.

  3. 03

    Payment, insurance and logistics are areas for separate collaboration. Payments and deposits operate only after a payment provider’s deposit method is confirmed.

  4. 04

    Basic listing and renting are meant to work without a subscription or partnership.

  5. 05

    Ads and paid placement are always labelled as ads, and companies without a signed agreement are never shown as partners.

PROCESS

How it works.

After we receive a proposal we review the business, legal and operating conditions, then discuss and agree before starting. Results and timing depend on the proposal.

  1. 01

    Proposal

    Tell us through the inquiry form what you offer, your resources and the role you have in mind.

    →
  2. 02

    Review

    Business, legal and operating checks, plus item-specific qualifications.

    →
  3. 03

    Discussion

    Agree roles, responsibilities and operating conditions together.

    →
  4. 04

    Agreement

    A contract where one is needed. No one is shown as a partner before signing.

    →
  5. 05

    Operation

    Start step by step, from a small area and category.
