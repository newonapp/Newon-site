BabyLog

From pregnancy planning to a child's growth,
connecting every family moment.

BABYLOG aims to be a platform that connects the records, information, communication and wide range of services that parenting and family life need into one experience.

How It Connects

One record
reaches the whole family.

The areas are not isolated features; they connect around one child profile and one family account.

  1. 01

    Child profile and family account

    The starting point of every record and permission, with stage and basic details per child.

  2. 02

    Everyday records

    Feeding, sleep, diapers, meals, growth and health records build up on one timeline.

  3. 03

    Summaries, statistics, schedules

    Records turn into growth charts, a reference-only AI summary and hospital and education reminders.

  4. 04

    Family sharing

    Within the permitted scope, parents, grandparents and guardians see the same information and keep adding to it.

  5. 05

    Explore and market

    The stage that leads on to institutions, services and goods suited to the child's stage. Being prepared for the next version, with no personalized ads based on children's data.

Children's, health and family data is never shared automatically with other NEWON apps (LIVON, SHAREON, PETLOG, ONGIL, MY WORLD) without explicit consent and lawful authority.

Proposed app menu structure

The five bottom tabs proposed in the integrated business plan, grouped so families keep finding things in the same place as features grow.

  1. 01

    Home

    Family schedule, growth summary, today's to-dos, safety guidance

  2. 02

    Growth

    Pregnancy, parenting, health, school, learning and growth records

  3. 03

    Discover

    Institutions, booking, market, Books, Toy Club, welfare, safety

  4. 04

    Community

    Family experiences, questions, reviews and information

  5. 05

    My

    Family account, permissions, subscriptions, devices, settings

A proposed structure that may differ from the current app screens. Emergency child-protection features are never hidden in complex menus.

27 Long-term Areas

Twenty-seven long-term areas,
five groups.

The full expansion scope of BABYLOG designed in the integrated business plan. The number of areas is a long-term planning unit and does not mean everything is built. Areas share common account, record and transaction systems, so there is no separate app or server per area. Features available in the app today are written in the present tense; other areas are written as plans.

Building on the parenting and family services it offers today, BABYLOG is expanding into everyday life, education, care, business and public-sector areas. Some of the businesses and features introduced here are part of future development or partnership plans.

Inside the App

The screens a family
opens every day.

Illustrative screens built from the features in today's app and the proposed menu structure. The actual app may look different.

02 · Growth Journey

From pregnancy and birth
to every stage of growth.

It starts from one child profile and continues into the records and information each stage needs.

  • Pregnancy & birth

    Start recording from pregnancy in the app and manage weekly changes and checkup schedules together.

  • Child profile

    Manage growth stage, health information and basic schedule for each child in the app.

  • Expanding by growth stage

    Widening the records and management a child needs into play, education and family-life stages as the child grows.

03 · Parenting & Records

Feeding, sleep, meals, growth —
records every day.

Repeated parenting moments are logged with ease, and small records add up to a child's growth story.

  • Feeding, sleep, diaper, meal and play records

    Easily log everyday moments in the app and check them in a daily summary.

  • Growth & health records

    Keep height, weight, vaccinations and health history in one timeline.

  • Growth statistics

    See growth trends and daily patterns in charts.

04 · Family Connection

Family sees and manages
together.

Parenting is something a family does together. Guardians and family check and continue the same records.

  • Shared family management

    In the app, parents and guardians look at the same child's records and schedules together.

  • Permissions per guardian

    Step by step, we are designing scopes of access by role: parents, grandparents, guardians and teens. See the principles under Roles & Permissions below.

05 · Learning & Education

Play, learning,
and links to education.

Starting from schedules and widening into play and learning information and links to education institutions.

  • Hospital and education schedules

    Gather hospital visits, vaccinations, daycare, kindergarten and education schedules and family plans in the app.

  • Play, reading and learning information

    BABYLOG aims to provide activity information matched to each developmental stage, such as play by age, reading and sensory activities.

  • Education content and institutions

    Expanding into connecting education content with programs of institutions such as daycare centers and kindergartens.

06 · Memories & Milestones

Keeping passing time
as memories.

BABYLOG aims for an experience that lets families look back together on the fast-changing early years.

  • Photos, diary and growth memories

    Building an experience that gathers photos, diary entries and month-by-month growth records into one archive.

  • Growth albums and reports

    Expanding into an experience that turns growth records into albums and reports.

07 · Smart Assistance

Reference-only help
to organize records.

AI is a reference tool that organizes and presents records.

  • AI record summary and general information

    In the app, record patterns are organized for reference and general information suited to age and situation is provided. It does not replace medical diagnosis or professional advice.

08 · Explore & Discover

Toward the products and services
a family needs.

Roles & Permissions

Parents, grandparents, guardians, teens:
different scopes of access.

Family sharing is in the app today. The role-based permissions below are the design principles BABYLOG follows; detailed features are settled during development.

Parents and legal guardians

Create the child profile and manage all records. As main administrators they decide on family invitations, sharing scope, export and deletion; data on children under 14 is handled on the basis of legal-guardian consent.

Grandparents and family members

See and add to records and schedules within the scope they are invited to, focused on what is needed to care for the child together.

Guardians and caregivers

A limited permission is planned so caregivers only see the schedules and records needed while looking after the child. Family links are designed with situations that require verifying the relationship or lawful authority in mind.

Teens themselves

The scope a teen manages grows with age, and records that need privacy are planned not to be shown to parents automatically. At adulthood, records move to the young person's own account.

There is no structure in which an adult child's records keep being shown to parents automatically.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can I keep parenting records on the website?

No. Records and account management are in the BABYLOG app for iOS and Android. The website only introduces the service and routes inquiries.

How do I use it with my family?

In the app you can invite parents, grandparents and guardians to see the same child's records and schedules. Role-based permissions are being designed step by step following the principles above.

Does the AI judge my child's health or development?

No. The AI only summarizes records for reference; it does not diagnose, assess development or prescribe. If you are worried about health, please contact a medical institution.

When will community, booking and market be available?

They are being prepared for the next app version and no release date has been set. We will announce them in the app and on this website when they launch.

Is there a paid subscription?

The app offers a Premium subscription. Prices and terms follow what the App Store and Google Play show in the app. Other products such as a family subscription are proposals and are not on sale.

What happens to records if I delete my account?

How to delete an account and what is deleted are explained on the account deletion page. Read the account deletion guide

Can I report a missing child or child abuse to BABYLOG?

No. In an emergency, please use official routes such as 112 first. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child's location or identity.

Is my child's information shared with other NEWON apps?

Not automatically, and never without explicit consent and lawful authority.
