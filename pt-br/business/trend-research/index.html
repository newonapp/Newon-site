OVERVIEW

Information overload.
No decision structure.

Conversations about ‘what’s trending’ show up often in product, marketing, and strategy meetings. The harder part is knowing which signals are noise and which shifts matter for your decisions.

News, reports, competitor updates, social and community signals, and regulatory change each offer clues — but rarely in a form teams can decide from.

TREND RESEARCH starts by defining topic and time horizon. Then we collect, classify, and structure signals into themes, direction of change, and product, content, and strategy implications in one trend brief.
