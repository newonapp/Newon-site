SCOPE LEVELS

A guide to project complexity.

Not fixed packages — we scope and quote based on your goal and the depth you need.

01FOCUSED MARKET BRIEF

  • Single market or category
  • 3–5 core questions
  • Desk research focus
  • Summary brief (~10–15 pages)
  • Opportunity and risk points

02MARKET & COMPETITIVE MAP

  • Segment and player map
  • Compare 5–10 key competitors
  • Positioning, pricing, messaging summary
  • Opportunity areas and implications
  • Presentation summary optional

03CUSTOM RESEARCH

  • Multiple markets or categories
  • Primary research (interviews/surveys) optional
  • Deeper trend, regulation, and risk analysis
  • Custom presentation
  • Follow-up validation or BUILD linkage
