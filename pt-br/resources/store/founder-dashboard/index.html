HOW TO USE

How to use it.

  1. 01

    Fill Overview on Monday

    Refresh last week’s numbers and Active Products first
    to lock the starting state of the week.

  2. 02

    Three Founder priorities

    Write only three must-finish items on Weekly Priorities.
    Park everything else.

  3. 03

    Read Product and Growth

    Scan sprint, bugs, experiments plus acquisition,
    activation, retention — decide whether priorities shift.

  4. 04

    Check Business and Customers

    Skim revenue, cost, runway, feedback, and support queues.
    Promote only urgent issues.

  5. 05

    Friday Decision Log

    Record decisions and parked experiments,
    then roll into next week’s Overview.
