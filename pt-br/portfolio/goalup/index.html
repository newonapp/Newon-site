GoalUp

Goal tracking · Challenges · AI growth coach

App inteligente de metas: definição, análise de progresso e insights de crescimento com IA.

Ver projeto App Store Google Play

Ideia

GoalUp is

a smart growth platform to set goals, stay consistent, and record your progress over time.

Manage daily actions and long-term goals in one system—and use AI analysis for more efficient achievement.

Challenges, growth stats, and execution logs help you see consistency become visible progress.

Principais funções

Goal management

Create goals and organize them by category. Set repeat cycles, alerts, priority, and time ranges.

Challenges

Run challenges solo or with friends. Keep steady practice fun and engaging.

Goal alerts

Recurring alerts and reminders help you never forget your goals.

Growth analysis

Analyze success rate, streaks, and completion trends. See consistency as data.

Stats visualization

View weekly, monthly, and yearly completion in charts. See your growth journey intuitively.

AI growth analysis

AI analyzes goal patterns and suggests failure causes and improvements. Build more effective growth routines.

Streak tracking

Manage streaks and growth history. Small steps become lasting habits.
