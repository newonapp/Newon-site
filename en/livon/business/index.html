Principle

Forty categories, not forty apps.

  1. 01

    One platform built on a shared data structure, life stages, event-led connections and managed official sources — the only way to keep updates manageable and information accurate.

  2. 02

    Standard products and our own platform — not custom client work.

  3. 03

    No prices are set. Prices for subscription, AI, enterprise, advertising and referral revenue are all proposals and not applied. No fees are charged on public benefits or statutory payments, and paid ads are always kept apart from general information.

Roadmap

Five stages.

  1. 01

    Now

    Stabilise the LIVON app

    Verify Life Stage, Today Feed, Explore, My Life, Community, existing data, API and anonymous use — stabilising the free core that works without an account first.

  2. 02

    Next

    Structure the 40 categories

    Classification, official sources, search and filters, links to life stages — with source, date and region on public information.

  3. 03

    Later

    Life Event Explorer

    Information connected around events such as enlisting, a first job, moving out, marriage, birth and retirement

  4. 04

    Later

    Personalisation, AI, community

    Interests, personalised information, explanations, community quality. Accounts and sync are considered only if existing anonymous records are kept.

  5. 05

    Later

    Enterprise, public, global

    Enterprise solutions, institutional work, official data links, localisation. The market opens only once real sellers exist.

Operating Principles

We keep general information apart from licensed services.

  • No insurance sales or brokerage.

  • No sale or recommendation of financial products.

  • No investment advice or personal recommendations.

  • No diagnosis, treatment or other medical practice.

  • No legal or tax representation.

  • No fees on public benefits.

  • Performance-based fees in regulated fields such as medical and legal are on hold pending legal review.

  • The market is in preparation: no products, booking or payment are shown until real sellers exist.

  • Personal used-goods trading is not run in LIVON; it points to SHAREON.

  • Core life information stays free and usable without logging in.
