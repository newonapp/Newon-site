Before you write

  • Include the inquiry type and your organisation.

  • Tell us the life area you have in mind.

  • Add timing, scope and links to any material.

  • For a correction, send the page address and the official source you checked — it helps us verify quickly.

What we cannot answer

  • Personal legal, medical, financial or tax advice — we point to the right official body.

  • Eligibility or amounts for public support — each agency's notice and office decide.

  • Applying or filing on someone's behalf.

  • Emergencies — please use the official emergency services first.

Medical, legal, insurance, financial and welfare information is general guidance with links to official bodies. It does not replace professional judgement.

FAQ

About contacting us

01Where will the reply come from?

We reply to the contact details you left on NEWON's contact page or the email address you wrote from.

02Can I contact you inside the LIVON app?

The app is in preparation. For now, please use NEWON's official contact page or email.

03Can you recover records saved on my device?

My Life records are stored only on your device. NEWON cannot see or recover them, so we recommend backing up your device.
