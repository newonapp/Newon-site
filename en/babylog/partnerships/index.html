01 · Process

From proposal to delivery,
five steps.

Cooperation is reviewed in this order. An inquiry does not promise an outcome at any step.

01

Proposal

Send what you would like to work on through the inquiry form.

02

Review

We look at fit with BABYLOG's direction and what is possible now.

03

Discussion

We discuss scope, terms and timing together.

04

Contract

Agreed terms are put into a contract.

05

Delivery

Cooperation proceeds within the contracted scope.

CHILD SAFETY & SOCIAL IMPACT

Twelve types of institutions
for child protection and public interest.

We seek institutions to cooperate with in order to convey official information accurately for children who need protection and support.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

This page is a call for cooperating institutions. Only institutions with a confirmed agreement are shown as partners, and referring to an official source is distinct from official cooperation.

01

Child rights public bodies

Purpose
Accurately guide official information on children's rights and protection.
Value provided
A channel that delivers official information to families and citizens.
Official cooperation process
Inquiry → verify the institution's official standing → agree scope → written agreement → published once confirmed
Operating and privacy conditions
No child personal data is exchanged; only publishable official information is handled.
