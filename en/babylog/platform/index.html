BabyLog

From pregnancy planning to a child's growth,
connecting every family moment.

BABYLOG aims to be a platform that connects the records, information, communication and wide range of services that parenting and family life need into one experience.

How It Connects

One record
reaches the whole family.

The areas are not isolated features; they connect around one child profile and one family account.

  1. 01

    Child profile and family account

    The starting point of every record and permission, with stage and basic details per child.

  2. 02

    Everyday records

    Feeding, sleep, diapers, meals, growth and health records build up on one timeline.

  3. 03

    Summaries, statistics, schedules

    Records turn into growth charts, a reference-only AI summary and hospital and education reminders.

  4. 04

    Family sharing

    Within the permitted scope, parents, grandparents and guardians see the same information and keep adding to it.

  5. 05

    Explore and market

    The stage that leads on to institutions, services and goods suited to the child's stage. Being prepared for the next version, with no personalized ads based on children's data.

Children's, health and family data is never shared automatically with other NEWON apps (LIVON, SHAREON, PETLOG, ONGIL, MY WORLD) without explicit consent and lawful authority.

Proposed app menu structure

The five bottom tabs proposed in the integrated business plan, grouped so families keep finding things in the same place as features grow.

  1. 01

    Home

    Family schedule, growth summary, today's to-dos, safety guidance

  2. 02

    Growth

    Pregnancy, parenting, health, school, learning and growth records

  3. 03

    Discover

    Institutions, booking, market, Books, Toy Club, welfare, safety

  4. 04

    Community

    Family experiences, questions, reviews and information

  5. 05

    My

    Family account, permissions, subscriptions, devices, settings

A proposed structure that may differ from the current app screens. Emergency child-protection features are never hidden in complex menus.

five groups.

The full expansion scope of BABYLOG designed in the integrated business plan. The number of areas is a long-term planning unit and does not mean everything is built. 09 · Our Commitment

A child's safety and a family's trust
come first.

The standards BABYLOG puts first as a service that handles children's and families' records.

  • Protecting children's and families' personal data

    We handle children's and families' personal data with care; how it is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

  • Trustworthy parenting and health information

    AI-organized content is for reference and does not replace medical, legal or educational professionals.

  • Data management that respects guardian permissions

    The principle is that the family decides how far records are shared.

  • An accessible service

    Used through the iOS and Android apps, with accessibility continuously improved so anyone can use it easily.

  • Responsible child safety and protection information

    We guide official information and reporting channels, and do not accept reports or identify a child's location or identity.

  • Public-interest access to information

    BABYLOG aims to let children and families in need reach official systems and institution information free of charge.

  • Limits on the role of AI

    AI is limited to summarizing records and general information; it never diagnoses, assesses development, identifies faces or selects children for foster care or adoption.

  • Minimal location features

    Location-related features are designed for the minimum needed for safety, not constant monitoring, balancing guardian permissions with children's rights.

Roles & Permissions

Parents, grandparents, guardians, teens:
different scopes of access.

Family sharing is in the app today. The role-based permissions below are the design principles BABYLOG follows; detailed features are settled during development.

Parents and legal guardians

Create the child profile and manage all records. As main administrators they decide on family invitations, sharing scope, export and deletion; data on children under 14 is handled on the basis of legal-guardian consent.

Grandparents and family members

See and add to records and schedules within the scope they are invited to, focused on what is needed to care for the child together.

Guardians and caregivers

A limited permission is planned so caregivers only see the schedules and records needed while looking after the child. Family links are designed with situations that require verifying the relationship or lawful authority in mind.

Teens themselves

The scope a teen manages grows with age, and records that need privacy are planned not to be shown to parents automatically. At adulthood, records move to the young person's own account.

There is no structure in which an adult child's records keep being shown to parents automatically.

PUBLIC WELFARE & CHILD SAFETY

We put a child's safety
and rights first.

Eight public-interest areas, from public parenting support to child safety, protection, independence and verified giving, connecting children and families in need with official information and institutions.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership, and it does not replace professional judgment, official reporting or legal procedures.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

Global Services

Services to take abroad
and how each country is localised.

Building on the shared family records of the current app, we are reviewing how to extend the services below to each country's context. All of this is a future plan; none of it runs as a separate overseas service today.

Services under review for expansion

  • Country-specific pregnancy and parenting information

    Pregnancy, birth, parenting, vaccination and growth information and official support programmes for each country, based on official local sources.

  • Shared family management

    Shared parenting records, family schedules and growth records that fit many kinds of families and living situations.

  • Education and growth services

    We review connecting each country's school system, school calendar, learning and career information with local institutions and services.

  • Family life service connections

    Baby goods, family life services, booking, community and related marketplaces are reviewed only after checking each country's consumer protection rules.

  • Child protection and social impact

    We review linking programmes for vulnerable children, children in care and education support with local child welfare institutions. Children's identities and cases are never made public.

Localisation criteria by country

  • Language and culture

    Not only the interface language but family terms, date and unit formats and differences in parenting culture.

  • Children's data and guardian consent

    We check each country's children's data protection law and the age and method of guardian consent, and open only features that meet them.

  • Child-related laws

    Child protection, reporting systems, foster care and adoption are reviewed country by country, and we link only official procedures and official bodies.

  • Official health and education information

    Vaccination schedules, growth standards and school systems follow official local sources, without wording that reads as a medical judgement.

  • Payments and transactions

    Subscriptions, commerce and booking are reviewed after checking local payment methods, currency, tax and consumer protection rules.

  • Data storage and customer support

    We check where family data is stored and the requirements for transfers abroad, and prepare customer support in the local language.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can I keep parenting records on the website?

No. Records and account management are in the BABYLOG app for iOS and Android. The website only introduces the service and routes inquiries.

How do I use it with my family?

In the app you can invite parents, grandparents and guardians to see the same child's records and schedules. Role-based permissions are being designed step by step following the principles above.

Does the AI judge my child's health or development?

No. The AI only summarizes records for reference; it does not diagnose, assess development or prescribe. If you are worried about health, please contact a medical institution.

When will community, booking and market be available?

They are being prepared for the next app version and no release date has been set. We will announce them in the app and on this website when they launch.

Is there a paid subscription?

The app offers a Premium subscription. Prices and terms follow what the App Store and Google Play show in the app. Other products such as a family subscription are proposals and are not on sale.

What happens to records if I delete my account?

How to delete an account and what is deleted are explained on the account deletion page. Read the account deletion guide

Can I report a missing child or child abuse to BABYLOG?

No. In an emergency, please use official routes such as 112 first. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child's location or identity.

Is my child's information shared with other NEWON apps?

Not automatically, and never without explicit consent and lawful authority.
