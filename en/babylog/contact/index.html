Contact Information

Before you get in touch.

Including these details helps us reply sooner.

  • App features, errors and account

    • Your device type (iOS or Android), the app version and the screen and situation where the problem happened

  • Subscription

    • The store you subscribed through (App Store or Google Play). Prices and terms follow the app screen in the store.

  • Account deletion

    • Please check the method and scope on the account deletion page first.

  • Business partnership and listing

    • Company or brand introduction, the closest partnership type and product safety and labeling information

  • Institution and public cooperation

    • Type of institution, the work you want to improve together (notices, schedules, guardian communication and so on) and child data protection standards

  • Child safety and welfare cooperation

    • Only cooperation proposals from institutions and companies are accepted. For individual reports or counseling, please use official routes such as 112.

  • Press and media

    • Outlet name, topic and deadline

  • Investment

    • The purpose of the inquiry and contact details. We share what can be disclosed.

  • NEWON · Inquiries go to NEWON’s contact page and can also be sent to newon@newon.app. This BABYLOG page does not collect personal data itself.

  • BABYLOG and NEWON · BABYLOG is a core NEWON platform. Questions about using the app are received through the same contact page.

  • Replies · We reply after review. An inquiry does not guarantee cooperation or a contract.

  • Keep personal data out · Please include only the contact details and content needed for a reply. You do not need to include unnecessary personal or sensitive data such as a child's name, photo or health information, or national ID numbers.

  • In an emergency, use official channels first. If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

FAQ

Common questions before you write.

Does an inquiry confirm a partnership?

No. We review the content and get back to you, but an inquiry does not guarantee cooperation or a contract. Only companies and institutions with a confirmed contract are shown as partners.

Can I report a missing child or child abuse here?

No. In an emergency, please use official routes such as 112 first. BABYLOG does not take reports or confirm a child's location or identity.

Can I sign up, pay or book on the website?

No. The website only introduces BABYLOG and connects inquiries. Records and account management are in the BABYLOG app (iOS and Android).

I want to delete my app account.

The method and scope of account deletion are on the account deletion page. See account deletion

Where do press and investment inquiries go?

Use ‘Contact us’ on the matching purpose above: NEWON's contact page opens with the press or investment type selected. You can also write to newon@newon.app.

How are my inquiry and personal data handled?

Inquiries go through NEWON's official inquiry form, and personal data is handled under the privacy policy. The BABYLOG web pages do not collect personal data separately.
