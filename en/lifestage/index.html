Why Life Stage

When life changes,
the information you need changes too.

From school and career questions to living on your own, a first job, a home, family, and a new daily life after work.

Each stage brings different choices, and different information.

Life Stage aims to make it easier to find information and services that fit where you are.

Life Journey

From the teens to the 70s,
one continuing journey.

Questions and choices shift with each stage. Life Stage connects the information and services that fit the present.

01

Teens

A time to find possibility

Exploring direction through path, school, and new experience.

  • Career exploration
  • School information
  • Learning and growth
  • Preparing to stand on your own

02

20s

A time to begin a life of your own

Independence, a first job, and the start of a daily life you build yourself.

  • Independence and housing
  • First work and adult life
  • Everyday money
  • Self-development

03

30s

A time to widen life’s base

Work and living in balance, and choices around home, relationships, and family.

  • Work and career
  • Housing and money
  • Relationships and family
  • Health and daily living

04

40s

A time to find life’s balance

Work, family, and personal aims held together while looking ahead.

  • Career and work
  • Family and living
  • Money management
  • Health and growth

05

50s

A time to prepare new possibility

Using what you have learned to prepare the next chapter of living and new chances.

  • Life transitions
  • Preparing for later years
  • Health
  • New learning and activity

06

60s

A time to start a new daily life

Designing life after work, and forming new activities and relationships.

  • Life after work
  • Leisure and hobbies
  • A healthy day
  • Social activity

07

70s

A time to keep living in your own way

Continuing a healthy, lively day that fits how you want to live.

  • Healthy living
  • Leisure and community
  • Local living information
  • Finding living support when needed

The themes for each decade are typical examples. Pace and needs differ from person to person.

When care or living support is needed, we may later connect with Ongil. The two services are not linked today.

Platform

Matched to the stage you are in,
connecting what you need.

Not a site that only collects tips by age, but a living platform that grows from finding information to connecting services.

01

Browse by life stage

The aim is an experience for browsing information and living topics that fit age and situation.

02

Personal guidance

Planned

We plan to introduce tailored guidance step by step, so information that fits interests and living situation is easier to find.

03

Living-service connections

Planned

We will expand toward browsing and connecting outside services and information in housing, money, learning, health, and leisure.

04

Experts and local services

Planned

We plan to widen, step by step, the ability to find relevant experts and local services when they are needed.

How it works

Find the next step
that fits you now.

Planned journey

This is a planned journey, not a live apply-and-book flow.

01

02

Browse what you need

03

Business expansion

Toward every stage of life,
a wider connection.

A future expansion plan, not a claim of current operations.

Unconfirmed partners, revenue, user counts, and service areas are not shown.

  1. 01
    A life-stage information platform

    Build a base so people from the teens through the 70s can browse living information and key services for each stage.

  2. 02
    More personal guidance

    Grow tailored guidance that reflects interests and living situation, not only age.

  3. 03
    Wider living services and experts

    We plan to expand connections with services, experts, and local offers in housing, money, learning, health, and leisure.

  4. 04
    A full life-stage platform

    The aim is a living platform people can keep using as their stage of life changes.

LIFE STAGE VISION

Even as life changes,
the connection continues.

Life Stage respects each person’s pace and choices, and grows as a platform that connects the information and services needed at every new beginning and change.
