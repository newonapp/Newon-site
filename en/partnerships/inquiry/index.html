HOW IT WORKS

These steps are for orientation only. Agreements are made only after review by both sides and in writing.

  1. 01 01 Proposal Send your proposal through the form or by email.
  2. 02 02 Internal review The relevant business team reviews the proposal and fit.
  3. 03 03 Meeting If useful, we meet to confirm goals and scope.
  4. 04 04 Terms We work out roles, duration and revenue sharing.
  5. 05 05 Agreement We sign a contract or agreement on what was agreed.
  6. 06 06 Partnership We start on the agreed schedule and terms.
