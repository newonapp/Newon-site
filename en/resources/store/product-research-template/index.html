recording observation,
forming hypotheses,
supporting or breaking them with evidence,
and moving findings into product decisions.

that connects study results to what you build.

OVERVIEW

Insight is not
a pile of notes.

Interview, pain, alternatives, competitors, reviews,
hypothesis, evidence, insight, decision —
one flow so research survives into the product.

Separate gut feel from evidenced findings
by giving them different cells.

This template turns research into sprint input,
not decorative report filler.

HOW TO USE

How to use it.

  1. 01

    Narrow the research question

    On Research Question, write only what this round must answer.
    Park the rest for a later round.

  2. 02

    Split quote from interpretation

    In Interview and Review Mining,
    keep user words and team interpretation in separate cells.

  3. 03

    Make hypotheses testable

    On Hypothesis, name the observation that would falsify it,
    then attach support or pushback on Evidence.

  4. 04

    Insight as one line

    Do not dump long summaries on Insight.
    Leave only conclusions that change the product.

  5. 05

    Close with Decision Log

    On Product Decision, record do / do-not
    and the linked evidence IDs.
