NEWON AI · ENTERPRISE

AI that understands company work
and connects it to action.

From an AI assistant shaped to your data and work environment, to work-running agents, system integration, and a company AI platform. We expand solutions for AI adoption step by step.

Core solutions ↓ Inquire about Enterprise AI ↗

This page introduces the Enterprise AI business. It is not a live self-serve product. We separate work that can be scoped through inquiry from later expansion. Scope is confirmed through inquiry.

AI NEWON

Why Enterprise AI

Beyond repeat tasks,
connecting how the company works.

Company information and work sit across documents, teams, and systems. People hunt for answers and move between tools to finish repeat work.

NEWON Enterprise AI aims to build information search, work support, automation, and system links step by step, around how the company actually operates and stores data.

  1. 01

    Scattered internal information

    Help people find work information that lives in documents and systems when they need it.

  2. 02

    Repeat work

    Support automation of drafting, sorting information, and handling routine requests.

  3. 03

    Unconnected systems

    Support linking the work tools and data a company already uses.

  4. 04

    Different ways of working

    Design an AI approach that fits size, industry, and how each team works.

  5. 05

    Running AI after launch

    Expand toward ongoing management of use, quality, cost, security, and access.

Core solutions

Four pillars of
the Enterprise AI business.

This is not a one-chatbot build. Scope is set through inquiry. Unbuilt platform features are marked as later plans.

Scoped through inquiry means range is set after reviewing needs. Later expansion is not available today.

Enterprise architecture

Company data and work systems,
as one AI experience.

Not a structure where every dataset is fed in automatically or every system is open. Access stays limited by permission and security policy.

  1. 01

    DATA

    Internal documents, databases, manuals, and operations data.

  2. 02

    KNOWLEDGE

    A knowledge base so AI can search and use company documents and work information.

  3. 03

    AI ASSISTANT & AGENT

    Search, work support, drafting, and approval-based action.

  4. 04

    INTEGRATION

    Links to ERP, CRM, groupware, and work tools.

  5. 05

    GOVERNANCE

    User access, data policy, run approval, records, cost, and quality.

Whether a link is possible is decided after reviewing APIs, access, data shape, and security needs.

Security & governance

Designed with access,
approval, and operations.

We do not claim unbuilt security features or certifications we do not hold. The list below is design and review work.

  1. 01

    Access

    User and organization access, and the range of data that can be used.

  2. 02

    Sensitive data and retention

    Review handling of sensitive information, retention, and deletion against company needs.

  3. 03

    Approval before action

    Sensitive runs and outside-system changes are designed with owner approval.

  4. 04

    Records and quality

    Run and change history, quality checks, usage, and cost.

  5. 05

    Integration rights

    Review rights to link outside systems and each company’s security requirements.

We do not use claims such as perfect security, full regulatory coverage, no data leaving the company, or completed international certification. We review each company’s security needs.

Implementation

From diagnosis to operations,
built in stages.

Scope and timing can change with real requirements.

  1. 01

    Work diagnosis

    Understand the work environment, repeat tasks, data, and the aim of introducing AI.

  2. 02

    Adoption design

    Review which work to apply, the range of adoption, data access, system links, and operating needs.

  3. 03

    PoC and limited build

    Build in a limited work area and check whether it can be used in real work.

  4. 04

    Link to live work

    Connect proven features to existing tools, systems, and data, and set needed approval steps.

  5. 05

    Operate and improve

    Review use, quality, cost, security, and usefulness, then improve.

  6. 06

    Results and further range

    Review operating results and widen applied work and features in stages.

A later introduction model — not a standard package that starts on request.
