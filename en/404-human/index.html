CORE IDEA

💡 This is not a game of simply picking the right answer.

01 / Think like AI

To survive, you must find the answers AI expects.

Overly emotional, human behavior raises suspicion.

02 / Keep your humanity

The safest choice is not always the best choice.

Keep abandoning humanity for survival and you may reach a different outcome.

03 / Two metrics in conflict

HUMAN DETECTION and HUMANITY push the player in opposite directions.

“Not being caught” and “staying human” are not the same goal.

CREATOR'S NOTE

✍️ Why this game was made

An era where AI writes,
makes images,
and writes code.

As technology mirrors human ability piece by piece,
the opposite question appeared.

“Then what makes a human
human to the very end?”

404: HUMAN started from that question.

It is not a game about fighting AI.
It puts you in a situation where you must act like AI to survive,
so you choose for yourself what humanity means.
