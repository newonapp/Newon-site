SOCIAL CONTENT vs MANAGEMENT

Content system and
monthly ops differ.

Social Content builds strategy, pillars, format, message, design, and templates. Social Management covers posting, comments/DM, scheduling, daily ops, and community. Monthly ops are not base scope.

01

02

SOCIAL MANAGEMENT

CTA SYSTEM — Save, Share, Comment, Follow, Visit, Download, Sign Up, Contact. Chosen by goal; not forced onto every post.
