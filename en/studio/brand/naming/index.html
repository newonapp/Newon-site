OVERVIEW

Even a name
needs strategy.

A strong name is not just a pleasant-sounding word.

It should carry what the brand does, who it is for, the impression it should leave, and how far it can grow.

Newon Naming analyzes brand and product, defines keywords and meaning axes, explores multiple directions, and develops candidates that can become a real brand.

We weigh criteria like these together.

  • Easy to remember
  • Easy to pronounce
  • Connected to brand character
  • Distinct from other services
  • Works in digital contexts
  • Scales as the product grows
  • Usable in global contexts

NAMING DIRECTIONS

We do not build names
from one method alone.

We set and explore different naming directions based on brand character.

01

DESCRIPTIVE

Descriptive
Names that make the product or service role easy to grasp.

02

COMPOUND

Compound
Combine two or more words or meanings into a new name.

03

EVOCATIVE

Evocative
Names that suggest the emotion or experience the brand wants.

04

SYMBOLIC

Symbolic
Names that symbolize brand philosophy or story.

05

INVENTED

Invented
Create a new word or expression as a distinctive brand asset.

06

GLOBAL

Global
Directions that are less language-locked and easier across markets.

We do not use every method on every project. We choose directions that fit the brand.
