INQUIRY CATEGORIES

What would you like to discuss?

There are six inquiry types. We do not take custom web or app production, custom development, design agency work or production quotes.

  • 01

    Platform participation & transactions

    For sellers and brands, service providers, rental businesses and space operators who want to list products, services or assets on a NEWON platform and take part in transactions. Listing and transaction features open according to each platform's launch stage and business verification.

    Sellers · Service providers · Rental businesses

  • 02

    Brand, service & content partnerships

    Propose brand campaigns, service integrations, or expert and creator content relevant to platform users. Partnership format and terms are agreed according to each platform's operating standards.

    Brand campaigns · Service links · Content

  • 03

    Enterprise service adoption

    Ask about adopting NEWON's standardized products being prepared on each platform, such as SaaS for childcare and education institutions, SaaS for care and welfare facilities, or employee family-welfare solutions. We review against standard products, not custom development.

    Institution SaaS · Employee welfare · Provider tools

  • 04

    Public sector & organization collaboration

    Local governments, public agencies, welfare and education institutions and organizations can propose public information services, local programs and public-interest collaboration. Public projects follow the applicable laws and procurement and contracting procedures.

    Local government · Public agencies · Institutions

  • 05

    Technology & global partnerships

    Ask about collaboration that helps the platforms grow, such as technical integration, data protection and infrastructure, localization for overseas markets, and licensing.

    Integration · Localization · Licensing

  • 06

    Other business inquiries

    Send any NEWON business proposal that does not fit the types above. Outsourced development or production requests are not accepted under this type either.

    NEWON business in general

SIX PLATFORMS

Business areas by platform

Inquiries are reviewed against NEWON's six platforms. Many of the business areas described are still in preparation or under review; each platform's business page shows the current status.

BABYLOG

A family-life platform built around an app for pregnancy, birth, parenting and growth records, expanding into SaaS for childcare and education institutions, parenting-brand and employee family-welfare solutions, public parenting-support information, parenting products and services, and overseas licensing.

View business page →

PETLOG

A pet-life platform built on daily and health records for pets, expanding into community and commerce, connections with qualified providers such as vets, groomers and carers, SaaS for pet businesses, public animal-welfare collaboration and global licensing.

View business page →

MY WORLD

A travel platform that starts from map-based travel records and extends toward booking partnerships for stays, transport, packages and activities, travel commerce, SaaS for business travel and travel companies, and local tourism and tourism-agency collaboration.

View business page →

SHAREON

A sharing-economy platform for renting and sharing physical assets between individuals and businesses (C2C, B2C, C2B, B2B), preparing to expand into rental-business and space-operator listings, business asset sharing, SaaS, public and global areas. The app has not launched, so no rentals, payments or settlements are operating yet.

View business page →

LIVON

A platform connecting life-stage information and services such as education, careers, housing and financial education. Rather than selling products directly, it focuses on information, user experience and solutions for organizations; most business areas are in preparation or at the concept stage. The app is being prepared for launch.

View business page →

ONGIL

A senior-life platform built on seniors' daily life, family connection, healthy living, and care and welfare information, expanding into tools for care providers, SaaS for care and welfare facilities, employee family-care welfare, public and local-government collaboration, and global licensing.

View business page →

WHO CAN INQUIRE

Who can send an inquiry

  • 01

    Sole traders, experts & creators

    Individual business owners, experts and creators considering platform listings, service provision or content collaboration. Some areas require business or qualification checks.

  • 02

    Companies & brands

    Companies, brands and sellers considering participation and transactions, brand and service partnerships, or enterprise service adoption.

  • 03

    Public bodies & organizations

    Local governments, public agencies, childcare, education and welfare institutions, and non-profits proposing public information or public-interest collaboration.

  • 04

    Technology & global partners

    Technology companies proposing integration or infrastructure collaboration, and overseas companies and institutions considering localization or licensing.

INQUIRY PROCESS

How inquiries are handled

Inquiries are reviewed in the order below. How long a review takes depends on the inquiry.

04STEPS

  1. 01

    Inquiry received

    Inquiries sent with the form below or by email arrive at NEWON's official inquiry mailbox.

  2. 02

    Proposal review

    We check the inquiry type, related platform and proposal against each platform's business direction and operating standards.

  3. 03

    Further information if needed

    If the review needs it, we ask for more material or information by email.

  4. 04

    Discussing fit and terms

    Where there is potential to work together, we discuss scope and terms. An inquiry does not guarantee partnership approval or a contract.
