OVERVIEW

What this collaboration is

Newon promotion is not selling leftover ad slots. We look for introductions that stay out of the way of the product and still give people useful information. Brands in parenting, pets, productivity, daily life, and travel — areas Newon already serves — come first.

New service promotion

Introduce a new product that is relevant to Newon users.

How we run promotion

The product experience comes first.

  • No heavy or forced ad exposure
  • Brands that relate to the service come first
  • Content should be useful, not only promotional
  • We may decline work that does not fit Newon

FIT

A good fit for brands in

  • Parenting
  • Pets
  • Productivity and lifestyle
  • Daily life, personal finance, and travel

We focus on brands that can sit naturally next to a Newon service.
