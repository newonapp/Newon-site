OVERVIEW

What this collaboration is

Product development at Newon follows how we plan and ship our own apps. It is not only screens or a list of features. We can move from the problem, through structure and UI/UX, into development and launch. You can inquire at the idea stage.

FROM IDEA TO LAUNCH

How an idea becomes a service

  1. IDEA Idea

    We confirm the idea and the problem to solve.

  2. PLAN Plan

    We design the features and product structure.

  3. DESIGN Design

    We make the flow and UI/UX concrete.

  4. BUILD Build

    We develop a product people can actually use.

  5. LAUNCH Launch

    We test, check, and ship the service.

FIT

A good fit if

  • You have an app idea but are not sure how to build it

  • You want an MVP without a long detour

  • You want to improve an existing web or app

  • You are a person, team, or company launching a digital service

  • You want planning through launch in one path
