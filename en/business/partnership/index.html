OVERVIEW

What this collaboration is

A Newon partnership connects what each product and brand already does well. It is not a logo swap. We look for work that creates a real experience for people. That can mean service partnerships, joint campaigns, content, or longer business collaboration.

SCOPE

Partnership types

01

Service partnership

Connect products and features so people get a new experience.

02

Brand partnership

Joint campaigns and content that combine a brand with a Newon service.

03

Content partnership

Content and information that can live in Newon apps and on the web.

04

Long-term partnership

Ongoing product and business work, not a single campaign.

FIT

A good fit if

  • You run a product or service that can connect with Newon

  • You want to collaborate on a digital service

  • You want to plan joint content or a campaign

  • You are looking for a longer-term business partner
