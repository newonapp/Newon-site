PRINCIPLES

Partnership principles

  1. 01No unsecured partner, track record or contract is ever shown.
  2. 02Showing official information is not the same as being an official partner.
  3. 03Care, hotel and grooming businesses are checked before being connected.
  4. 04Insurance partnerships only with properly licensed businesses.
  5. 05Owners' records and personal data are never given to partners without consent.
  6. 06Fees and terms are set only by written agreement after discussion.
