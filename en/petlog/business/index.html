ROADMAP

Step by step,
following real demand.

This is the plan, not current operations; no unconfirmed partnership, revenue or user number is shown.

  1. Stage 1

    Complete PETLOG (top priority)

    First backup and restore, security and app stability, then higher quality for records, health and family care. After that, user growth and validating subscriptions.

  2. Stage 2

    Professional services

    Vet information, demand research for grooming, hotel and daycare booking, and preparing a small booking pilot. Payment connects only after legal, payment-provider contract and security review.

  3. Stage 3

    PETLOG Business

    Validating booking, CRM, customer and pet management SaaS and enterprise solutions in a small pilot, and refining the business screens and tools.

  4. Stage 4

    Commerce and community

    Popular categories, a group-buy marketplace with seller checks, community and short video, and education content.

  5. Stage 5

    Public and global

    Lawful insurance partnerships, public bodies and local government, multilingual localisation and global licensing.

TRUST

Legal and trust principles

  • PETLOG does not diagnose disease or replace a vet's judgment.
  • Vet booking, advertising and brokerage models follow legal review.
  • Insurance solicitation, brokerage and sales only with proper licences and contracts.
  • No trading of live animals, no unverified person-to-person adoption brokering, and no illegal breeding or sales.
  • Provider checks, liability, safety and insurance are built into care services.
  • Only licensed pet funeral providers are listed.
  • Showing official public information is not the same as being an official partner.
  • Owners' records, personal and location data are never shared with businesses or sellers without consent.
  • Sellers are checked before selling, and regulated items such as animal drugs are not sold pending legal review.
  • Booking and payment do not run before legal, payment-provider contract and security review, and are never offered on the web.
