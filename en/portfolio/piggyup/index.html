PiggyUp

Saving habits · Spending analysis · AI savings coach

From spending logs to savings goals and AI insights—record daily spending lightly, spot money leaks, and build saving habits.

View project App Store Google Play

Idea

PiggyUp is

a smart savings-habit app that lets you log today's spending and keep savings goals going—without a heavy ledger.

Instead of making money care harder, we turn small daily entries into a path toward saving.

Log spending to see where money goes. Set savings goals so small steps stack into bigger change.

Key features

Spending log

Record today's amount and category with ease. Check daily spending flow quickly—no heavy input.

Savings goals

Set target amounts and plans, then track progress at a glance. Small savings add up toward your goals.

Savings alerts

Manage repeat spend, risk signals, and log reminders. Stay on top of your spending habits.

Spending analysis

Analyze category patterns and monthly outflow. See where you spend most, easily.

Charts & stats

Visualize savings, spending flow, and goal progress. Understand the numbers at a glance.

AI savings insights

AI analyzes habits and suggests savings points. Build more efficient spending routines.

Goal progress

Track goal completion and growth over time. Make small savings visible as bigger change.
