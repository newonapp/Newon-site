PetLog

Pet logs · Health · Family sharing · AI analysis

A digital lifebook for pets—daily life, health, and memories, built together as a family growth archive.

View project App Store Google Play

Idea

Turn every moment into one life story

PetLog is an all-in-one pet life platform—from daily routines and health to growth and memories—in one place.

Meals and walks, weight and health logs, vaccines and clinic schedules, photos and memories—keep every moment organized.

PetLog is not just a log. It is an AI life partner that builds data over time, helps you understand health changes, and supports better pet care.

Key features

Meal logs

Food, treats, water intake, and nutrition notes.

Walk logs

Walk time, distance, activity, and exercise patterns.

Health records

Vaccines, clinic visits, medications, test results, and health history.

Scheduling

Clinic appointments, vaccination dates, grooming, and checkup schedules.

Statistics

Daily patterns, weight trends, health flow, and activity data—visualized.

Family sharing

Share records with family members and manage care together.

Community

Share tips, swap experiences, Q&A, and pet life stories.

AI health analysis

Routine and activity analysis, health change detection, and AI insights.
