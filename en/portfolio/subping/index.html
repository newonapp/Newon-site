SubPing

Subscriptions · Recurring bills · Payment alerts · Spending insights

From Netflix to phone bills, insurance, rent, and tuition—organize every recurring charge in one place and spend smarter without missing a payment.

View project App Store Google Play

Idea

We repeat dozens of payments every month.

Yet most of us move on without knowing how much we spend , where it goes , or why we keep paying .

SubPing is built to surface hidden spending, help you understand your habits, and cut costs you don’t need.

Managing money isn’t about spending less. It’s about putting more toward the life you want.

Key features

hub

Manage every recurring bill in one place. Netflix, AI tools, phone bills—everything in one hub.

analysis

See how much you spent this month at a glance. Category totals and this month’s flow, organized for you.

reminders

Get notified before upcoming charges. Trial end dates and due dates, ahead of time.

subscriptions

Spot unnecessary spending easily. Find and drop subscriptions you no longer use.

cancel

See how much you could save by canceling. See potential savings the moment you cancel.

category

Streaming, phone, living, insurance, education—see category totals quickly. Streaming, phone, living—totals by category.

widgets

This month’s spend and next payment—without opening the app. This month’s spend and next payment on your home screen.
