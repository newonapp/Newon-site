Newon+

Newon Membership · Unified account · App hub

From productivity and health to parenting, finance, goals, and travel— experience Newon's premium apps with one membership.

View project App Store Google Play

Idea

Newon is

a unified membership platform that connects every Newon app with one account.

No separate subscriptions—one membership covers productivity, health, parenting, finance, pets, goals, travel, and lifestyle apps.

Manage installs, subscription status, premium benefits, family sharing, and usage stats in one place.

Key features

Package subscription

Productivity, wellness, family, and all-in-one packages. Choose only what you need.

Benefits center

New app releases, events, discounts, and membership bonuses.

Unified login

Sign in to every Newon app with one account. No re-signup—use services anytime.

Membership dashboard

See subscription status, period, benefits, savings, and usage.

Family sharing

Share your subscription with family and enjoy premium together. One subscription, more value for everyone.

Backup & sync

Store all records safely. Keep your data when you switch devices.
