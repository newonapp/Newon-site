SAVY

Spending · Income · Subscriptions · AI analysis

A smart finance partner that brings spending, income, and subscriptions together—and helps you understand where money goes and build better spending habits with AI.

View project App Store Google Play

Idea

The easiest way to understand your money flow

SAVY is a smart finance app that lets you log spending, income, and subscriptions without a heavy ledger—and see your money flow at a glance.

Record daily spending, analyze patterns, and get help cutting unnecessary expenses.

The more you log income and spending, the clearer the picture—and clearer records lead to better habits.

Key features

Expense logging

Enter today’s spending quickly and manage it by category.

Income logging

Salary, side income, allowance, business revenue— manage all income in one place.

Subscription management

Recurring bills, streaming, phone, insurance— keep everyday subscriptions together.

Spending analysis

Category share, monthly patterns, recurring spend— spot unnecessary expenses easily.

Charts & statistics

Graphs and charts make money flow easy to grasp.

Smart alerts

Subscription due dates, budget overruns, spending reminders, and goal alerts.

AI spending insights

Habit analysis, spend forecasting, savings opportunities, and tailored insights.
