OX MONTH

Habit tracking · Monthly logs · O/X check

Build habits with a simple O/X check—track your month with logs and stats in one habit app.

View project App Store Google Play

Idea

OX MONTH is

an app to check daily follow-through simply—without heavy logging—and manage habits and goals for the month.

OX MONTH is designed for anyone who wants lightweight, honest daily check-ins. It supports 13 languages—including Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, and more—and is available in 173 countries.

Key features

Today’s check

Log the day with a simple O/X Lightweight habit tracking without tedious input

Monthly habits

See the flow of the month at a glance Spot consistency and patterns intuitively

Monthly goals

Set this month’s goals Track progress together

Habit templates

Start fast with ready-made templates

Statistics
