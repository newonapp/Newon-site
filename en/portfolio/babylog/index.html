BabyLog

Childcare logs · Growth · Family sharing · AI analysis

A digital growth archive—from pregnancy through teens—that helps families build a lasting story from every record.

View project App Store Google Play

Idea

Turn every moment into one growth story

BabyLog is an all-in-one growth platform—from pregnancy through school years and teens—to record and manage your child's daily life in one place.

From feeds and sleep to health, learning, schedules, and memories—keep every moment organized.

BabyLog is not just a log. It is an AI growth partner that builds data over time, helps you understand change, and supports better parenting.

Key features

Parenting logs

Feeds, sleep, diapers, meals, play, and mood—logged with ease.

Child profiles

Growth stage, health info, school details, and core schedules—organized per child.

Growth & health

Height, weight, BMI, milestones, vaccinations, and health history.

Scheduling

Clinic visits, vaccinations, classes, school events, and family plans in one place.

Growth statistics

Growth trends, daily patterns, sleep, and learning changes—visualized clearly.

Family sharing

Share records with parents, grandparents, and caregivers together.

Community

Share experiences, swap tips, ask questions, and tell growth stories.

AI parenting insights

Growth, sleep, and routine pattern analysis— with AI-powered parenting insights.
