FitOn

Workout · Sports · AI analysis · Community

Explore and log running, gym, home workouts, soccer, basketball, tennis, swimming, golf, cycling, yoga, and more—then connect AI activity insights, community, challenges, and sports shopping in one sports & fitness platform.

App Store Google Play

Idea

FitOn is

not an app for only one sport.

Running apps for running, gym apps for the gym, home-workout apps for home training, other apps for soccer, basketball, or tennis, and still other services for community and gear shopping.

FitOn connects those scattered workout experiences.

Key features

Explore every workout

Browse running, gym, home workouts, soccer, basketball, tennis, badminton, swimming, golf, cycling, hiking, yoga, and more. Find what you want to do today, faster.

Workout logging

Manage activity type, time, distance, reps, volume, and personal records. See multiple sports as one flow.

AI workout analysis

Analyze patterns and progress from your logs and goals. Frequency · volume · goal progress · sport mix · consistency · trends · general guidance (not medical diagnosis)

Workout community

Share check-ins, routines, records, sports stories, and tips with others. Running · gym · home · soccer · basketball · tennis · swimming · golf · hiking · free talk

Challenges

Join challenges by distance, session count, streaks, or sport. e.g. 3×/week · 50km/month run · 30-day home workout · weekend hike

Goal tracking

Set personal workout goals and check progress and completion rate. Build steady habits from small goals.

Habits / streaks

Mark workout days and review streak length and habit flow. See consistency at a glance.

Sports shopping

Explore popular apparel, shoes, equipment, and gear. Compare listed prices across linked channels—no lowest-price guarantee.

Scope

  • Product planning
  • UI/UX
  • Development
  • App launch
  • Operations
