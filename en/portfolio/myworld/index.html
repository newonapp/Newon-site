My World

Travel journal · Travel map · AI report

An all-in-one travel journal to log countries and cities, manage itineraries and photos, and explore AI travel reports and stats.

View project App Store Google Play

Idea

My World is

an all-in-one travel platform to record trips and keep memories.

See visited countries and cities on a world map,

and manage itineraries, photos, notes, AI travel reports, and travel stats in one place.

Key features

Travel map

See visited countries and cities on a world map. Visit status by country and city is logged automatically.

Visited countries

Organize countries, cities, and regions systematically. Set travel goals along the way.

Trip itinerary

Manage plans and completed trips in one place. Check everything on a calendar.

Travel journal

Save photos, notes, and travel memos together. Keep memories for the long run.

Travel stats

Countries visited, travel days, flights, distance, continent progress, and more. See your travel data at a glance.

AI travel report

AI analyzes patterns and shares travel style, recommendations, and summaries. Reflect on trips more deeply.

Travel goals

Set countries and travel goals you want to hit this year. Fill goals and complete your world map.
