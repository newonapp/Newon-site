BEFORE YOU CONTACT

Please check
before you write.

  1. 01

    The website does not handle booking, orders, payment or refunds. MY WORLD currently has no bookable products.

  2. 02

    Subscription cancellation and payment history are in your app store's subscription settings.

  3. 03

    For account and data deletion, please check the steps on the account deletion page first.

  4. 04

    For app errors, include your device, app version and the screen where it happened so we can check faster.

  5. 05

    For partnership proposals, see the checklist on the Partnerships page. Review may take some time.

  6. 06

    In an emergency such as a lost passport or an accident, contact local emergency numbers and official bodies such as your embassy or consulate first.

  1. 01

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Review & reply

    We read your message and reply. Partnership inquiries may take some time to review.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

  1. Q1Can I book or buy on the website?

    No. The website introduces the business. Booking, ordering, payment and sign-in are not offered on the web.

  2. Q2When will flight, stay and package booking start?

    We plan to proceed step by step after partnership contracts with eligible businesses and a regulatory review. No timing has been set.

  3. Q3What are the prices or fees?

    Subscription prices and listing or affiliate fees have not been set. We will announce them once confirmed.

  4. Q4How do I delete my account and data?

    The account deletion page explains the steps and how data is handled.

  5. Q5How is it different from SHAREON?

    MY WORLD focuses on travel records, planning and travel-related connections; SHAREON focuses on sharing and renting items, equipment and spaces. The two services are not currently connected.

  6. Q6Can I write in Korean or English?

    Yes. We accept both Korean and English.

This page introduces the MY WORLD business. The website does not offer booking, ordering, payment, sign-in, seller registration or record entry; the actual features are in the MY WORLD app.
