Building warmer days for seniors,
together.

Partnership Vision

No single organization can complete care on its own.

An older adult's day involves family, caregivers, welfare centers, local government, daily services and devices. ONGIL wants to build a structure that connects information on the older adult's consent, so that everyone caring for the same person is not scattered across different apps and phone calls. There are no partnerships, contracts or pilots to announce today; what follows are collaboration models we would like to explore.

Now
No confirmed partnerships or contracts · proposing models and pilots
First focus
Day-care centers · home-care centers · welfare centers · local governments
Approach
Start with a small pilot · expand on results
Principles
Older adult's consent first · no health data in ads · no customer funds held

ONGIL is growing from the senior daily-life features it offers today in its web preview into health and family care, friends and gatherings, care and welfare, safety devices, and enterprise and public-sector solutions. Many of the businesses and features on this page are part of future development or partnership plans; only the web preview features are available today.

PoC & Pilot Proposals

Pilots we would like
to explore together.

Proposals that start small and are judged on real metrics. No pilots are running today; scope, duration and metrics are agreed with each partner.

PoC

Guardian–older adult check-in and daily-life pilot

Day-care centers · home-care centers · local governments

Goal
Connect older adults and distant guardians on consent and see how check-ins and daily management help.
Scope
A small number of consenting households among one institution's users and guardians
Metrics
Family invitation acceptance, weekly check-ins per older adult, fewer guardian enquiries
Preconditions
Separate consent design for sensitive data, app release, data-processing agreement with the institution

PoC

Welfare-center Friends open-gathering pilot

Welfare centers · community centers

Goal
Test participation and return visits through open gatherings with clear organizers.
Scope
A few walking or hobby programs at one welfare center
Metrics
Participation and return rates, reports and time to resolve
Preconditions
Report and block process, no location disclosure, a named organizer

PoC

Employer parent-care benefit pilot

Employers offering family-care benefits

Goal
See whether employees find it easier to get care information and share it with family.
Scope
A small group of volunteer employees
Metrics
Participation, continued use, employee satisfaction
Preconditions
Employee and family data kept apart, personal consent, no individual records shared with the employer

PoC

Certified safety-device integration test

Smart-device · telecom companies

Goal
Check the reliability of linking a software SOS contact list with certified devices.
Scope
A test environment and a few consenting users
Metrics
Alert delivery success, accuracy of battery and connection alerts, false alarms
Preconditions
KC and radio-certified devices, location-related registration and legal review, no rescue guarantees

PoC

Life Box small trial supply

Daily-goods and hobby-kit suppliers · welfare centers

Goal
Check real demand, returns and satisfaction for hobby and daily kits.
Scope
A small group order or gift subscriptions
Metrics
Reorder intent, returns and complaints, unit economics
Preconditions
Product safety checks, withdrawal and refund notices, legal review of payments

Data & API

Data and API
possibilities.

There is no public API or data-sharing contract today. These are directions to explore with partners, all based on the older adult's consent and minimal collection.

  • Receiving public information

    Public data such as welfare schemes and care facilities, shown with official source and update date.

  • Institution workflow links

    Exporting institution SaaS visit schedules and care records to an institution's existing systems.

  • Certified device status

    Only the minimum needed for safety, such as SOS, battery and connection status.

What we will not link

No use of health data for ads or insurance underwriting, no third-party sharing without consent, no unauthorized combining with other apps.

Joint Services & Campaigns

Joint services
and campaigns.

Not advertising, but joint activities that actually help older adults and families.

  • Digital-life classes

    Easy smartphone and app classes with welfare centers and lifelong-learning institutions.

  • Phone and financial scam prevention

    Scam-prevention guidance based on official materials, and family conversation campaigns.

  • Neighbourhood walking groups

    Regular walks linking local parks and welfare centers (linked to the Friends plan).

  • Guardian check-in week

    A family campaign encouraging children living away to check in on their parents.

Process

How partnerships work

  1. 01

    Proposal

    Send your area of cooperation and organization details through the business inquiry form or email.

  2. 02

    Fit review

    We check qualifications, operating conditions and user-protection standards. Early suppliers are limited to verified businesses.

  3. 03

    Scope

    We agree roles and the scope of information processing. NEWON does not hold customer funds.

  4. 04

    Terms and contract

    We review revenue structure and contracts such as data-processing agreements. Health data is never used for ads.

  5. 05

    Pilot

    We start small and refine with user feedback and metrics. Anything is announced only with both parties' consent.

Partnership principles

  • Older adults' dignity and choice
  • Protecting personal and health data
  • Consent-based family connection
  • Verifying qualified care and medical providers
  • Transparency about actual partnerships
  • Consumer protection
  • Accessibility and inclusion

We do not claim false partnerships or results. Medical institutions, care institutions, local governments or companies are never shown as partners without a formal agreement, and confirmed partnerships are announced only with consent.

Expected Value

What partners
can expect.

  • Institutions

    Tools that reduce repetitive guardian contact and record-keeping, and build trust with families.

  • Local government and public sector

    Easier delivery of official information to local older adults, and digital program management.

  • Companies

    Reaching senior and family customers, and distinctive employee benefits.

  • Older adults and families

    A daily life where scattered information and contacts come together. This is the final test for every partnership.
