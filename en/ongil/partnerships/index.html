Why Partner

Why work with ONGIL?

  1. 01

    A touchpoint older adults can use

    On screens built around large type and plain language, your service reaches the older adults and families who need it with accurate information.

  2. 02

    Trust that extends to family

    On top of consent-based family connection, the same information reaches the family members who help choose services.

  3. 03

    Information, not advertising

    We separate official information from ads and never choose ads using health data. Being presented honestly is itself a source of trust for partners.

  4. 04

    Partnerships that start small

    We start with small pilots and refine them with user feedback. We do not promise aggressive expansion.

Partnership Principles

The same principles for every partnership.

  1. ✓01

    The first suppliers are verified businesses.

  2. ✓02

    No false partnerships or results; a partnership is announced only with the partner's consent.

  3. ✓03

    NEWON does not hold customers' money.

  4. ✓04

    Health information is never used for ad targeting or partner marketing.

  5. ✓05

    No personal data goes to partners without the older adult's own consent.

  6. ✓06

    Official evaluation and designation information is never mixed with ads.

  7. ✓07

    No brokerage income from long-term care insurance services.

We never show false partnerships or results. A confirmed partnership is announced only with the partner's consent.

What we check before partnering

When we receive a proposal, we review the items below together. Some fields may need additional documents.

ItemWhat we check
Qualifications & registrationBusiness registration, institutional designation, worker qualifications required in the field
User protectionCancellation and refunds, complaint handling, liability and insurance
Personal dataScope of processing, processing agreements, retention and deletion
Labelling & adsExaggerated claims about effects or results; how ads are labelled
Regulatory fitWhether medical, long-term care, transport or job-placement law applies
Operating capacitySupport channels, service area, experience serving older adults

Process

Partnership process

  1. 01

    Proposal

    Send your field of cooperation and organisation details by email or the inquiry form.

  2. 02

    Review

    We check qualifications, operating conditions and user-protection standards.

  3. 03

    Discussion

    We agree roles, data handling and the partnership structure together.

  4. 04

    Agreement

    Necessary agreements, such as data processing, are signed in writing.

  5. 05

    Pilot

    We start small and refine with user feedback.

Helpful to include with a proposal

  • About your organisation and service area
  • The field and form of cooperation you have in mind
  • Qualifications, registrations and designations
  • How you protect users and handle personal data
  • Contact person

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q01Can we list our service or join right now?

No. The ONGIL app is being prepared for its first release, and business connections are under review. Leave a proposal and we will contact you when the stage is right.

Q02What are the fees or charges?

No prices or fees are set. Each partnership structure is decided after review and discussion by field, and no figures are published on this website.

Q03Does ONGIL collect payments and settle them?

As a principle, NEWON does not hold customers' money. Payment and settlement are not decided.

Q04Will our partnership be announced?

Only once it is confirmed and you agree. Partnerships under review are not announced.

Q05Can individual care providers make a proposal?

Yes, though qualifications and registration are checked by field, and any connection model for workers is set only after checking job-placement rules.
