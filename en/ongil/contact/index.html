Privacy & Safety

Before you
get in touch.

  1. 01

    Please do not include health information or national ID numbers in your inquiry. Personal information is used only to answer your inquiry, and the website takes no sign-ins, bookings or payments.

  2. 02

    Emergencies cannot be handled through inquiries. Please call your local emergency number (119 in Korea).

AppThe ONGIL app for iOS and Android is being prepared for its first release in Korea. 'Available' labels on this page refer to the web preview, which needs no sign-in and stores data on this device only.

SafetyONGIL does not diagnose, prescribe or treat, and does not respond to emergencies. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (119 in Korea) first.
