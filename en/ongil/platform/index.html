Design Rules

The same rules
across all 18 areas.

However many areas there are, using them should feel the same. These are ONGIL's shared design rules.

  1. 01

    Large type by default

    Three text sizes, high contrast and buttons that are easy to press.

  2. 02

    Plain language

    Jargon is spelled out; terms that must stay come with an explanation.

  3. 03

    Records without judgement

    Numbers and notes are kept as entered, never scored.

  4. 04

    Sharing starts switched off

    The person turns on each item they share with family.

  5. 05

    Official sources named

    Welfare, care and medical information names its source and links to the official body.

  6. 06

    Ads kept apart

    If ads ever appear they are never mixed with information, and never chosen using health data.
