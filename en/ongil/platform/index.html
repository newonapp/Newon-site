Area 25 · ONGIL Global

Services to take abroad
and how each country shapes them.

Area 25 ONGIL Global, broken down into the services it would expand. No service is offered outside Korea, and no target country or timeline has been set; the first app release and validation in Korea come first.

  • Ageing markets first

    Countries with fast ageing and strong family-care needs are reviewed first, opening with the core daily tools (health, medication and day records) adapted to how older adults live there.

  • Local care services

    Collaboration models with local care organizations, care homes and home-care providers would be built around each country's care system.

  • Families across borders

    Families living in different regions or countries could share an older adult's daily-life and care information safely, only as far as they allow. Sharing stays off by default and consent can always be withdrawn.

  • Health and safety, localized

    Health records, daily support, safety alerts and emergency-contact arrangements are reviewed against each country's systems and infrastructure.

  • Senior community and social impact

    Friends, hobbies and leisure, community participation and support for older adults in hardship would be connected with local organizations.

Localization by country

  • Language and easy screens

    Plain sentences, large type and high contrast in each language, with dates, units and forms of address adapted locally.

  • Care culture

    Defaults for family connection and community features reflect how families share care and how people meet in each country.

  • Privacy and cross-border transfer

    Each country's privacy law, sensitive-data rules and cross-border transfer consent are checked before deciding where data is stored.

  • Care and welfare systems

    Public care and welfare information is shown only from local official sources; Korean schemes are never copied across.

  • Payments and subscriptions

    No paid feature opens before local payment methods, currency, tax and auto-renewal notice rules are checked.

  • Local partners

    Work with local organizations and companies starts with a small pilot, after eligibility and operating conditions are confirmed.

Medical, location and emergency rules, per country

Rules on medical services, health data, location data and emergency response differ by country and are reviewed country by country. Emergency guidance follows each country's official emergency numbers; ONGIL does not replace emergency response.

Area 28 · New Signature

Safe Band —
consent-based location and SOS

A smart safety wristband concept: press a button to ask for help, and guardians can check location within what the older adult has agreed to. It is not a released product and is under review. The basic SOS contact list and safety settings come first in software; the wristband, connectivity and location are decided separately after certification, security and connectivity checks.

28ONGIL Safe Band

Features under review

SOS button
Press to ask the registered contacts for help.
Guardian alerts
Notifications to registered guardians.
Location check
Only within the purpose and period the older adult agreed to.
Safe-zone alerts
Notice when leaving a set area.
Battery and connection alerts
Low battery or lost connection notices.
Emergency contact
Helps reach guardians and official emergency services (119, 112).
Fall detection and voice calls
Reviewed only after technology, performance and connectivity are verified.

Safety operating principles

  • Respecting the older adult's decisions and consent
  • No unlimited tracking for family
  • Limited purpose, period and viewers for location
  • Access log kept
  • Institution monitoring only under separate contracts and verified permissions

Because of GPS error, network failures and flat batteries, Safe Band is never presented as guaranteeing prevention of going missing or of accidents, or rescue. Location-information law, KC and radio certification are checked first.

By User Type

How each user type
uses ONGIL.

Within one platform, each role sees and does different things. Institution manager and seller accounts are extended with separate work permissions.

  • Older adults

    See today's schedule, health, check-ins and emergency contacts at home, keep daily records and find friends, gatherings and welfare information. They decide every share.

  • Guardians and family

    View the records and schedules the older adult allows and send check-ins. Sharing care, booking services and gift subscriptions are planned.

  • Caregivers

    Tools are planned to manage assigned schedules, visit tasks, care records and institution communication, sharing only permitted information with guardians.

  • Institution managers and sellers

    Separate work accounts for institution information, staff schedules and product information. They never get automatic access to older adults' personal data.

How It Connects

Five common systems
linking the 29 areas.

The 29 areas are not separate apps; they share the systems below. A senior app needs simple, large screens, so the areas are found within a few screens rather than laid out as dozens of menus.

  • Profile, consent and permissions

    Senior account, family and caregiver links, per-item sharing and consent history. Every area follows these permissions.

  • Records and schedules

    Health readings, medication, daily life, appointments and care records share one record structure.

  • Safety and contacts

    One emergency contact list serves the home screen, Safe Band and device integrations. The emergency button stays within reach on main screens.

  • Discovery and connection

    Friends, gatherings, events, welfare, institutions and market information in one discovery experience.

  • Subscriptions, devices, institution accounts

    Personal and family subscriptions, Life Box, device management and institution SaaS follow the same account and payment rules. Payments stay off until legal review.

Privacy · Security · Trust

Privacy, security
and trust principles.

Health, location and care data are the most sensitive information there is. These principles come before any feature.

  • Separate consent for sensitive data

    Health data needs separate consent, with limited access and retention.

  • No automatic access

    No structure in which guardians or institutions automatically see all of an older adult's data.

  • Lawful representation

    Guardian links only with the person's consent or lawful authority to act for them.

  • Minimal location data

    Limited purpose, period and viewers, with an access log. Location-related registration and legal review come first.

  • Separated from advertising

    Health data is never used for ad targeting, and ads or commercial recommendations are labelled apart from care and health information.

  • No combining across apps

    Health, location and care data are never combined with other Newon apps without explicit consent.

  • Limited role for AI

    AI does not diagnose, adjust medication or judge emergencies, and says honestly what it cannot do.

Emergencies go to official numbers

ONGIL is not an emergency service. In Korea: 119 for emergencies, 112 for missing persons or crime, 129 for welfare counselling, 109 for emotional support.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use ONGIL now?

Not yet. The iOS and Android app is being prepared for its first release.

Is ONGIL a caregiver-matching service?

No. ONGIL is a comprehensive senior platform connecting health and daily records, family care, friends and gatherings, welfare information and safety. Finding care providers is one part of it, and it opens step by step with verified institutions.

Can family see all of my parent's records?

No. Family see only the items the older adult allows. The default is 'not shared', and health records and location are never shared automatically.

Does the AI judge my health?

No. The AI helps with schedules and organizing records. It does not diagnose, adjust medication or judge emergencies; please consult a medical professional.

Does ONGIL help in an emergency?

ONGIL is not an emergency service. In Korea, call 119 for emergencies and 112 for missing persons or crime. Welfare counselling is at 129 and emotional-support counselling at 109.

Can I buy a Safe Band or a Life Box?

Not yet. Both are business concepts, not released products, and prices and contents are not set.

Does ONGIL cost anything?

There are no paid plans or products today. SOS and emergency contacts, finding friends and welfare information are planned to stay free.

Is my health data used for advertising?

No. Health data is never used for ad targeting and is not combined with other Newon apps without explicit consent.

Can I donate through ONGIL?

Not yet. Giving is a planned way to help older adults in need together with welfare institutions and communities. No donations are collected today.
