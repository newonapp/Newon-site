Areas 01–06

Daily life and health,
family and care

Records that carry the day, healthy living, family connection, care at home, care institutions and the day of a hospital visit.

01Daily Life

Daily routines, made easy

See today's to-dos, schedule and routines at a glance in large text. From the morning check-in to the evening memo, the day flows on one screen. Used in the web preview with no sign-in; records are stored on this device only.

Services

  • Daily check-in (mood and how you feel)
  • Calendar
  • Repeating routines with completion marks
  • To-do list
  • Memos
  • Living-expense notes (no advice or judgement)
  • Sync across devices (in preparation)
For
Older adults who manage their own day
Contact us →Back to index ↑

02Health & Wellness

Records and information for healthy living

Write down medication times, measurements such as blood pressure and glucose, and hospital dates (web preview). Records are easy to show at a hospital visit. Numbers are not judged or diagnosed; medical care comes from medical institutions.

Services

  • Medication list and taken marks
  • Weight, blood pressure, glucose and pulse you enter
  • Hospital and health-screening dates
  • Sleep notes
  • Symptom notes for hospital visits
  • General healthy-living and exercise information (in preparation)
For
Older adults and families keeping healthy habits

No medical advice, diagnosis or prescription. Records are kept exactly as entered, and health information is never used for ad targeting.

Contact us →Back to index ↑

03Family Connection

Family connection on your terms

Choose item by item what to share with family. The default is 'not shared', and you can preview what family would see before switching anything on. Sharing choices and the preview are in the web preview.

Services

  • Per-item sharing choices
  • Preview of what family would see
  • Help-request notes
  • Family invitation and connection (in preparation)
  • Shared schedules (in preparation)
  • Ending a connection, withdrawing consent (in preparation)
For
Older adults and family living apart

Health records and location are never shared automatically. Connection between family devices is being prepared with accounts.

Contact us →Back to index ↑

04Home Care

Understanding care at home

Plain-language guidance on home care types such as home visits, bathing and nursing visits, and how to use them. Long-term care benefits and non-covered daily help are explained separately. ONGIL does not provide or broker care services.

Services

  • Guide to home-care types
  • General long-term care process (linked to official sources)
  • How non-covered daily-help services differ
  • Local provider search
  • Consultation connection (after partner agreements)
For
Older adults needing care at home, and families

Long-term care benefits are used through NHIS grading and designated providers.

Propose a partnership →Back to index ↑

05Care Facilities

Find care institutions nearby

Choose a region and type to find nearby care and welfare facilities (web preview). Check contacts and locations, and save facilities to compare with family. Please confirm evaluations and accreditation with official sources.

Services

  • Search by region and type
  • Day care vs. care facilities explained
  • Contact and location
  • Save facilities
  • What to compare
  • Visit-consultation booking (in preparation)
For
Families and older adults exploring facilities
Contact us →Back to index ↑

06Hospital Companion

Not alone on hospital days

We are preparing a way to find and connect hospital escort and mobility services. It is designed around public transport and taxis, and involves no medical acts or medical judgement. Initial partners are planned to be verified businesses only.

Services

  • Hospital dates (web preview)
  • Escort service information
  • Mobility services for people with limited mobility
  • Escort provider connection (after partner agreements)
  • Sharing dates with family
For
Older adults who find hospital trips hard alone, and families

Escort staff qualifications, insurance and operations are verified before any partner agreement.

Areas 07–12

Daily needs and leisure,
learning and welfare

Meals and daily goods, things to enjoy nearby, learning, neighbors and welfare information. Public welfare information is, in principle, free.

07Food & Daily Needs

Meals and everyday convenience

An area we plan to grow into: helping people find meal support, grocery shopping, housework and laundry services. Public information such as local senior meal programs comes from official sources; private services start only after agreements with verified businesses.

  • Meal and lunch-box information
  • Grocery and essentials delivery information
  • Housework, cleaning and laundry services
  • Local free-meal programs (official sources)
  • Daily-service partner connection (after agreements)
For
Older adults who need help with meals and housework
Propose a partnership →Back to index ↑

08Senior Shopping

Daily goods that fit

We are preparing a shopping area that makes mobility, safety and convenience products easy to choose. Initial sellers are planned to be verified businesses only. No products are on sale today, and no payments are taken on the web.

  • Convenience products
  • Mobility and safety products
  • Large-text product descriptions
  • What to check before buying
  • Verified brands (after agreements)
For
Older adults, and families looking for gifts

Products covered by welfare-equipment benefits are distinguished from general goods. No exaggerated claims.

Propose a partnership →Back to index ↑

09Leisure & Culture

Things to enjoy nearby

Explore public information such as tourism and nearby places by region (web preview). Save places you like and sort them by name or date to look at again.

  • Local tourism and culture information
  • Nearby places
  • Save what interests you
  • Sort alphabetically or by date
  • Culture program partnerships (in preparation)
For
Older adults who enjoy outings and leisure

Travel records and memories on a map belong to Newon's MY WORLD; ONGIL focuses on leisure and culture information nearby.

Contact us →Back to index ↑

10Learning

Learning that continues

Find local lifelong-learning classes and save the ones you like (web preview). Programs for smartphones, digital life and fraud prevention are in preparation.

  • Lifelong-learning class information
  • Classes by region
  • Save classes
  • Digital-life education (in preparation)
  • Fraud-prevention guidance (in preparation)
For
Older adults who want to keep learning
Contact us →Back to index ↑

11Community

Neighbors through shared interests

Posts and group plans can be kept as drafts on this device (web preview). A shared community opens only after user-protection measures such as reporting, blocking and fraud response are ready.

  • Draft posts (this device)
  • Group plan drafts
  • Meetup schedule drafts
  • Public community (in preparation)
  • Reporting and blocking (in preparation)
For
Older adults who want to share interests with neighbors

Nothing is published to other people today.

Contact us →Back to index ↑

12Welfare Information

Not missing the support you can get

We are preparing plain-language guidance on government and local welfare, linked to official sources. Public welfare information is planned to be free; eligibility and applications are handled by official institutions.

  • Official welfare information
  • Support by region
  • Application routes and contacts
  • Help organizing documents
  • Save benefits of interest
For
Older adults and families exploring welfare benefits

We do not confirm eligibility. Please check with the official institution.

Contact us →Back to index ↑

Areas 13–18

Safety and participation,
care tools and access

Safety in a familiar home, work and participation, tools for families and caregivers, and an app anyone can use.

13Housing & Safety

Safe in a familiar home

Keep a list of people to call quickly on this device (web preview). Home safety checks and housing improvement information are in preparation. ONGIL does not respond to emergencies.

  • Emergency contact list (this device)
  • Home safety checklist (in preparation)
  • Home improvement services (in preparation)
  • Local housing support (in preparation)
  • Phone and financial scam prevention (in preparation)
For
Older adults living alone, and families

In an emergency, call 119 first. The contact list never calls anyone automatically.

Contact us →Back to index ↑

14Jobs & Participation

Staying connected through work and activity

An area we plan to grow into: helping people find senior jobs, volunteering and talent-sharing that keep them connected to society. We point to official program information and do not act as a job placement service.

  • Senior job information (official sources)
  • Volunteering information
  • Talent sharing and intergenerational programs
  • Local activity news
  • Participating institutions (after agreements)
For
Older adults who want to keep working and taking part
Propose a partnership →Back to index ↑

15Family Care Tools

Sharing family care

We are planning tools for several family members to share care schedules and roles. This follows the family connection feature and puts the older adult's wishes first. A family subscription is under review as a paid candidate.

  • Shared care calendar
  • Dividing roles
  • Visit and call notes
  • Organizing care information
  • Communication notes with institutions
For
Families caring for a parent together
Contact us →Back to index ↑

16AI Assistance

A helper you can ask in plain words

The web preview has a helper that handles a fixed set of requests, such as 'add a hospital appointment tomorrow'. It says honestly when it cannot help. Connecting a language model (AI) and voice input are under review.

  • Fixed requests (add schedules and records)
  • List of what it can do
  • Honest answers when it cannot help
  • Conversational search (under review)
  • Voice input (under review)
For
Older adults who find screens hard to use

It does not make medical or legal judgements or important decisions. Health information is never used for advertising.

Contact us →Back to index ↑

17Care Provider Tools

Tools for care providers

We are planning scheduling, record and family-communication tools for home caregivers and small providers. Care records are protected as personal data; terms are to be agreed. Institution, enterprise and public-sector solutions are introduced on the Business page.

  • Visit schedules
  • Care records
  • Communication with guardians
  • Training and qualification records
  • Work reports
For
Care workers, daily-help staff and small care providers
Propose a partnership →Back to index ↑

18App & Accessibility

An app anyone can use

Large text, high contrast and clear buttons by default. The web preview is running; the iOS and Android app, with five tabs (Home, Health, Care, Connect, My), is being prepared for its first release in Korea.

  • Three text sizes
  • Keyboard and screen-reader support
  • Page titles announced on view change
  • Web preview (no sign-in)
  • iOS and Android app (Korea release in preparation)
  • Voice guidance (under review)
For
Everyone
