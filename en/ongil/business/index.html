ONGIL is growing from the senior daily-life features it offers today in its web preview into health information, family connection, care and welfare, and enterprise and public-sector solutions. Some of the businesses and features introduced here are part of future development or partnership plans.

Business Model

Business models
and revenue opportunities

B2C (individual older adults) · B2B (care and senior daily-life providers) · B2G (local governments, welfare and public institutions) · Enterprise SaaS (standardized solutions for institutions and companies) · Global (overseas platforms and licensing). Family members and guardians are a user segment alongside older adults, not a separate transaction category.

The revenue items below are candidates under internal review; no prices, fees or contracts have been set. Revenue from Korean long-term care insurance benefit services (NHIS fees and co-payments) and from general non-covered services are reviewed as separate structures; ONGIL takes no brokerage revenue from benefit services.

  1. 01

    Personal premium subscription

  2. 02

    Add-on services and subscription for families

  3. 03

    Commission on daily goods (verified sellers only)

  4. 04

    Partnerships with lawful non-covered care and daily services

  5. 05

    SaaS for care and welfare institutions (agency Pro)

  6. 06

    Operating tools for care providers

  7. 07

    Education and leisure program partnerships

  8. 08

    Advertising (no targeting based on health data)

  9. 09

    Enterprise senior solutions and employee family-care benefits

  10. 10

    Welfare and public-sector contracts

  11. 11

    Global SaaS and licensing

Essential safety information and public welfare information stay free. Health information is never used for ad targeting, and NEWON never holds customer funds. Prices, payment and settlement have not been decided.

Business Roadmap

Growing
in five steps.

No release dates or revenue targets have been set. Each step follows only after the previous one is stable and users are protected.

  1. 01

    A daily-life information base

    Preparing the first app release in Korea, building on the web preview's daily and health records and information search.

  2. 02

    Family connection & wellbeing

    Widening consent-based family connection and healthy-living information.

  3. 03

    Care, welfare & community

    Connecting care, welfare and community services through verified businesses and official sources.

  4. 04

    Enterprise, institution & public solutions

    Widening provider and institution SaaS and enterprise and public solutions through agreements.

  5. 05

    Global expansion

    Considering overseas platforms and licensing after reviewing each country's systems and regulation.
