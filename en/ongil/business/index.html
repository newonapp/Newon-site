Revenue Model

Revenue models under review (hypotheses)

Revenue models shown are internal hypotheses. Prices and fees are not confirmed and are not public prices. Revenue from Korean long-term care insurance benefit services (NHIS fees and co-payments) and from general non-covered services are reviewed as separate structures; ONGIL takes no brokerage revenue from benefit services.
