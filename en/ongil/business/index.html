How It Works

ONGIL handles information and connection —
not customers' money.

ONGIL organises information and makes connections between users and service providers. As a principle, payment for services is not held by ONGIL.

Revenue Opportunities

Revenue comes after trust.

Candidates under review · no prices set

The revenue candidates ONGIL is reviewing are family, business and institution subscriptions, transaction commissions on services, and advertising. Senior care and daily-living services meet steady needs, but we believe no model can last without the trust of older adults and their families.

That is why no prices or commission rates are set, and payment and settlement are not decided. Each model is adopted only after user-protection review, legal review and discussion with partners — and some candidates may not be adopted at all.

  • No prices, fees or commission rates are published; none are set.
  • No brokerage income is taken from long-term care insurance services (official fees and co-payments).
  • As a principle, NEWON does not hold customers' money.
  • Health information is never used for ad targeting.

Free, regardless of revenue

  • Essential safety information and official emergency channels
  • Public welfare information and how to apply
  • Types of care and long-term care procedures
  • Basic daily records and accessibility features such as text size

Regulatory Guardrails

In regulated fields,
standards come first.

Care, health, transport and commerce are governed by law. We check these standards before starting any business. This table is general guidance and not legal advice.

Regulatory Guardrails
FieldStandard to checkONGIL's rule
Long-term careLong-Term Care Insurance Act · assessment and designated providersInsured services are only explained via official procedures; no brokerage income.
Medical & healthMedical Service Act · medical acts, patient inducement and brokerageNo diagnosis, prescription or judgement; no hospital promotion or brokerage.
Hospital escort & mobilityPassenger Transport Service Act · paid transportDesigned around public transit and taxis; the operating model is reviewed separately.
Connecting care workersEmployment Security Act · job placementThe connection model is set only after checking whether it counts as placement.
Commerce & adsE-Commerce Act · Labeling and Advertising ActNo exaggerated claims; ads are labelled as ads.
Privacy & locationPersonal Information Protection Act · Location Information ActSeparate consent for sensitive data, sharing off by default, withdraw any time.
