About ONGIL

Growing older, life's possibilities continue.

Starting a new hobby, sharing news with family, looking after healthy days, meeting new people in the community. Growing older is not life coming to a stop but the making of another kind of everyday life. ONGIL aims to offer the information and connections that let older adults carry on at their own pace and in their own way.

Now
Web preview · no sign-in, stored on this device only
Next
iOS and Android app being prepared for first release in Korea
Together
Older adults, families, care providers, welfare institutions, companies and local governments
Principle
The person's own choice and consent first · health data never used for ads

ONGIL is growing from the senior daily-life features it offers today in its web preview into health information, family connection, care and welfare, and enterprise and public-sector solutions. Some of the businesses and features introduced here are part of future development or partnership plans.

02Our Mission

For days that are more comfortable,
more active and more connected.

ONGIL does not see older adults only as people to be cared for. We respect them as people who choose, act and carry on their own lives.

  1. 01

    Independence

    Managing one's own schedule, medication and records is the starting point. We help people keep doing what they can for longer.

  2. 02

    Connection

    Connections with family, neighbors and the community, as far as the person chooses. What is shared is set item by item and can be withdrawn at any time.

  3. 03

    Wellbeing

    Help to keep up habits, activity and health dates — everyday records and general information, not diagnosis or judgement.

  4. 04

    Safety

    Keep the people to call ready and look out for everyday risks. Essential safety information is free.

  5. 05

    Care

    When help is needed, find trustworthy care and welfare information and official routes. ONGIL does not provide care itself.

  6. 06

    Dignity

    Important choices are always put to the person first, in large text and plain words, so anyone can use it on their own.

03The Senior Life Journey

From today's routines
to the life ahead.

Not every older adult has the same needs. We do not sort people by age or health; eight areas let each person start where they need to.

  1. 01

    Daily life

    See check-ins, schedules, routines, to-dos and memos at a glance in large text. Available in the web preview, with no sign-in and storage on this device.

  2. 02

    Health & wellness

    Record medication, measurements, hospital dates and sleep yourself (web preview). Records are not judged or diagnosed; healthy-living information is an area we will widen.

  3. 03

    Family connection

    Share only the items you choose with family. Sharing choices and a preview are in the web preview; connection between family devices is in preparation.

  4. 04

    Safety & support

    Keep a list of people to call on this device. Home safety and scam-prevention information is in preparation, and ONGIL does not respond to emergencies.

  5. 05

    Care & welfare

    Plain-language guidance on care types and official routes, and a local care-facility search. Welfare guidance linked to official sources is in preparation.

  6. 06

    Learning & leisure

    Find and save public information on lifelong-learning classes, tourism and culture by region. Travel memories belong to Newon's MY WORLD.

  7. 07

    Community & participation

    Posts and group plans can only be kept as drafts on this device for now. A public community and job and volunteering information open step by step once protections are in place.

  8. 08

    Later-life planning

    The direction is to help people look ahead at the daily-life and care information later life may need. Retirement and life-stage information is LivOn's role; any link is considered only with the person's consent.

04Core Experience

Everyday experiences,
made easier.

Finding information, managing schedules, family communication, safety, care and welfare information, leisure, learning and community. We build on what the web preview offers today and widen it in the app.

  1. 01

    Schedules & daily management

    Check-ins, calendar, routines, to-dos and expense notes on one screen. Available in the web preview.

  2. 02

    Health records & general information

    Medication, measurements and hospital dates kept exactly as entered, organized to show at a hospital visit.

  3. 03

    Family communication

    Switch on shared items one by one and preview what family would see. Cross-device connection is in preparation.

  4. 04

    Safety, care & welfare information

    An emergency contact list, care-type guidance and a local facility search. Welfare information links are in preparation.

  5. 05

    Leisure, learning & community

    Find and save classes, tourism and places. The local community opens once user protections are ready.

05For Seniors & Families

Connecting seniors' daily life
with their families' care.

Older adults and their family or guardians have different permissions. The older adult decides what is shared and with whom.

  1. 01For seniors

    Find information easily, manage your own life, and keep up new experiences and social connections. Every decision about records and sharing is yours.

  2. 02For families

    Share family news, understand a parent's daily life and look at support information together. Family see only the items the person allows.

  3. 03Consent and separate permissions

    The default is 'not shared'. Health records and location are never shared automatically; a preview shows what family would see first, and a connection can be ended at any time.

06Beyond Senior Life

From seniors' daily life
to a wider care and welfare ecosystem.

Building on the daily experience of individuals and families, ONGIL aims to grow into a senior life ecosystem connected with care providers, welfare institutions, companies and local governments. It is not a care provider itself, but a platform that connects people with the right information and institutions.

  1. 01

    Care providers

    Accurate information about qualified providers such as home care and daily help, with scheduling, record and guardian-communication tools under review.

  2. 02

    Welfare institutions

    Easier access to information and programs from day-care centers, care facilities and welfare centers. Public welfare information is, in principle, free.

  3. 03

    Daily services & companies

    Connecting everyday services such as meals, goods, mobility and education, and companies' senior solutions. Initial suppliers are planned to be verified businesses only.

  4. 04

    Local & public sector

    Solutions for older residents' information access and digital inclusion, reviewed together. No official public-sector partnership has been confirmed yet.

Long-term care insurance benefits in Korea are used through the National Health Insurance Service grading and designated providers. ONGIL does not broker benefit services or collect co-payments, and NEWON never holds customer funds.

07Trust & Responsibility

The more precious daily life is,
the more trust matters.

Seven commitments ONGIL keeps.

  1. 01

    Privacy · Records in the web preview are stored on this device with no sign-in. Health information is never used for ad targeting.

  2. 02

    Accurate health information · Health information is general information only, and records are kept exactly as entered, without judgement. Diagnosis, prescription and treatment come from medical institutions.

  3. 03

    Choice and consent · The person decides what to record and with whom to share it. Withdrawing consent is designed to be easy.

  4. 04

    Separate family and guardian permissions · Family see only the items the person allows. Location sharing is considered only with separate consent.

  5. 05

    Qualified care and medical institutions · Designation, evaluation and qualification information is checked against official sources. Institutions without a confirmed partnership are never shown as partners.

  6. 06

    Limits of emergency features · ONGIL does not respond to emergencies, and the contact list never calls anyone automatically. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (119 in Korea) first.

  7. 07

    Dignity and autonomy · Words and screens that respect a self-chosen life — not worry or pity.

08NEWON Ecosystem

ONGIL's place
within Newon.

ONGIL is an independent Newon business focused on daily life and care connections in later life. Each service runs on its own; connections are considered only with the person's consent.

  1. 01LivOnA life-stage information platform. Life-stage information such as retirement planning is LivOn's role; we are reviewing how it could continue into ONGIL with the person's consent.
  2. 02PETLOGRecords of daily life with a companion animal, for older adults who live with pets.
  3. 03MY WORLDAn app for keeping memories of travel and places. Travel records belong to MY WORLD; nearby leisure and culture information belongs to ONGIL.
  4. 04Newon BusinessAutomation and digital tools for institutions and companies, including workflow questions from care and welfare institutions.
