Why Ongil

When daily life needs a hand,
Ongil is there.

As people grow older, the information and services daily life needs can change.

Help with health, getting around, meals, living at home, and local activities can mean searching in many separate places.

Families can also find it hard to know which services are needed, and where to look.

Ongil aims to make it easier to browse and connect that information and those services in one living platform.

Core Services

The daily life Ongil connects

From living support to health and care, family, and the local community. Helping people find the service they need, when they need it.

01

Daily living support

Browse and connect help for meals, getting around, housework, and everyday convenience.

  • Find meal and living-convenience services
  • Guidance on getting around and going out
  • Connect housework and daily-living support
  • Living information that fits the person’s situation

02

Health and care

Check health-related information, and browse and connect the care services that are needed.

  • Health and daily-management information
  • Browse care services
  • Information on specialist organizations and providers
  • Connect care that fits the person’s situation

Ongil does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. We are preparing around health information and care connections.

03

Family connection

Help seniors and families share the information they need and stay in touch.

  • Family conversation
  • Share living information that is needed
  • See care-related dates and notes
  • Browse services together as a family

The senior’s consent and choice come first. Health and living information is not shared with family automatically.

04

Local life

Browse living information, activities, and services around where people live.

  • Local welfare and living-service information
  • Culture, leisure, and hobby activities
  • Local community and program guides
  • Nearby living-support services

We plan to introduce tailored guidance step by step, so information that fits each person’s situation is easier to find.

How it works

An easier way
to find the help you need

Planned journey

This is a planned journey, not a live apply-and-book flow.

01

Choose the help you need

Pick daily living, health and care, family, or local life.

02

Browse information and services

See information and services that fit the area you chose and the person’s situation.

03

Review and connect

The aim is to review what a service offers and how it works, then connect to the help that is needed.

Business expansion

Start with daily life,
then a wider connection.

A future expansion plan, not a claim of current operations.

Unconfirmed partners, revenue, user counts, and service areas are not shown.

  1. 01
    Find senior living information and services

    Build a base so seniors and families can find the living information and services they need in one place.

  2. 02
    Widen local services and specialist links

    We plan to expand connections with local living-support services, care providers, and specialist organizations step by step.

  3. 03
    Family links and more personal guidance

    With the senior’s choice and consent, grow family conversation and information sharing, and make service browsing fit each person’s needs more closely.

  4. 04
    A full senior living platform

    The aim is to grow into a platform that connects daily living, care, local activity, and living services.

ONGIL VISION

A more comfortable day,
a steadier connection.

Ongil is widening the link between living and care so seniors can keep leading their own days, and families can be there when it matters.
