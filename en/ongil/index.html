Ongil

Warm connection,
through the years of daily life.

Daily life, health, and care — connected with family and community. A full living platform for a more comfortable, self-directed life.

Explore Ongil → Business inquiry

Back to Ongil on the homepage

Why Ongil

When daily life needs a hand,
Ongil is there.

  1. 01

    As people grow older, help with health, getting around, meals, housing, and leisure can mean searching in many separate places.

  2. 02

    Ongil does not treat seniors as people to be managed. From independent living to ongoing care, the aim is to respect choice and autonomy.

  3. 03

    As life changes, the services that are needed should still connect on one platform.

Who Ongil is for

Across different living situations,
the links that are needed.

Not a service for one age or one health state. As life changes, people should be able to choose the areas they need on the same Ongil.

01

Seniors living independently

People exploring a healthy day, hobbies, leisure, new learning, local activity, and everyday convenience.

02

Seniors who need some daily help

People exploring living support for meals, getting around, housework, and going out.

03

Seniors looking for health and care information

People exploring healthy-living information and, when needed, care and specialist organizations.

04

Families looking after a parent’s days

Families who want to browse living information and services together, while respecting the person’s consent and choice.

05

Local organizations and providers

Groups that offer or connect living services, local programs, and specialist support for seniors.

Core Services

The daily life Ongil connects

Daily living, health, care, family, and local life connect on one platform. The four below are the directions we introduce today.

01

Daily living support

Find and connect help a day may need — meals, getting around, housework, and convenience.

  • Browse living information
  • Review links to local living support
  • Guidance that fits the situation
02

Health and care

Browse healthy-living information and, when needed, care and specialist organizations.

  • Healthy-living information
  • Browse care and organizations
  • No medical diagnosis or treatment by Ongil

Ongil does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Related features need a review of rules.

03

Family connection

Help seniors and families talk when it matters, and share living information with consent.

  • Sharing by choice
  • Browse services together
  • Respect, not watching or control

The senior’s consent and choice come first. Health, location, and living records are not shared with family automatically.

04

Local life

Browse facilities, programs, leisure, and people around where someone lives.

  • Local information
  • Leisure, learning, and groups
  • Community grows after protection rules are in place

Living areas

Many sides of living,
on one Ongil.

How the four directions extend into real life. Services Ongil does not run itself are described as plans to browse and connect.

01 Living areas

01

From small daily moments to the help that is needed.

Make living information and services easier to find. Help Ongil does not provide is a plan to connect local providers.

  • Meals and eating support
  • Shopping and daily goods
  • Housework, cleaning, laundry
  • Household goods and nearby amenities
  • Guidance that fits the situation

Getting around and hospital visits are covered in the mobility area below.

02 Living areas

02

Connection in daily life, for healthier days.

Help people understand health and habits, and find information. This does not replace a clinician’s judgment.

  • Healthy eating information
  • Activity programs
  • Sleep and habit guidance
  • Local health programs
  • Health content by interest

Medication reminders, checkup information, and health analysis need a review of rules. Ongil does not diagnose or treat.

03 Living areas

03

When help is needed, a way to find fitting care.

A platform to browse and connect care and specialist organizations. Ongil does not hire care workers or provide specialist care itself.

  • Home-visit and at-home living support
  • Day-care and related facilities
  • General long-term care information
  • Compare what is offered and how it works
  • Care guidance for families

Consultation, booking links, and later schedule tools are under review. Unconfirmed partners, staffing, or licenses are not shown as live.

04 Living areas

04

Closer as a family, while respecting each day.

Help people talk and share living information when it is needed. How much is shared is chosen by the person.

  • Family conversation
  • Share dates and service notes
  • Browse living support together
  • Coordinate roles and schedules
  • Share important information by choice

Examples of a later shared experience. Families do not watch or decide in place of the person.

  • Look up a local culture program together when a parent wants to join.
  • Review what a living-support service offers, and how it works, when daily help is needed.
  • With the person’s consent, share what is needed and coordinate dates when care schedules matter.
  • Browse housing and living-convenience services together when the home situation changes.

Family messaging and alerts are a later plan. Health, location, and living records are not shared automatically.

05 Living areas

05

A more comfortable life in a familiar place.

Browse housing and living-environment information that fits how people live. Ongil does not provide moves into facilities or specialist conversion.

  • Senior-friendly housing information
  • Safety and convenience at home
  • Browse home-improvement services
  • Household convenience goods
  • Local housing-support information

Smart-home and convenience-tech links are under review. The direction is to connect providers and specialists.

06 Living areas

06

Daily life that still goes where you want to go.

Make transport and going-out information easier to find. Ongil does not run vehicles or mobility services itself.

  • Public transport and travel information
  • Mobility services for people who need them
  • Transport for hospital visits
  • Going-out and accompaniment
  • Visits to culture and leisure places

Booking links and situation-based convenience are reviewed around connections with mobility providers.

07 Living areas

07

New enjoyment and learning, still part of the day.

Explore activity and experience by interest. Ongil is not only a health-and-care service.

  • Hobbies and culture
  • Lifelong learning
  • Travel and experience programs
  • Activity, leisure, and clubs
  • Volunteering and meeting across ages

Local programs, events, and digital learning are introduced as part of leisure and learning.

08 Living areas

08

New connection that starts nearby.

A local living platform to browse information, people, places, and programs around where someone lives.

  • Local welfare, culture, and sports places
  • Community-center and public programs
  • Local events and interest groups
  • Volunteering and meeting across ages
  • Local living-service providers

Community features are planned to grow after operating policy and user-protection rules are in place.

09 Living areas

09

A calmer day, as digital gets easier.

A shared base so people can use digital services more easily.

  • Guidance for phones and devices
  • Online booking and public services
  • Everyday digital settings and basic finance information
  • Online safety and fraud awareness
  • Links to local digital learning

Design aims include larger type, clear buttons, plain guidance, and voice support. Voice browsing is under review.

When it is needed,
on one Ongil.

A planned experience, not a live apply-and-book flow.

02

Browse information and services

See information, local services, and programs that fit the situation and interests.

03

Compare, connect, and keep using

Review what is offered and how it works, then connect to an organization or provider. As life changes, new needs can still be found on the same Ongil.

NEWON

Ongil’s place
inside Newon.

Ongil is the Newon business focused on senior living and care. Today the products are not technically linked, and data is not shared automatically.

  1. 01
    LivOn

    A platform for life changes and new starts from the teens through the seventies. It does not replace Ongil’s senior living and care focus.

  2. 02
    Ongil

    A platform focused on seniors’ daily life and care, and on family and community connection.

  3. 03
    NEWON AI

    AI technology and services that can support tailored living information and guidance.

  4. 04
    Consumer · Business · Commerce

    Everyday apps, solutions for companies and institutions, and trade in goods and living services.
