01ONGIL · Senior Life & Care Platform

ONGIL aims to help older adults keep their familiar routines for longer, and reach trustworthy information and people more easily when they need help. Our direction is a senior life-care super app where older adults, families, care providers, businesses and welfare agencies connect in one place.

02Why ONGIL

Help was always close by —
the way to it was not.

Growing older comes with small gaps between information, family, care and technology. ONGIL started in those gaps.

  1. 01

    Information is scattered

    Care, welfare, health and daily-life information is split across institutions and websites, so in the moment it matters it is hard to know where to start.

  2. 02

    Decisions arrive suddenly

    A hospital discharge, a fall, the day living alone gets harder — care decisions come without warning, and families must learn a great deal in very little time.

  3. 03

    Family is far away

    Family members living apart want to know how things are; older adults want to keep their own lives without feeling watched. Both deserve respect.

  4. 04

    Screens are hard

    Small text, complicated menus and unfamiliar terms are barriers in themselves. Technology should never become more burden than help.

ONGIL sets out to close these four gaps with one warm connection.

03Mission & Values

So that everyone can grow older
at their own pace, in familiar places,
and with respect.

ONGIL does not see older adults only as people to be cared for. Their own everyday life comes first; help is designed to arrive when it is needed, and only as much as is needed.

  1. 01

    Dignity

    We respect each person's decisions and way of life. Neither family nor a service decides for them.

  2. 02

    Independence

    We help people keep doing what they can for longer — help supports those tasks rather than taking them over.

  3. 03

    Connection

    We connect people with family, neighbours and institutions as far as they choose, and let them disconnect at any time.

  4. 04

    Care

    When help is needed, we help people find trustworthy information and verified institutions and businesses.

  5. 05

    Participation

    Learning, leisure, work and local activities keep people connected with society.

  6. 06

    Accessibility

    Large text, high contrast, plain language and clear buttons are the default, not an option.

04Life Journey

As life changes,
it continues on the same ONGIL.

Life circumstances matter more than age. We design ONGIL so records and connections carry on unchanged as circumstances shift.

  1. 01

    An active everyday

    Managing schedules, routines and health habits on one's own, and widening the day with learning and leisure.

  2. 02

    A little help, sometimes

    When meals, outings or housework start to need some help, finding trustworthy information and services.

  3. 03

    Together with family

    Sharing schedules and news with family within limits the person sets, so family can lend a hand when needed.

  4. 04

    When care is needed

    Comparing home care, day care and facility information, and moving on to official guidance and procedures.

  5. 05

    Reconnecting locally

    Rebuilding ties with the community through welfare centers, lifelong learning and volunteering.

05Who We Connect

With older adults at the centre,
we connect five groups.

ONGIL's V3 direction is a senior life-care super app in which older adults, families, care providers, businesses and welfare agencies connect within one flow.

  1. 01

    Older adultsIn charge of their own life

    People who record their own lives and find and choose the information and help they need. Every design decision at ONGIL starts here.

  2. 02

    FamiliesReassurance with respect

    Family members who, even from far away, see news and schedules within the limits the person allows, and share care roles.

  3. 03

    Care providersLess complicated work

    People helping older adults on the ground, such as home-care and daily-living support workers. We are reviewing ways to ease scheduling, records and family communication.

  4. 04

    BusinessesAn honest point of contact

    Companies offering everyday services such as meals, daily goods, transport and education. Connections would start with verified businesses.

  5. 05

    Welfare & public bodiesInformation that reaches people

    Welfare centers, day-care centers and local governments that run services. We are reviewing cooperation so their information reaches the older adults who need it.

06Family Connection

Family connection is
conversation, not surveillance.

The older adult decides what to share, and with whom. ONGIL's family features start from this one sentence.

  1. 01

    The default is 'share nothing'. The person turns on each item themselves.

  2. 02

    Health records and location are never shared automatically.

  3. 03

    Before sharing, we show exactly what the family will see.

  4. 04

    A connection can be ended at any time; once ended, nothing more is visible.

Partly built · in preparation Sharing settings and the preview are built into the web preview. Real connection between family members' devices will be prepared after the first release, together with accounts.

07What We Do · What We Don't

What ONGIL does —
and what it does not.

A trustworthy platform is as clear about what it will not do as about what it can.

What ONGIL does

What ONGIL does not do

✓Keeps health records exactly as entered, organised for reference at the doctor's.

✕Diagnose, prescribe, treat or judge readings.

✓Clearly points to the official channels (such as 119) to call in an emergency.

✕Respond to emergencies or guarantee rescue.

✓Shares information with family only within limits the person sets.

✕Share location or health records without consent.

✓Provides essential safety and public welfare information free of charge.

✕Use health information for ad targeting.

✓Reviews connecting people with information on services from verified businesses.

✕Hold customers' money.

✓Explains long-term care procedures in plain language and points to official bodies.

✕Broker long-term care insurance services or collect co-payments.
