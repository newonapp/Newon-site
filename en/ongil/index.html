About ONGIL

A caring path between older adults and their families.

ONGIL is a comprehensive senior platform that brings together health, daily life, care, safety, family, friends, hobbies, welfare, long-term care and smart devices. It is not just a way to find a care worker: it helps older adults manage their own day and meet people nearby, lets families far away check in only as far as they are allowed, and leads to trustworthy care and welfare information when it is needed.

Now
Web preview · no sign-in, stored on this device only
Next
iOS and Android app being prepared for first release
Core users
Older adults · guardians and family · caregivers (institutions are separate business customers)
Principles
Consent first · essential safety and welfare information free · health data never used for ads

ONGIL is growing from the senior daily-life features it offers today in its web preview into health and family care, friends and gatherings, care and welfare, safety devices, and enterprise and public-sector solutions. Many of the businesses and features on this page are part of future development or partnership plans; only the web preview features are available today.

Problem → Solution

The problems older adults and families face,
and how ONGIL approaches them.

In a super-aged society, the weight of care falls not on one older person but on the whole family. Breaking the problem down makes the starting point clear.

  1. Problem

    Health and medication information is scattered

    Blood-pressure notebooks, pill packets and appointment slips are kept apart, making it hard to explain at the doctor's and hard for family to follow.

    ONGIL's approach

    One record that the older adult enters themselves. It is kept exactly as entered, without judgement or diagnosis, and organized so it is easy to show at an appointment.

  2. Problem

    Family far away worries

    Calls and family group chats help, but daily checking is hard, and too much checking is a burden on the older adult.

    ONGIL's approach

    Only the items the older adult allows are shared. The default is 'not shared', family see a preview first, and sharing can be withdrawn at any time.

  3. Problem

    Care and welfare information is hard to find

    Long-term care grades, day care, home care and benefit schemes use difficult terms and come from many sources.

    ONGIL's approach

    Care types and official routes explained in plain words, and public welfare information given free with its source. Eligibility and applications stay with official agencies.

  4. Problem

    Fewer relationships narrow daily life

    After retirement, moving or losing a spouse, there are fewer people to meet and fewer reasons to go out.

    ONGIL's approach

    ONGIL Friends is planned to help people meet again through open neighbourhood gatherings, walking and hobby friends and welfare-center programs. Basic participation is planned to stay free.

  5. Problem

    Safety devices and services do not talk to each other

    Emergency buttons, smartwatches and telecom check-in services each use their own app and contact list.

    ONGIL's approach

    An emergency contact list comes first in software; Safe Band and Devices then review consent-based connection of certified devices, step by step.

Core Values

For days that are more comfortable,
more active and more connected.

ONGIL does not see older adults only as people to be cared for. It respects them as people who choose, act and carry on their own lives.

  • Independence

    Managing your own schedule, medication and records is the starting point. Key features are designed to work without a guardian's help.

  • Connection

    Connected with family as far as you allow, and with neighbours through shared interests, without revealing exact addresses or live location.

  • Wellbeing

    Helps keep up habits, activity and health appointments. These are everyday records and general information, not diagnoses or judgements.

  • Safety

    Organize who to contact in an emergency and spot everyday risks early. SOS, emergency contacts and essential safety information stay free.

  • Care

    Helps you find trustworthy care and welfare information and official routes when help is needed. Long-term care benefits and private services are never mixed.

  • Dignity

    Important choices are always yours first. Large text, plain words, wide touch targets and easy undo are the default.

Use Cases

Moments when
ONGIL helps.

Typical situations, noting what can be tried in the web preview today and what opens later in the app.

  • Morning check-in and medication

    The older adult notes mood and how they feel and marks the medicine taken. Recording works in the web preview today; passing it to family is being prepared in the app.

  • Getting ready for the doctor

    Blood-pressure and glucose records, symptom notes and the appointment come together on one screen to show the doctor. Hospital-escort connection is planned.

  • Siblings sharing care

    One sibling takes hospital visits, another groceries, another the evening call. Shared family schedules and roles are planned after the first app release.

  • Making new friends nearby

    Finding a walking group in the local park or a knitting circle at the welfare center. Friends is planned, starting with verified open gatherings.

  • A family looking into care centers

    Choosing area and type to find day-care centers and care homes, and saving favourites. Facility search is in the web preview today; ratings should be checked with official sources.

Who ONGIL Serves

Three core users,
different permissions.

Older adults, guardians and family, and caregivers may use the same app, but what they can see and do differs. Institution managers and sellers are separate business customers with their own work permissions.

  • Older adults

    Manage health, medication and daily life, and find friends, gatherings, events and welfare information. They decide what to record and with whom to share it. As a principle, older adults are not charged for basic features.

  • Guardians and family

    See the health records and schedules the older adult allows, check in and share care with siblings. Service booking, Life Box gifts and Safe Band alerts are part of future plans.

  • Caregivers

    Tools are planned for care workers to manage assigned schedules, visit tasks and care records, and to communicate with guardians within permitted limits.

Consent and permissions

There is no structure in which guardians or institutions automatically see everything. Health records and location are never shared automatically, and any connection can be ended at any time.

Long-term Vision

From older adults' daily life
to a wider care ecosystem.

ONGIL's long-term direction is a comprehensive senior life platform connecting independent living, social relationships, safety and family care. It aims to connect people with the right information and organizations, not to be a care provider itself.

  1. 01

    Older adults and families

    Daily health and life records, family care and Friends come first, to create a reason to use ONGIL and to earn trust.

  2. 02

    Caregivers and private services

    Information and scheduling and record tools for home care, hospital escorts and daily support, starting with verified providers.

  3. 03

    Care institutions and employers

    SaaS for day-care centers, home-care centers and care homes, and family-care benefits for employers.

  4. 04

    Local government, public sector, overseas

    Public programs, certified safety-device integration and overseas markets are reviewed after regulation and operations are verified.

Long-Term Care Insurance services are used through the National Health Insurance Service's grading and designated institutions. ONGIL does not broker these services or collect co-payments, and NEWON does not hold customer funds.

Trust & Responsibility

The more precious daily life is,
the more trust matters.

Seven commitments ONGIL keeps.

  • Privacy

    Web preview records are stored on this device without sign-in. Health data is never used for ad targeting and is not combined with other Newon apps without explicit consent.

  • Limits of AI

    AI is a helper for summarizing records and reading out schedules. It does not diagnose illness, adjust medication or judge emergencies.

  • Choice and consent

    You decide what to record and who sees it. Withdrawing consent is designed to be just as easy.

  • Limits on location

    Location is reviewed only with separate consent, with limited purpose, duration and viewers, and an access log. Families never get unlimited tracking.

  • Official information vs ads

    Designation, ratings and qualifications come from official sources, and ads or recommendations are labelled separately. Institutions are never shown as partners without a confirmed agreement.

  • Dignity and autonomy

    Words and screens that respect a self-directed life, not excessive worry or pity.

Emergency vs counselling

ONGIL does not respond to emergencies, and the emergency contact list does not call anyone automatically. In Korea: 119 for emergencies, 112 for missing persons or crime, 129 for welfare counselling, 109 for emotional support.

NEWON Ecosystem

Where ONGIL fits
within Newon.

ONGIL keeps its own senior brand and experience. Links with other Newon services are at the planning stage, and personal health, location and care data are never combined across apps without explicit consent.

  • LIVONA life-event information platform. Life-stage information such as retirement planning and welfare schemes is covered by LIVON, while ONGIL focuses on daily life and care in later years.
  • BABYLOGA family growth record platform. Linking grandparents' shared family activities is under review.
  • PETLOGA pet life record. Older adults living with pets can keep their pet's life in PETLOG.
  • SHAREONA neighbourhood rental platform. Renting daily-living, hobby and assistive items only as long as needed is under review.
  • MY WORLDA travel record app. Travel records are kept in MY WORLD, while ONGIL covers nearby leisure and culture.
  • Newon BusinessDigital tools and a partnership desk for institutions and companies, including workflow automation for care and welfare institutions.
