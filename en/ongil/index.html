NEWON / ONGIL

NEWON ONGIL·CARE FIRST. STAY CONNECTED.

Care and safety
for older adults and families.

A senior-care platform we are building for care, safety, health routines, and daily living support.

Family connection is part of care — information is shared only in the range the person agrees to.

This page is a business introduction. Home-care booking, live matching, and emergency rescue are not offered yet.

01 · Leisure (extra)

Leisure (extra)

Classes, groups, and trips are an extra area — not Ongil’s core. Core is care and safety.

Key functions

  • Explore interests and hobbies
  • Class, group, and trip information
  • Apply, book, and manage dates
  • Activity records and meeting people
  • Search by interest, experience, alone or together, online or offline, area, and how far you can travel.
  • See dates, place, fee, materials, and access in one place.
  • After you join, keep a record and look for the next class or group.

Example uses

  • Finding a beginner watercolor class and keeping a work record
  • Joining a beginner park-golf group and planning the next date

02 · Family connection

Family connection

Connect for shared care when it matters. Health and living information is shared only with consent.

Key functions

  • Check-ins and messages
  • Shared family calendar
  • Hobbies and trips together
  • Help requests and shared roles
  • Family does not watch every moment. People share the dates and information they choose.
  • Plan exhibitions, outings, or clinic visits together, and ask for help with shopping or going along.
  • Sensitive information is not shared without separate consent.

Example uses

  • Planning a weekend exhibition and meal together
  • Coordinating a clinic visit and whether someone comes along

03 · Care & safety

Care & safety

Care links, safety check-ins, health and medication routines, and daily living support are the core.

Key functions

  • Care-service discovery (planned)
  • Safety and check-in direction (planned)
  • Medication and clinic living support
  • Daily help and welfare/care information search
  • Home-care booking, live matching, and emergency detection or rescue are not offered yet.
  • Living and care information focuses on search and preparation.
  • Ongil does not diagnose, prescribe, or handle emergencies. Health information is not shared without consent.

Example uses

  • Seeing a morning class and an afternoon clinic on one calendar
  • Listing the help you need and sharing roles with family
