FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Q1Can I rent or book on the website?

No. The website only introduces SHAREON. Listing, booking requests and chat happen in the SHAREON app, which is still in pilot development and not in the app stores.

Q2Do I need a partnership contract to list?

No. Anyone — individual or business — signs up and lists directly in the app. Some items may need qualification or safety checks.

Q3Do I need a subscription?

Basic listing and trading are designed to work without one. A subscription for pros and businesses is under consideration, with no prices set.

Q4How are deposits handled?

Rentals that need a deposit open only once a PG-supported deposit method is ready. SHAREON does not build its own escrow, and payment, deposits and settlement are not operating today.

Q5Why approval-based rather than instant booking?

At first, we think lenders should be able to see each request and decide. Once rentals build up and trust tools are in place, we will consider instant booking.

Q6How do ads appear?

Paid placements and ads are always labelled as advertising, never mixed in unmarked.
