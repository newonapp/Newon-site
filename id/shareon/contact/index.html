  • Partners, institutions & external5

Use & joining

  1. 01General questions

    General questions such as what SHAREON is and where launch news will appear. The app has not launched yet.

    Contact us →

  2. 02Features & use

    Questions and feedback on features such as booking, rental units or deposit terms. As the app has not launched, there is no hands-on user support yet.

    Contact us →

  3. 03Personal rental

    How to lend or borrow as an individual. Basic listing is meant to work without a subscription.

    Contact us →

  4. 04Business listing

    How rental shops and small businesses join, and business verification. Direct sign-up without a partnership contract. Please tell us your items and area.

    Contact us →

  5. 05Space partnerships

    Taking part as a meeting-room, filming-space or rehearsal-room operator, and empty-hours pilots. Please include the space’s use and your operating rights.

    Contact us →

  6. 06Business asset management

    Internal sharing of equipment and rooms, and adopting the business tools. These tools have not been released.

    Contact us →

Partners, institutions & external

  1. 07Delivery & insurance partners

    Transaction infrastructure such as delivery and collection, insurance and guarantees, and payment providers. Reviewed after the rules and contract structure are checked.

    Contact us →

  2. 08Institutions & public sector

    Shared-resource collaboration with local governments, universities and public bodies, following each institution’s procurement and contract process.

    Contact us →

  3. 09Technology & API

    Technical collaboration such as stock, booking or delivery integration. No public API is offered today.

    Contact us →

  4. 10Press & media

    Interviews and information requests. Please include your outlet and deadline. Undecided figures and dates cannot be shared.

    Contact us →

  5. 11Investment

    Investment inquiries about NEWON and the SHAREON business. Answers stay within the published business information; undecided financial information is not provided.

    Contact us →

HOW IT WORKS

How an inquiry reaches us.

  1. 01

    Choose a type

    Pick the closest inquiry type in the list above. If several fit, choose the one for your main purpose.

  2. 02

    Contact us

    ‘Contact us’ opens NEWON’s unified contact page with SHAREON already selected. For email, write to newon@newon.app.

  3. 03

    Review

    We read your inquiry. Partnership proposals continue through the partnership process (intake → fit and legal review → pilot scope).

  4. 04

    Reply

    We reply where needed. Timing depends on the inquiry, and no response deadline is guaranteed.

BEFORE YOU WRITE

Please check
before you write.

What we can answer now, and what we cannot yet.

  • We can answer

    • What SHAREON is and what is being prepared
    • The basic structure of personal rental, business listing and space participation
    • Reviews of pilot, trade-infrastructure and institutional proposals
    • Press and investor questions within the published business information

  • We cannot share yet

    • Undecided fee rates and prices
    • Launch dates, launch areas and countries
    • Customer support for real rentals, bookings, payments or refunds
    • Undecided figures and financial information

  • Include only the minimum needed for a reply (name, email and your question).

  • Do not include sensitive personal data such as national ID numbers, card numbers, bank passwords or health information.

  • Do not include the exact address where an item is kept, or other people’s contact details or personal data.

  • SHAREON inquiries are received only through NEWON’s contact page and official email (newon@newon.app).

  • As the app has not launched, there is no customer support for real rentals, bookings, payments or refunds.

  • Fee rates, prices and launch dates are not decided, so specific figures or dates cannot be given.

  • Reply times depend on the inquiry, and no response deadline is guaranteed.
