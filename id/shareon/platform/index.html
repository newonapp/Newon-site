HOW IT WORKS

From exploring to reviews,
in six steps.

Goods, equipment and spaces all follow the same flow.

  1. 01Explore

    Explore goods and spaces

    Find goods, equipment and spaces by field, area and period. Map and area search show places nearby and further away.

  2. 02Details

    Check the details

    Check photos, components, condition, facilities, capacity, opening hours and house rules. Exact addresses are not shown before a trade.

  3. 03Terms

    Check the schedule and terms

    Check the provider’s terms — dates, rental unit, handover method, a deposit — and agree to them before the trade.

  4. 04Request

    Book or request

    Request your dates and the provider confirms. Spaces go through booking approval under each space’s conditions of use.

  5. 05Handover & Return

    Handover, use and return

    Both sides check condition and components at handover, use it for the agreed period, and check again at return. Delivery and return shipping are agreed between the parties.

  6. 06Record & Review

    Record and review

    The trade is recorded and both sides leave reviews. If something goes wrong, reporting and dispute handling follow.

SHAREON services are used through the mobile app; this website does not offer booking or payment.

USER SCENARIOS

One rental,
followed role by role.

Scenes drawn to explain the service structure.

  • Borrower — camping gear for two days

    Find camping gear by area and date, check the components and terms, then send a booking request. Once the provider confirms, meet, check the condition and take it. Check again at return and leave a review.

  • Lender — a camera kit not in use

    List photos, components, terms and available dates. Review requests from people and businesses, and at return compare the condition with the handover record.

  • Rental business — existing stock as it is

    A rental business that has passed business verification lists its products and takes requests from people and businesses on terms that match how it already operates.

  • Space operator — a studio’s idle hours

    List the studio’s facilities, capacity, opening hours and house rules, and approve booking requests for idle time slots.

  • Company — event kit to another business

    After an event, lend the remaining sound and lighting kit to another business. Bulk and long-term rentals follow terms agreed between the businesses.

PRIVACY, SAFETY & TRUST

Basic principles
for trust and safety.

Goods and spaces change hands between people and businesses meeting for the first time, so the rules of the trade come first.

  • 01

    Identity and business verification

    Individuals take part after identity verification; businesses after business verification, which checks the business details.

  • 02

    Trade information and records

    Both sides agree to the terms before a trade and keep a record of the handover and return they both checked. Borrower and lender responsibilities follow the trade terms.

  • 03

    User reviews

    Reviews are left by users who completed a trade and, together with the trade record, can be used as a reference when judging the other party.

  • 04

    Reporting and disputes

    Users and items can be reported and blocked, and disputes are handled through evidence and objections. Damage liability is never decided automatically from photos alone.

  • 05

    Privacy protection

    Exact addresses and contact details are handled only as far as a trade needs, and location data is removed from photos.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

How do people and businesses take part?

Individuals borrow or lend goods and equipment after identity verification; sole traders and companies list goods and spaces after business verification. C2C, B2C, C2B and B2B trades all follow the same flow in the same app.

Can I borrow both goods and spaces?

SHAREON covers goods and equipment — home, digital, filming, camping, baby and industrial kit — and work, shoot, rehearsal and gathering spaces. Fields that need specific laws and licences are handled within what those rules allow.

How do booking, handover and return work?

Check the provider’s terms and send a booking request; the provider confirms. At handover and return both sides check the condition and keep a record, and delivery and return shipping are agreed between the parties.

Is it a service for buying and selling?

No. SHAREON is a rental platform for borrowing for a set period and giving back, without transfer of ownership. It does not handle second-hand or new-product sales.

Is the deposit SHAREON’s revenue?

No. A deposit is a trade term set by the parties and is not SHAREON’s revenue. It is never deducted without legal or contractual grounds.

Where can I find fees and prices?

The website does not give detailed prices. The principle is that trade terms can be checked before each trade, and terms for businesses are explained through a business inquiry.

What happens if something is damaged?

Disputes are handled through handover and return records, evidence and objections. Liability is never decided from photos alone, and lenders take out their own insurance where needed.

Are there things that cannot be listed?

Dangerous, illegal or regulated items cannot be listed. Items that need specific laws and licences, such as medical and rehab aids or industrial equipment, are handled only within what those rules allow.

Where do I send questions about a trade?

Pick an inquiry type on SHAREON Contact and you are taken to NEWON’s unified contact page. Booking, payment and refund handling for individual trades are not done through website inquiries.

Can I book on the website?

No. SHAREON services are used through the mobile app; this website does not offer booking or payment.
