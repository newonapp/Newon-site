OVERVIEW

Launch is inspection,
not vibes.

“Looks fine” is not a production bar.

Content, design, SEO, performance, accessibility,
legal, analytics, and final QA
should pass on one shared checklist.

This document is the last common list
the team can walk before going live.

MODULES

Eight zones that
catch pre-publish gaps.

01

CONTENT

Typos, links, CTAs, contact, policy pages.
Start with whether the writing actually works.

02

DESIGN

Desktop · Tablet · Mobile —
type and spacing that hold across devices.

03

SEO

Page title, meta description, Open Graph,
sitemap, robots — checked before index day.

04

PERFORMANCE

Image weight, load, fonts, JS errors.
Trim a heavy first paint.

05

ACCESSIBILITY

Contrast, alt text, keyboard, form labels.
Clear a basic accessibility bar.

06

LEGAL

Privacy Policy, Terms, cookie notice.
Confirm required notices for publish.

07

ANALYTICS

Analytics, conversion events, Search Console.
Ship with measurement on.

08

FINAL QA

Forms, 404s, redirects, production domain.
Walk the live path yourself.

HOW TO USE

How to use it.

  1. 01

    Start on staging

    Walk Content and Design first
    on an environment close to production.

  2. 02

    Bundle SEO and performance

    Check meta, OG, images, and fonts together.
    If they miss the bar, delay publish.

  3. 03

    Click legal and forms for real

    Open Privacy, Terms, contact forms,
    and cookie notice along the live click path.

  4. 04

    Prove analytics is firing

    Confirm analytics and conversion events
    show on staging or production before launch.

  5. 05

    Sign off after Final QA

    Pass 404s, redirects, and domain checks,
    then record owner and time on Launch Sign-off.
