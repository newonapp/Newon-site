Collection and use of personal information

Purpose
Reviewing and replying to your inquiry, and keeping a record of how it was handled
Items
[Required] inquiry type, name or contact person, email address, subject, message, whether you agreed to this notice · [Optional] who you are contacting us as, company or organization name, area of cooperation, phone, related platform, company website, reference URL · [Automatic] the page address the inquiry was sent from, language, time of submission
How it is handled
What you enter is delivered to NEWON's official email (newon@newon.app) through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email forwarding service operated outside Korea. According to FormSubmit's documentation, submissions are kept by that service for 30 days. The NEWON website does not store your inquiry on any server or database of its own.
Retention
Kept for as long as needed to review and reply to your inquiry; requests for access or deletion are handled in line with applicable law. Everything else follows the Privacy Policy.
Your right to refuse
You may refuse to agree to the collection and use of your personal information. If you do not agree, this form cannot be sent, but you can still email newon@newon.app directly.
