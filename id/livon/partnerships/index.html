Sources vs Partners

Public information sources
are not business partners.

Government and public bodies are shown only as sources of public information unless an agreement exists.

Principles

Partnership principles

  • Official sources are shown; no false or exaggerated claims.

  • Paid ads and partnerships are always labelled apart from information.

  • User data is kept to a minimum and never shared without consent.

  • LIVON never stands in for licensed selling, brokering or advice.

  • Performance-based fees in medical and legal fields are not discussed until a legal review is complete.

  • No organisation is shown as a partner before an agreement is signed.

How it works

How a partnership comes together.

01

Inquiry

Tell us the type, purpose and life area through NEWON's contact page.

02

Review

We check accuracy, value to users and regulatory conditions such as licensing and privacy together.

03

Proposal

We agree the scope and how it is shown — source, ad or partnership. Terms are agreed privately, not published here.

04

Agreement

Roles, responsibilities, update cycle and exit terms are agreed in writing.

05

Start & review

We start within the agreed scope and review labelling and accuracy together, regularly.

FAQ

Questions before partnering

01Who are LIVON's partners today?

This page describes partnership types. When an agreement is signed, it is shown only within its scope.

02What does a partnership cost?

Terms are agreed case by case and there is no public price list. No numbers appear on this page.

03Can I join the market as a seller?

The market is in preparation. Leave your interest through a ‘Content & expert cooperation’ inquiry and we will consider it as the market is prepared.

04Can LIVON use our public information?

LIVON presents published public information with its source and date checked. If something needs correcting, please contact us.
