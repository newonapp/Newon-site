Why LIVON · All-in-One Life Platform

Life's big decisions
are almost always firsts.

The day before enlisting, the month of a first salary, the evening you first face a lease, the moment you first think about caring for a parent. It is a first for everyone, and the information you need is scattered across agencies, portals and forums.

LIVON began to put those questions back onto one flow — a single person's life. Its job is not to collect more information, but to connect it to the life you are living now.

  1. 01Firsts

    Most of life's turning points happen to us once.

  2. 02Scattered

    What you need sits with different bodies and fields.

  3. 03Repeated

    Every new stage means searching all over again.

Life Journey

From growing up to later life,
one continuous journey.

Five chapters of life and the question that comes up most in each. Ages are typical examples — everyone's pace differs.

  1. 01 · Teens

    Growing up

    “What should I learn, and where am I heading?”

    School, study, first thoughts about the future

  2. 02 · 20s

    Finding a path

    “A first job, military service — where do I start?”

    University, certificates, military service, a first job

  3. 03 · 20s–30s

    Standing alone

    “Living alone for the first time — what do I need?”

    A first home, moving, driving, managing money

  4. 04 · 30s–50s

    Family

    “With a family, which schemes apply to us?”

    Marriage, birth, parenting, caring for parents

  5. 05 · 50s onward

    Later life

    “How do I prepare for life after work?”

    Health, retirement, pensions, putting affairs in order

A Person's Journey

How one person
uses LIVON.

Example: someone in their 20s preparing to move out. Not a real screen — it shows the order in which LIVON connects information.

  1. 01

    Pick a stage

    Start from your current life stage.

    Example20s — moving out, housing and first-job topics come first.

  2. 02

    Widen to an event

    See every field one event touches.

    Example‘Moving out’ — housing, pre-contract checks, registration and living costs.

  3. 03

    Check the official source

    Schemes are confirmed with the body that runs them.

    ExampleHousing support — the official portal link, date checked and region.

  4. 04

    Keep it on your device

    Save to-dos and plans without an account.

    ExampleAdd the ‘before signing’ checklist to My Life.

  5. 05

    Move on when needed

    Get pointed to another NEWON app or service.

    ExampleSharing things you no longer use — in SHAREON.

Principles

The principles LIVON keeps.

  • Free, no account

    Core information and on-device records are free and need no sign-up.

  • Source, date, region

    Public information comes with its source, the date checked and the region.

  • Status as it is

    Every field and feature says available, in preparation or planned.

  • Not a professional

    Medical, legal, insurance and finance stop at general information and official channels.

  • Ads are ads

    Paid ads and partnerships are always labelled apart from information.

  • Minimal personal data

    Only what is needed — and, where possible, only on your device.

  • No fake market

    No products, booking or payment until real sellers exist.
