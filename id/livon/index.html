Our Vision

Not just collecting information —
connecting it to the life you live now.

LIVON is an All-in-One Life Platform. Its strength is connection, not volume. It links information across fields around your life stage and what you are going through.

  1. 01

    Stage Start from where you are, from the teens to 70+.

  2. 02

    Event One life event — enlisting, a first job, marriage, retirement — shows every field it touches.

  3. 03

    Source Public schemes always show the official source, the date checked and the region covered.

  4. 04

    Free LIVON on the web is free and works without signing up or logging in. Personal records stay on your device.

  5. 05

    Classification The 40 categories are a content classification. Each one shows what is actually available.

Life Journey

From growing up to later life,
one continuous journey.

Five chapters of life and the areas people open most in each, with key topics and the linked areas (A–E). Ages are typical examples — everyone's pace differs.

  1. 01 Teens

    Growing up

    School, study, first thoughts about the future — from settling in and study plans to finding youth counselling.

    • A Education & Career

  2. 02 20s

    Finding a path

    University, certificates, military service, a first job — admissions and scholarships, service procedures, CVs and contracts.

    • A Education & Career
    • B Life Essentials

  3. 03 20s–30s

    Standing alone

    A first home, moving, driving, managing money — pre-contract checks, registration, budgets and credit basics.

    • B Life Essentials
    • C Finance & Housing

  4. 04 30s–50s

    Family

    Marriage, birth, parenting, caring for parents — registration, birth and childcare schemes, long-term care.

    • D Family & Relationships
    • C Finance & Housing

  5. 05 50s onward

    Later life

    Health, retirement, pensions, putting affairs in order — check-ups, pensions, senior welfare, inheritance and funerals.

    • E Wellness & Later Life

How the NEWON platforms divide the work

Each app focuses on its own job and LIVON points to them. There is no data link between the apps today.

Check birth support and parental leave in LIVON, then keep feeding, sleep and growth records in BABYLOG.

LIVON explains retirement planning, pensions and senior welfare; ONGIL handles day-to-day care and support for older adults and their families.

When moving out means buying or selling things, LIVON does not run personal used-goods trading itself — it points to SHAREON.
