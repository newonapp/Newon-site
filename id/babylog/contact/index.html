Before You Write

Before you get in touch.

  1. 01

    Official inquiry form

    Inquiries go to the official NEWON inquiry form. This BABYLOG page does not collect personal data itself.

  2. 02

    App questions

    Questions about app features, usage, errors and accounts go through the same form. Records and accounts themselves are managed in the app.

  3. 03

    Replies

    We reply after review. An inquiry does not guarantee cooperation or a contract.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

Your inquiry is sent through the official NEWON inquiry form. Personal data is handled according to the Privacy Policy. Privacy Policy
