05 · AFTER YOU WRITE

What happens after
we receive it.

01

Received

After sending, a confirmation screen appears and the message reaches the official inbox.

02

Reviewed

We check the type and product, and e-mail you if we need missing details.

03

Answered

We reply to the e-mail you gave. It can take longer when there are many inquiries.

04

Follow-up

If a next step is needed, such as re-sending, a fixed version or licence terms, we follow up.

Reply times depend on the inquiry, and an inquiry does not mean a purchase or licence agreement.

06 · SUPPORT SCOPE

What support covers
and what it doesn't.

Included

  • Contents, formats and terms of Studio products
  • Buying steps, delivery and re-sending
  • How to use web tools and software
  • Opening files and checking errors
  • Explaining licence scope
  • Business product launch and adoption information

Not included

  • Commissioned design, development or production
  • Quote requests or outsourcing consultations
  • Filling in documents or entering data for you
  • Legal, tax or medical judgements
  • Support for other companies' products
  • Platform app account issues (use each app's support)

FAQ

Contact
FAQ.

01How long does a reply take?

We reply by e-mail in order after review. It depends on the inquiry and how many we've received.

02Can I call?

Studio inquiries are taken through this form and by e-mail (newon@newon.app) only.

03Should I send platform app problems here?

App account, payment or feature questions are best sent through each app's own channels. Studio Contact handles Studio products.

04Can I commission design or app development?

No. NEWON doesn't do outsourced production. Studio only offers its own standard products.

05Can I include personal data?

Apart from the name and e-mail we need to reply, please leave out sensitive data such as ID numbers or health information.

06Can I attach files?

This form doesn't take attachments. If a screenshot helps, reply to our e-mail with it.

07I sent it but didn't see the confirmation screen.

It may be a network problem. Try again later or e-mail newon@newon.app directly.

Your message is sent through NEWON's official inquiry form, and personal data is handled under the Privacy Policy. Privacy Policy
