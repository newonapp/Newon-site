Studio

Digital products
NEWON plans and builds itself.

NEWON Studio is NEWON's digital product business: we plan and build templates, productivity tools, software and standard business products, and offer them to individuals and businesses.

01 · WHAT STUDIO DOES

What does
NEWON Studio do?

  1. 01

    We turn everyday and work problems that come up again and again across NEWON's six platforms into digital products. Parenting records, pet care schedules, travel budgets, rental asset management, goal tracking and family care plans are all things many people handle in much the same way.

  2. 02

    Products come in four forms: digital templates you download and fill in, productivity tools that run in the browser, software for keeping records and schedules over time, and standard products that businesses and organisations use together.

  3. 03

    Every product is a standard product NEWON plans and owns. Once made, it is used by many customers on the same terms. We do not do custom design or development for clients, or production on commission.

02 · WHY OUR OWN PRODUCTS

Why do we build
our own products?

To turn what we learn running the platforms into products anyone can use on the same terms.

  1. 01
    Needs seen on the platforms

    The record, schedule, cost and operations problems we mapped while designing six platforms' service areas are where product planning starts.

  2. 02
    Tools needed outside the app

    Not everything happens inside an app. A printed checklist or a budget kept in a spreadsheet is often what people use alongside it.

  3. 03
    Same quality, same terms

    A standard product has the same contents and terms for everyone. Terms are not set per client and nothing is built separately.

  4. 04
    Products that keep improving

    Each product carries a version; improvements go into the next version. It is not a one-off deliverable.

03 · FOUR PRODUCT FAMILIES

Four product
families.

Each family is used differently and by different people. Even on the same topic, a personal planner and an operating sheet for a business are designed as separate products.

01

DIGITAL TEMPLATES

Digital templates

Documents and sheets you download and fill in. Fixed sections and a set order help you cover everything, even the first time.

Product types
  • Planners
  • Checklists
  • Record sheets
  • Workbooks
  • Document kits
  • Spreadsheet forms
Used for
Organising things with known steps: a birth-preparation list, a travel checklist, monthly pet spending, the documents for starting a project.
For
People preparing for something for the first time, and small businesses that reuse the same forms
02

PRODUCTIVITY TOOLS

Productivity tools

Calculation, generation and conversion tools that run in the browser with no installation. Values are calculated on your device and results can be copied or saved straight away.

Product types
  • Calculators
  • Date & period tools
  • Generators
  • Converters
  • Checkers
Used for
Short, exact jobs: counting days to a date, adding up subscriptions, working out a percentage change, making a QR code or a password.
For
Anyone who needs a quick calculation, and practitioners who want fewer repeated sums
03

SOFTWARE PRODUCTS

Software products

Products for keeping records, schedules and costs in one place over time. Easier to enter and look up than a template, with things documents can't do, such as period summaries and reminders.

Product types
  • Record keeping
  • Scheduling
  • Budget & spending
  • Goal tracking
  • Operations
Used for
Building up growth records by date, seeing rental assets' schedules and income on one screen, checking goal progress each week.
For
Individuals and families keeping the same records for a long time, and businesses reviewing operations regularly
04

BUSINESS SOLUTIONS

Standard business products

Standard products for the operating work that repeats in each industry: childcare facilities, pet shops, stay and experience businesses, rental businesses, schools and welfare organisations. They help several staff work to the same forms and rules.

Product types
  • Industry operating templates
  • Calculation & analysis tools
  • Business software
  • Industry packages
Used for
Recurring team work: daily operating logs, booking and rental schedules, inventory and asset registers, monthly reports.
For
Small workplaces and organisations where several people share the same work

04 · FOR INDIVIDUALS AND BUSINESSES

What individuals
and businesses can use.

Within the same product family, individuals and businesses use products to a different extent and on different terms.

What individual customers can buy

  • Planners, checklists and record sheets for your own and your family's life
  • Spreadsheet forms and workbooks for personal schedules, budgets and goals
  • Browser tools for calculation and generation (13 free today)
  • Software for keeping personal records over time (in planning)
  • Document kits that freelancers and sole traders use for their own work

Standard products businesses can use

  • Industry operating templates (daily logs, schedules, registers, report forms)
  • Calculation and analysis tools (cost and income, utilisation, staffing and scheduling)
  • Business software shared by several staff (in planning)
  • Packages that bundle what an industry needs
  • Team and business licences for use across a workplace

For business customers too, a product's contents and features are fixed, just as for personal products. We do not offer separate development or design changes for an organisation.

07 · DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLES

Our product
development principles.

01

Designed in the order you work

Sections follow the order people actually work in, so a first-time user can follow along without a manual.

02

Only the information needed

Tools calculate on your device where possible, and we don't collect personal data a product doesn't need.

03

Scope stated up front

Each product page says what a product does and doesn't do. Health, care and finance products stay within recording and organising.

04

Easy-to-open formats

Widely used formats such as DOCX, PDF and spreadsheets come first, so you're not tied to one program.

05

Managed by version

Each product shows its version and last update, and changes are noted on its page.

06

Only what is confirmed

Unreleased products are never made to look on sale, and unconfirmed features, prices or reviews are never shown.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions
about terms of use.

01Can I commission design or development from NEWON Studio?

No. Studio only offers standard products NEWON has planned itself. We don't do custom design, web or app development, or production for individual clients.

02What can I buy right now?

The one paid product is the NEWON Project Starter Kit, requested by purchase inquiry on its Newon Store page. The 13 free web tools can be used straight away. Other products are in planning.

03Can I pay on the website?

No. The NEWON website has no log-in or payment. Products on sale are handled through the purchase inquiry on their Store page.

04Can I share purchased files with others?

By default, files are for the buyer or the buyer's own business work. Reselling, redistributing or sharing the original files is not allowed; each product's terms are on its page.

05What if several people at my company need it?

Use by several people falls under team or business licences. See the concepts on the Business Products page, then tell us the number of users and scope through a licence inquiry.

06What about refunds?

The Studio pages don't set a separate refund policy. Refund and cancellation conditions follow what is shown on each product page and in the purchase guidance.

07Can I get updated versions?

Every product shows its version. Whether and how updates are provided follows the product page; ask through a purchase & download inquiry if anything is unclear.
