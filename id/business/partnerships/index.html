01 · PARTNERSHIP TYPES

Six ways
to partner.

How we partner depends on the platform and field. Ordinary platform participation is kept separate from strategic partnership contracts.

01

PLATFORM PARTICIPATION

Platform participation and listing

Eligible people and businesses join a NEWON platform to offer or use goods, services or physical assets.

SHAREON aims to be an open marketplace that people and businesses join directly.

Inquire about this type →

02

BRAND & COMMERCE

Brand and commerce partnerships

Connecting products, brands and services relevant to each platform's users, such as parenting, pets and travel.

Brand campaigns and partner sales go ahead only after actual contracts and service operations are confirmed.

Inquire about this type →

03

SERVICE & CONTENT

Service and content partnerships

Connecting information and services users need in education, everyday life, travel, care and welfare.

We follow the qualification, licence and regulatory requirements of each field.

Inquire about this type →

04

BUSINESS SOLUTIONS

Business solution partnerships

Companies and institutions adopt NEWON's own platforms and standard SaaS, or take part in their ecosystems.

We do not offer custom web or app development.

Inquire about this type →

05

PUBLIC & INSTITUTIONS

Public and institutional partnerships

With education and welfare institutions, local governments and public bodies, we review access to life services, information links and the use of eligible digital solutions.

We never show public agreements or certifications that have not been signed.

Inquire about this type →

06

TECHNOLOGY & GLOBAL

Technology and global partnerships

We review partnerships that help our own platforms grow, such as integration, data protection, payment and settlement infrastructure and overseas markets.

API or account integration with outside businesses is not offered today; the scope of any integration is confirmed during review.

Inquire about this type →
