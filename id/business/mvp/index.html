    One continuous path from scope to release — not a handoff chain.

PROCESS

How we work

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    Share the idea, audience, timing, and references. A full brief is optional.

  2. 02

    Scope

    We lock what belongs in the MVP — and what does not.

  3. 03

    Structure and UX

    Core flows and screen structure before heavy build.

  4. 04

    Build

    We implement the agreed scope and share progress along the way.

  5. 05

    Test and release

    We check key scenarios, then deploy for validation.

  6. 06

    Learn and decide

    We help you choose keep, expand, or pivot based on early signal.
