02 / NEWON+ ACCOUNT

Sign up once.
Use every Newon app with one account.

Newon+ is the shared account and service hub for Newon apps. Create a Newon+ account once, then sign in to other Newon apps with that same account — no separate new sign-up in each app.

Installing another app does not log you in automatically. In each app, sign in with your existing Newon account.

  1. 01
    Create a Newon+ account

    Sign up once in Newon+.

  2. 02
    Install the Newon apps you need

    Get the lifestyle apps you want from the store.

  3. 03
    Log in with your Newon account

    Open each app and sign in with the same account.

  4. 04
    Use several apps with one account

    You do not create a new account for every app.

A shared account is not a free membership.

Being able to log in with one account does not mean every paid feature is free. In Newon+ you can choose and manage subscription packages, and see which apps are included. Monthly and yearly prices are shown in the Newon+ app.

04 / LIFE AREAS

Four life areas.

The 13 apps sit in four life areas. Each app has a different job. You pick the ones you need and use them with the same account.

01

Set a goal, practice it, and keep the count.

Manage goals in GoalUp, log daily practice in OX MONTH, and track repeats in CountUp. The three apps handle goal setting, habit practice, and number tracking — different jobs.

GoalUp→OX MONTH→CountUp

This is an example of using the apps together. Goals and logs are not auto-sent between apps today.

GoalUp Goal setting · action plans · growth OX MONTH Habit log · monthly goals · daily check CountUp Count tracking · targets · progress

02

See money flow, manage bills, and practice saving.

Track income and spending in SAVY, check recurring bills in SubPing, and run saving challenges in PiggyUp. The three apps split spending logs, subscription dates, and saving habits.

SAVY→SubPing→PiggyUp

This shows how the services can be used together. Unconfirmed auto-sharing is not shown.

SAVY Income & spending · money habits SubPing Subscriptions · billing dates · reminders PiggyUp Saving goals · spending habits · challenges

03

Your health, then family and pets.

Pillmate covers medication and health logs. EatOn covers meals and eating. FitOn covers workouts and activity. BabyLog covers a child’s growth. PetLog covers a pet’s daily care. Choose the apps you need and use them with a Newon account.

These apps are not one auto data flow. Family sharing in BabyLog or PetLog is inside that app — not sharing across all Newon apps.

Pillmate Medication schedule · reminders · health log EatOn Meals · cooking · dining · healthy eating FitOn Workouts · sports · healthy activity BabyLog Parenting log · growth · family sharing PetLog Pet daily log · health · family sharing

04

Travel sits inside the same Newon account.

Record trips, maps, and memories in My World. Travel and daily apps share one Newon account. My World does not pull money, health, or family data from other apps.

My World Travel journal · travel map · memories

08 / ONGIL

From your day
to a parent’s day.

Ongil is Newon’s senior platform — daily life, living support, family connection, and finding local and care information. The aim is to connect what seniors and families need, step by step, on top of Newon’s daily-life apps.

  1. 01

    Medication and health

    Pillmate
  2. 02

    Daily goals and activity logs

    GoalUp CountUp OX MONTH
  3. 03

    Living costs and recurring bills

    SAVY SubPing
  4. 04

    Family life

    BabyLog PetLog My World

Planned BabyLog and PetLog are not senior-care apps. They are mentioned only because family members can each use the Newon apps they need. Ongil will add senior-specific features such as family communication, living support, and care information. App data is not auto-shared today, and care booking or payment is not available.
