BabyLog

Childcare logs · Growth · Family sharing · AI analysis

From pregnancy through teens—manage your child’s growth and daily life in one app.

Lihat proyek App Store Google Play

Ide

BabyLog is

an all-in-one parenting journal to record your child’s growth and routines—and share them with family.

From feeds, sleep, and diapers to growth, health, and learning, keep every data point organized in one place.

Beyond simple logs, BabyLog analyzes change and supports smarter day-to-day parenting.

Fitur utama

Log pengasuhan anak

Log feeds, sleep, diapers, and daily routines with lightweight inputs. Fast, intuitive entry helps you stay consistent.

Profil anak

Manage growth, health, and schedules per child. Supports multiple children with ease.

Pertumbuhan & kesehatan

Track height, weight, and wellbeing— and see trends at a glance.

Penjadwalan

Visits, vaccinations, classes, school— keep important dates in one calendar.

Statistics

Visualize routines and growth data so patterns are easy to understand.

Berbagi keluarga

Invite family to view and help manage logs together. Built for parents and caregivers in one space.

Masyarakat

Share experiences and learn from other parents. Ask questions and browse parent-friendly discussions.

Wawasan pengasuhan AI

From your logs—patterns and practical next steps. Premium tiers add deeper summaries from your routines.
