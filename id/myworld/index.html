MY WORLD

Every moment of travelling the world,
in one place.

From the first idea to planning, booking, experiences, records and sharing. MY WORLD aims to be a global travel platform that connects travelers, travel businesses and local tourism.

NOW

Today the MY WORLD app is a travel-record service: mark the countries and cities you have visited on a world map, and look back on trips through routes, photos, stats and an AI travel report. Booking, commerce and a shared community are being prepared step by step.

OUR VISION

Scattered travel moments,
in one connected flow.

  1. 01

    From the excitement of planning to new places and memories that last — a trip is one connected journey.

  2. 02

    Today discovery, booking, records and sharing are spread across many services. Starting from the world map and travel records, MY WORLD aims to connect that flow.

  3. 03

    Together with travel agencies, stay and tour operators, brands, creators, regions and institutions, we are building a structure that helps them meet travelers more precisely.

EXECUTION ROADMAP

Not everything at once —
we expand step by step.

Travel is highly competitive, and legal responsibility and supplier contracts matter. So we expand in a validated order.

We are in stage 1 today. Stage 2 onwards are future plans and timing is not fixed.

  1. 01

    Stage 1 Current stage

    Complete the travel-record app

    Stabilise the world map, visited countries, records, photos and stats, and grow the user base.

  2. 02

    Stage 2

    Planning & AI

    Itineraries, place discovery, budgets and planning assistance.

  3. 03

    Stage 3

    Booking & package partnerships

    Validate real booking demand through partnerships with qualified stay, tour, activity and package providers.

  4. 04

    Stage 4

    Commerce & community

    Travel-gear shopping, secondhand, group buying, reviews and content.

  5. 05

    Stage 5

    Business SaaS, public & global

    Booking and operations SaaS for travel businesses, corporate travel, tourism-body cooperation and global licensing.
