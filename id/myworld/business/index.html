BUSINESS THESIS

The moment a traveler opens the map,
the next trip begins.

A travel record looks back at past trips, and it is also where the next trip begins. Because MY WORLD is there at that moment, it has business opportunities that lead naturally into planning, booking and preparation.

But travel is an industry with heavy supplier contracts and legal responsibilities. So MY WORLD strengthens the record service through subscriptions first, and opens partnerships and listings one at a time, after contract and review.

REVENUE OPPORTUNITIES

Revenue opportunities,
and honest caveats.

MY WORLD's first revenue base is a personal subscription for people who want to go deeper with their travel records. The more you record, the more valuable the map, statistics and reports become.

The next opportunity comes when travelers make plans. We can connect flights, stays and experiences through external affiliates, and widen to direct listings from local stays, experiences and travel-goods sellers, a business subscription for travel businesses, and clearly labelled ads.

All of this is still at the design stage. No prices or fees have been set, and no partnership contracts have been signed. We will open these as real businesses only after checking travel-business registration, cancellation and refund responsibilities, payment structures and ad-labelling rules. In the base design, NEWON does not hold transaction funds.

HOW MONEY FLOWS

Transactions stay with suppliers;
NEWON provides connection and tools.

The principles of the transaction structure MY WORLD is designing.

  1. 01

    Travelers discover

    Travelers discover partner and listed products on MY WORLD's map and planning screens. Ads and partnerships are labelled.

  2. 02

    Direct with the supplier

    Booking and payment take place with the contracted supplier or external partner. We clearly show who is responsible for ticketing, booking and refunds.

  3. 03

    NEWON holds no funds

    In the base design, NEWON does not hold transaction funds between travelers and suppliers.

  4. 04

    How NEWON earns

    NEWON's direction is to earn from subscriptions, direct-listing commissions, external affiliate revenue and ads. The same transaction is never charged twice.

EXECUTION ROADMAP

Not everything at once,
but step by step.

Travel is a competitive industry where supplier contracts and legal responsibility matter. So we expand in a validated order.

We are at stage 1. Stage 2 onwards are business plans with no set timing.

  1. 01

    Stage 1 Current stage

    A complete travel record app

    Stabilising the home world map, travel records, photos and statistics, and preparing app releases.

  2. 02

    Stage 2

    V2.0 · planning and AI

    The five-tab structure, stronger itinerary and budget features, AI planning help.

  3. 03

    Stage 3

    Validating booking partnerships

    Opening the Book tab with eligible suppliers through external partnerships and validating real demand.

  4. 04

    Stage 4

    Listings and community

    Direct listings, travel goods, and a shared community after server integration.

  5. 05

    Stage 5

    Business, public and global

    Business subscription for travel businesses, business travel, tourism-body partnerships, global licences.

TRUST & COMPLIANCE

Review principles for
a trustworthy travel platform.

We review and follow these principles before expanding. The scope of services may change depending on the outcome.

  1. 01

    Registration & contracts

    We check the registration requirements and contract structures needed to introduce and connect travel products.

  2. 02

    Ticketing & booking

    We clearly show who is responsible for ticketing and booking.

  3. 03

    Cancellation & refunds

    Cancellation and refund terms are shown together with the supplier's policy.

  4. 04

    Insurance & safety

    Insurance is limited to information and official links; we do not sell or broker it.

  5. 05

    Privacy & location

    Travel records and location data are processed and protected only as far as needed.

  6. 06

    Community safety

    A shared community comes with reporting, blocking and moderation policies.

  7. 07

    Reviews & ad labelling

    Fake reviews and unlabelled ads are banned; partnerships and ads are clearly labelled.

  8. 08

    Transaction funds

    In the base design, NEWON does not hold transaction funds.
