COMMERCE INFRASTRUCTURE

Discovery in MY WORLD,
transactions on shared infrastructure.

  1. 01

    MY WORLD is where travel gear, secondhand items and group-buying products are explored and discovered.

  2. 02

    For general products, we are reviewing using SHAREON — Newon’s shared commerce infrastructure — for orders, payments, settlement and refunds. It is not connected today.

  3. 03

    We do not charge double commission on the same transaction.

  4. 04

    Flights, stays, travel packages and tours are reviewed separately, in line with supplier contracts and applicable law.

TRUST & COMPLIANCE

Principles we review to build
a travel platform people can trust.

These principles are reviewed and followed before any expansion. Service scope may change depending on the outcome.

  • 01

    Travel-business registration and contracts

    We review the registration and contract structure required to sell or broker travel products.

  • 02

    Ticketing and booking responsibility

    We make clear whether the supplier or the platform is responsible for ticketing and booking.

  • 03

    Cancellation and refunds

    Cancellation and refund rules for stays, tours and packages are shown together with supplier policies.

  • 04

    Insurance solicitation rules

    Insurance guidance is offered only after review of solicitation and brokerage rules.

  • 05

    Personal and location data

    Travel records and location data are handled only as far as needed, and protected.

  • 06

    Companion and community safety

    Companion and community features come with reporting, blocking and moderation policies.

  • 07

    Reviews, ads and partner labels

    No fake reviews or unlabelled ads; partners and ads are clearly labelled.

  • 08

    Available vs planned

    We always distinguish features available today from features that are planned.

This page introduces the MY WORLD business. The website does not offer booking, ordering, payment, sign-in, seller registration or record entry — the actual features live in the MY WORLD app.

Items marked “In preparation” or “Under review” are long-term plans. Timing and scope depend on supplier contracts, applicable law and service validation.
