NEWON LABS · AI · DISCOVERY

Beyond AI that generates answers —
can AI find problems worth building?

Ideas are plentiful.
Places to attach AI are plentiful too.

But whether a problem is worth solving,
whether AI is the better solution,
and whether the signal is strong enough to prototype
have to be asked separately.

AI Discovery is not an answer-generating AI.
It is an exploration experiment that structures market signals and user problems
to find opportunities worth building.

THE QUESTION

Can AI make the "what should we build?"
exploration process more systematic?

There are many ideas, but it is hard to judge which problems are worth solving. Attaching AI and finding the right problem are different jobs.

WHY THIS EXPERIMENT

How we frame the problem.

01

PROBLEM

Many product ideas — hard to tell which problems are actually worth solving.
AI possibility and problem value get mixed together.

02

HYPOTHESIS

Structuring market signals and problem patterns helps compare product candidates more systematically.
Separating AI fit can reduce the wrong builds.

03

EXPERIMENT
