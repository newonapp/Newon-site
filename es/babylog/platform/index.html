PUBLIC WELFARE & CHILD SAFETY

We put a child's safety
and rights first.

Eight directions, from public parenting support to child safety, protection, independence and verified giving, for connecting children and families in need with official information and institutions. Everything below is a plan and not a released service until real institution cooperation and operation are confirmed.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership, and it does not replace professional judgment, official reporting or legal procedures.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

25 · Government Parenting Support

The support programs you need,
accurately, through official channels.

A public-information area connecting central and local government programs for birth, parenting, childcare and family support, and their official application channels.

Key areas

  • Birth support
  • Childrearing support
  • Childcare fees
  • Family welfare
  • Local support programs
  • Official application channels
Official information sources
The health and welfare ministry, local governments, official government welfare and civil-service portals
What we do not do · conditions
BABYLOG does not decide eligibility or apply on anyone's behalf; it points to official application channels. Guidance on life-stage systems is divided with LIVON.
Current status
Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed
