07

Public Welfare & Child Safety

Public welfare & child safety

4 · 25–28

Child safety, protection and welfare are planned areas focused on official information and connection to official bodies. They do not replace professional judgment, official reporting or legal procedures, and are described according to the systems of the Republic of Korea.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

  1. 25

    Government Parenting Support

    A public-information area connecting government and local-authority birth, parenting, childcare and family support programs and their official application channels.

    Planned
  2. 26

    Planned
  3. 27

    Missing Children Information

    A public-interest area connecting official reporting bodies for missing children, prevention information and the official guidance channels of public institutions.

    Planned
  4. 28

    Child Protection & Abuse Prevention

    A public-interest information area connecting child abuse prevention, guardian education, the official reporting system and child protection agency information.

    Planned

How BABYLOG earns

Revenue Opportunities

BABYLOG is considering a range of revenue models, including individual premium subscriptions, parenting commerce, connections to qualified services, institution SaaS, corporate family-benefit solutions, brand advertising, public-sector contracts and global licensing.

  • 01Individual premium subscriptions
  • 02Parenting commerce
  • 03Qualified service connections
  • 04Institution SaaS
  • 05Corporate family-benefit solutions
  • 06Brand advertising
  • 07Public-sector contracts
  • 08Global licensing

What is never monetized

Public-interest information on child protection, missing children, abuse prevention, adoption and foster care is kept separate from commercial transactions, and the information or protection needs of vulnerable children are never monetized.
