01 · Business Areas

Six areas, one family platform.

Every area is at a pre-launch review stage. “Revenue model” below is a structure type under review, not a price.

NEWON does not do custom web or app development, contract design or operations outsourcing for individual clients. We focus on standardized in-house products, licenses, platforms and automation products.

Confirmed prices and fees are published only after they are actually launched and approved. Revenue structures on this page are business directions under review, not prices.

09

Child Safety & Social Impact

Child Safety & Social Impact

8 groups

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

In an emergency, use official channels first.

If a child is in danger or you suspect a child is missing or being abused, please use official reporting channels such as 112 (police) first, not this website. BABYLOG does not accept reports or identify a child’s location or identity.

Please do not write a child’s name, photo, current location or abuse-related details on this website or in the inquiry form.

Business method
A public-interest area centered on official information and connection to official bodies: free public information, official institution links, nonprofit cooperation, corporate social contribution, institutional information solutions, and public and local-government cooperation.
Revenue model
No monetization targets children or protected persons. Paid institution products and corporate social contribution products are reviewed separately from public-interest information services, and no confirmed fees exist.

These criteria apply to every group in this category.

Structures we do not use

  • Fees for missing-child reports or tips
  • Trading of child location data
  • Adoption brokerage fees
  • Selling protected children's personal data
  • Opaque monetization of donations

How to read the labels

  • In the app · per store listing
  • Direction · no release date
  • Under review
  • Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed

“In the app” covers only the BabyLog app features described in the store listing. “Direction” and “Under review” are not released features or services, and scope and timing may change.

“Plan” is the vision for the child safety and social impact area. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, it is not a released service or a confirmed partnership.

Priorities

Reviewed step by step,
in five stages.

The direction is to design the business broadly but to develop and launch in the order of completing core recording features and confirming user demand.

01

Stabilize the core app

Top priority. Feeding, sleep, growth and health records, usability, data stability, backup and recovery, validating subscription value.

02

Family management and parenting information

Shared family management, parenting schedules, and trusted parenting content and support information.

03

Connect parenting services

Validating demand for postpartum care, childcare, childcare centers and experiences, and early partnerships.

04

Commerce and business SaaS

Baby shopping and subscriptions, and standard operations products for care companies and institutions.

05

Corporate benefits, public and global

Corporate family benefits, local government cooperation, multilingual service and licensing.

The order is a direction and promises no schedule, scope or launch. The child safety and social impact area is reviewed separately at the stage where official cooperation and an operating system are confirmed.
