BabyLog

Every moment of family growth,
in one connection.

BABYLOG aims to be a comprehensive family platform connecting pregnancy and birth, parenting and education, a child's growth and everyday family life.

02 · Our Vision

From pregnancy to high-school graduation,
connecting a family's time.

  1. 01

    What a family needs while a child grows — records, information, education, services — changes with each stage.

  2. 02

    BABYLOG aims to connect these scattered moments so families can focus more on their child's growth.

  3. 03

    A parenting log app is available today; the direction beyond it is being reviewed step by step.

04 · What We Connect

Starting from records,
connecting four things.

BABYLOG aims for a structure where family, learning and services connect around one record.

01

Records & growth

Gather a child's daily life, growth and health history in order.

02

Family & guardians

Guardians and family see and continue the same records together.

03

Learning & education

Learning, reading and career management, and links to education content and institutions, are under review as direction.

04

Products & services

A way to explore parenting and education products and family services is under review.

Daily & Growth

Feeding, sleep, growth:
a daily record.

At the center of BABYLOG is recording a child's day.

01

Daily log

Organize a child's day: feeding, sleep, diapers, bowel movements, baths, temperature.

02

Feeding & nutrition

Record meals from breast milk and formula to baby food and toddler meals, and food reactions.

03

Sleep & routine

See sleep patterns, naps, bedtimes and daily habits at a glance.

04

Growth & development

Keep moments like height, weight and first steps. Growth charts are for reference, not medical diagnosis.

Health & Family

Health records,
shared with family.

Some of this is in the app today and some is direction. See the platform page for the difference.

01

Vaccination & health

Manage vaccination schedules, checkups, hospital visits, prescriptions and allergy records. For emergencies or diagnosis, we point to medical care.

02

Shared parenting

Parents, grandparents and co-caregivers share schedules and records. Access rights and guardian consent matter.

03

Family schedule

A direction for organizing parental leave, sharing care roles and care plans for working families.

04

Reference AI

Summarizes records and gives general information. It does not replace medical diagnosis or prescription.

CHILD SAFETY & SOCIAL IMPACT

So that tomorrow is safe
for every child.

BABYLOG is preparing a future that goes beyond helping families raise children: connecting official information and institutions for children who need protection and support. From missing-child prevention to child protection, support for care facilities and independence. We put the safety and rights of every single child first.

Explore child protection business → Partnership inquiry
  1. 01
    Missing-child prevention and official search support

    A direction to guide prevention rules and the search information and reporting channels published by official bodies.

  2. 02
    Child care facilities and protected children

    A direction to connect facility information and official sponsorship and volunteer programs.

  3. 03
    Official information on adoption and foster care

    A direction to guide public procedures, official bodies and counseling channels accurately.

  4. 04
    Sponsorship, volunteering and independence

    A direction to guide official sponsor organizations and support systems for youth leaving care.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

07 · NEWON Connection

Built the NEWON way,
with standard products.

BABYLOG is introduced under the same principles as the other platforms NEWON runs.

  1. 01
    In-house products first

    Standardized in-house products, licenses, platforms and automation products — not per-client outsourcing.

  2. 02
    Direction with ShareOn

    Person-to-person trading is being considered in connection with ShareOn. The principle is to design it so a single transaction is not charged fees twice. This is not a confirmed policy.

  3. 03
    Direction with LivOn and Ongil

    Links to life stages after childhood are considered only as a long-term direction.

  4. 04
    An information website

    The website only introduces and routes inquiries. Features are used in the app.

How to read the labels

  • In the app · per store listing
  • Direction · no release date
  • Under review
  • Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed

“In the app” covers only the BabyLog app features described in the store listing. “Direction” and “Under review” are not released features or services, and scope and timing may change.

“Plan” is the vision for the child safety and social impact area. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, it is not a released service or a confirmed partnership.
