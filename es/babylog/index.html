Every moment of growing up together,
in one place.

From the moment you plan a pregnancy through a child's birth and every stage of growth. BABYLOG is building an integrated parenting and family platform that connects parents and children, families and care institutions.

02 · About BABYLOG

An experience where every moment
of parenting connects.

  1. 01

    Raising a child takes countless records, choices, information and preparation. From week-by-week pregnancy information to birth preparation, feeding and sleep, growth and health, education and care, and the family's schedule, the things parents manage never stop.

  2. 02

    BABYLOG aims to connect these experiences on one platform. So that parents can spend more meaningful time with their children, it aims for a digital ecosystem that brings together the records, information, everyday services and institution links a family needs.

  3. 03

    A parenting log app is available today; the direction beyond it is being reviewed step by step.

04 · Our Mission

Less burden on parents,
more precious time with children.

BABYLOG aims to go beyond simple records to a service that helps the everyday life of parents and families.

01

Structured growth records

An experience of recording a child's growth systematically, gathering daily life, growth and health history in order.

02

Family-centered care

Family-centered management where parents and guardians take part together, viewing and continuing the same records.

03

Information and links by stage

Information and services matched to each growth stage, and a platform that connects the parenting industry and specialist institutions.

04

Information for safe growth

Easy access to official information so every child can grow up safe and healthy.

Core Experience

What a family needs,
in one place.

The core areas BABYLOG connects. Some are in the app today and some are direction; see the platform page for the difference.

01

Daily records

Daily records such as feeding, sleep, diapers and growth, down to bowel movements, baths, temperature, naps and bedtimes, organized in the app.

02

Health & growth

Health, vaccination and development information: vaccination schedules, checkups and growth changes. Growth charts are for reference, not medical diagnosis.

03

Family sharing

Shared parenting for parents and guardians, who see the same records and schedules; access rights and guardian consent matter.

04

Parenting information

Parenting information suited to age and situation. A reference AI summarizes records and gives general information; it does not replace diagnosis or prescription.

Family First

Parenting is not done alone;
it is done together as a family.

A child's day is not completed by one person's records. BABYLOG aims for an experience where parents, guardians and family members each look after a child's growth together and stay in touch.

01

Shared parenting

Parents, grandparents and co-caregivers check and continue a child's records and schedules together.

02

Permissions per guardian

Permissions for each guardian matter as much as convenience. The principle is that the family decides who sees which records.

03

Family schedules and roles

A direction for organizing hospital and vaccination schedules, parental leave, care roles and care plans for working families.

04

A child's personal data

BABYLOG cares not only about a family's convenience but also about protecting a child's personal data.

05 · Beyond Parenting

Beyond parenting records,
toward a bigger connection for families.

BABYLOG aims to grow into a platform ecosystem that connects family users with parenting brands, education and care institutions, companies and public bodies. Starting from everyday family life, it aims to create wider value, from the operations and services of the parenting industry to child welfare and safety.

01

Families

Parents, guardians and families raising children. The central users who record, share and look for information.

02

Parenting Brands

Brands and sellers of baby goods, baby food and family lifestyle products, bringing families the goods and information they need.

03

Education & Care

Daycare centers, kindergartens, care institutions and education experts: partners for guardian communication and institution operations.

04

Companies & Public

Companies running employee family benefits, and government, local governments and public bodies: partners for family benefits and public parenting support information.

Lifestyle & Services

Toward parenting lifestyle
and community.

Information on baby goods, education and care services and a parent community are directions under review. The website does not offer purchasing, booking or community.

01

Shopping

Commerce for exploring goods and brands such as baby goods, children's furniture, clothing and food. Second-hand items needing safety checks are restricted separately.

02

Care

Connecting information on qualified providers of postpartum care, postpartum helpers and childcare. Provider qualifications and requirements are checked.

03

Education

Play, reading and education content by age, and parent education, based on expert cooperation and verified sources.

04

Community

A space for parents and families to share information and experience. Protection of children's photos and personal data comes first.

CHILD SAFETY & SOCIAL IMPACT

So that tomorrow is safe
for every child.

BABYLOG is preparing a future that goes beyond helping families raise children: connecting official information and institutions for children who need protection and support. Public-interest information on child protection, missing children, abuse prevention, adoption and foster care is kept separate from commercial transactions and centers on free access to information and links to official bodies.

Explore public welfare and child safety → Partnership inquiry
  1. 01
    Child safety and missing-child prevention

    A direction to guide everyday safety, accident and missing-child prevention information and the reporting channels official bodies publish.

  2. 02
    Child protection and abuse prevention

    A direction to connect abuse prevention education, official reporting systems and child protection agency information.

  3. 03
    Official foster care and adoption information

    A direction to guide official systems, procedures and responsible bodies accurately, with no private matching or transactions.

  4. 04
    Independence support and verified giving

    A direction to connect support systems for youth leaving care with verified public-interest bodies and corporate social contribution.

Child safety and social impact is a vision and plan. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, nothing here is shown as a released service or a confirmed partnership, and it does not replace professional judgment, official reporting or legal procedures.

The laws and support systems described in this area are those of the Republic of Korea and may differ in other countries.

07 · NEWON Connection

Built the NEWON way,
with standard products.

BABYLOG is a core NEWON platform and is introduced under the same principles as the other platforms NEWON runs.

  1. 01
    In-house products first

    Standardized in-house SaaS, platforms, licenses and automation products — not per-client outsourcing.

  2. 02
    Roles divided with SHAREON

    Sharing and renting baby goods and person-to-person trading are being considered in connection with SHAREON, with roles kept separate. The principle is to design it so a single transaction is not charged fees twice. This is not a confirmed policy.

  3. 03
    Roles divided with LIVON and ONGIL

    Guidance on life-stage systems such as childbirth support and parental leave is divided with LIVON, and links to life stages after childhood are considered only as a long-term direction.

  4. 04
    Roles of the web and the app

    The website introduces the brand, services, business and partnerships and routes inquiries; actual recording and management are used in the app.

How to read the labels

  • In the app · per store listing
  • Direction · no release date
  • Under review
  • Plan · institution cooperation not yet confirmed

“In the app” covers only the BabyLog app features described in the store listing. “Direction” and “Under review” are not released features or services, and scope and timing may change.

“Plan” is the vision for the public welfare, child safety and social impact areas. Until real institution cooperation and service operation are confirmed, it is not a released service or a confirmed partnership.
