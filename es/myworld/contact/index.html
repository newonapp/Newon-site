FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

  1. Q1
    Can I book or buy on the website?

    No. The website is a business introduction. Booking, ordering, payment and sign-in are not offered on the web.

  2. Q2
    What can I use in the app today?

    World map, visited countries and cities, routes, photos and notes, travel stats, the AI travel report, and itinerary, checklist and expense records. Some features may be premium.

  3. Q3
    When will flight, stay and package booking start?

    We plan to introduce them step by step after partnerships with qualified providers and legal review. Timing is not fixed.

  4. Q4
    What are the prices and fees?

    Subscription prices and partnership or sales fees are not final. They will be announced once confirmed.

  5. Q5
    How do I delete my account and data?

    See the account deletion page for the steps and how data is handled.

  6. Q6
    How is it different from SHAREON?

    MY WORLD focuses on travel records, planning, booking, and buying and reselling travel gear; SHAREON focuses on sharing and renting items, equipment and spaces. The two services are not connected today.

This page introduces the MY WORLD business. The website does not offer booking, ordering, payment, sign-in, seller registration or record entry — the actual features live in the MY WORLD app.
