BUSINESS STRUCTURE

Everyone a provider,
everyone a renter.

The six axes describe the business structure and how it earns; they do not mean everything is built.

  • 01

    C2C

    Person to person

    People lend their things or equipment to other people.

  • 02

    B2C

    Business to person

    Businesses rent equipment, spaces and items to people.

  • 03

    C2B

    Person to business

    Businesses rent equipment or items owned by people.

  • 04

    B2B

    Business to business

    Companies rent equipment, spaces and resources to each other.

  • 05

    Marketplace

    One marketplace

    Listing, search, booking, payment, deposits, settlement, reviews.

  • 06

    SaaS

    Operating software

    Inventory, booking, rental, returns, customers and operations.

REVENUE MODEL

Six revenue streams.

Fee rates and prices are not decided. Basic listing and renting are meant to work without a subscription.

#StreamFromHow it earns
01Transaction feePeople, businessesFee on completed rentals
02Business SaaSRental companies, businessesMonthly or annual subscription
03Premium sellersPeople, businessesAdvanced listing and management tools
04Ads and promotionRental companies, brandsPaid placement and ads (always labelled)
05Business contractsCompaniesBulk rental and asset-sharing solutions
06Public and globalInstitutions, partners abroadContracts and licences

Deposits are not platform revenue and are kept separate from it.

EXECUTION ROADMAP

Rentals first,
categories second.

Rather than adding categories quickly, SHAREON first builds enough items and renters in a specific area and category.

  1. 01

    Core marketplace First

    Registration for people and businesses, search, rental requests, chat, my page and a stable basic flow.

  2. 02

    Safe rentals

    Validating payment, deposits, returns, condition checks, reviews and dispute policy.

  3. 03

    Key categories

    Expanding from high-demand categories: household goods, electronics, cameras, camping, spaces.

  4. 04

    Businesses and B2B

    Professional rental companies, corporate asset sharing and business transactions.

  5. 05

    SaaS, public and global

    Operating software for rental businesses, public sharing projects, localisation and licensing abroad.

Where it stands: stage 1 is in pilot development in the app.

FOCUS

Focused on sharing and rental.

Of the 20 areas first considered, the six furthest from sharing and rental were set aside from SHAREON’s core business. No app feature is deleted by this; it sets sharing and rental as the centre of the business.

  1. 01

    An open sharing and rental marketplace

    A marketplace dedicated to sharing and rental, where people and businesses lend and borrow things, equipment and spaces directly.

  2. 02

    Rental operating SaaS for businesses

    SHAREON’s own standard product for rental companies to manage bookings, inventory, customers and settlement.

  3. 03

    Start small, go deep

    As a two-sided marketplace, SHAREON first builds supply and renters in a specific area and category, then widens.
