SHAREON

SHAREON Overview — Sin tenerlo en propiedad.
Justo cuando lo necesitas.

A way to use what you need sensibly, and to add new value to assets you are not using. SHAREON aims to be an open marketplace that connects people and businesses to share and rent physical assets.

WHAT IS SHAREON

Trading the right to use,
not ownership.

SHAREON is a sharing and rental platform where neighbourhood individuals and businesses lend and borrow things, spaces and equipment for just the period needed. Its first focus is small-item rental between nearby neighbours; buying and selling second-hand goods or running a general shopping mall is not SHAREON’s core business. What changes hands is the right to use something for a time, not ownership.

  1. 01

    You have to buy things you use once or twice a year

    Even things needed briefly, such as camping gear, a power drill or baby gear, usually have to be bought, and then they take up storage space. SHAREON aims to ease this with a rental flow for borrowing by the day or week and giving things back.

  2. 02

    Lending between neighbours lacks clear promises and responsibility

    With noticeboard posts or lending between friends, the schedule, security and return check are left to the people involved. SHAREON brings booking, pick-up and return records, reviews and disputes into one flow, and prepares to handle money through a licensed payment provider.

  3. 03

    Equipment and spaces at companies often sit idle

    Filming kits, meeting rooms and office laptops spend long periods unused. Handling rentals between people, business rentals and internal asset sharing on one platform is the long-term direction.

CORE VALUES

Three values
SHAREON holds to.

Before adding categories, we build trades in which even strangers in the same neighbourhood can lend and return with confidence.

  1. 01

    Only as much as you need

    Instead of buying, you borrow the right to use something for a period. Lenders set the fee, rental unit and availability themselves, and basic listing and renting are meant to work without a subscription.

  2. 02

    Close to home

    The starting point is small items handed over within a neighbourhood. Exact addresses are not shown before a trade, and contact and location details are used only as far as a trade needs.

  3. 03

    A trade ends when it comes back

    A rental is finished only when the item is back. Condition checks at pick-up and return, reviews, reporting and blocking, and dispute handling are designed before extra screens.

WHO IT IS FOR

Neighbours who borrow, neighbours who lend,
and businesses with assets.

Anyone can be both a borrower and a lender. Trades involving a business open only after business verification.

    People who borrow now and then

    Someone going camping for the first time, moving or fixing up a home, or needing something for a child for a short while. They borrow for the period needed instead of buying.

TRADE STRUCTURES

From lender to borrower:
four trade structures.

C2C, B2C, C2B and B2B describe who lends to whom. They are not all operating; the starting point is small-item rental between people.

Borrower →PersonBusiness

Lender ↓PersonBusiness

  • C2C

    Person → person

    A neighbour borrows something another neighbour owns. This is the trade SHAREON focuses on first, centred on small items such as camping gear, tools and baby gear.

  • C2B

    Person → business

    A business borrows an individual’s equipment, such as a camera kit, for work or an event. This is a rental with an individual as supplier, not a wanted or request board.

  • B2C

    Business → person

    Small businesses and companies with equipment or spaces, such as camera rental shops or space operators, lend to individuals. Opens after business verification.

  • B2B

    Business → business

    A business lends assets such as event or filming equipment to another business. Bulk and long-term rentals are reviewed with separate contract terms.

  • 05

    Internal

    Internal asset sharing

    Employees and departments borrowing laptops, equipment and meeting rooms inside a company. Kept apart from external paid trades and planned as a business SaaS subscription rather than a per-trade fee.

  • 06

    Free Sharing

    Free lending

    Lending to neighbours without a rental fee. No platform fee is planned for free lending, and the same trust tools, such as return checks and reviews, apply.

LONG-TERM VISION

From neighbourhood items
to company assets.

SHAREON’s medium- to long-term direction is to take a rental flow proven in one neighbourhood into spaces, business assets and partnerships. All of it is at the planning stage.

  1. 01

    Small-item rental nearby

    First confirm that lending and returning actually complete end to end in one neighbourhood and a few categories.
