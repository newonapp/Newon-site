SHAREON

SHAREON Platform — Find the assets you need,
connect the value of what you own.

SHAREON aims to be a sharing and rental marketplace where people and businesses list physical assets and rent them for the period needed. The eighteen service areas in preparation, and the rental system that lets each rental end safely.

A · RENTAL GOODS

Item rental

Things needed now and then but costly to buy. The rule is to start with items that are unlikely to be lost or damaged and easy to hand over in person.

01Everyday Rental

Everyday items

Borrow things used now and then at home, such as vacuum cleaners, pressure washers and moving tools, just for the day needed. The focus is on simple items that neighbours can hand over in person.

  • Cleaning equipment
  • Pressure washers
  • Moving tools
  • Home appliances

To checkA list of parts and basic instructions are collected at listing.

02Electronics

Electronics & IT

Borrow valuable devices such as laptops, tablets, projectors, monitors and game consoles briefly for trips, events or trying before buying.

  • Laptops
  • Tablets
  • Projectors
  • Monitors
  • Game consoles

To checkDevices need resetting so no personal data remains, plus condition checks at pick-up and return.

03Camera & Media

Camera, filming & audio

Borrow cameras, lenses, lights, microphones and audio equipment around a shoot. Individual creators and small production teams are the main users, and C2B trades, where a business borrows an individual’s kit, are common.

  • Cameras
  • Lenses
  • Lighting
  • Microphones
  • Audio

To checkEquipment condition and damage terms need to be agreed before the trade.

04Outdoor & Camping

Camping & outdoor gear

Borrow seasonal gear such as tents, chairs, tables and sleeping bags for short periods. One of the categories considered for an early neighbourhood pilot.

  • Tents
  • Camping chairs
  • Tables
  • Sleeping bags
  • Lanterns

To checkCleaning, drying and missing parts are checked on return.

05Sports & Hobby

Sports & hobby gear

Borrow skis and boards, golf gear, exercise equipment and hobby kit needed for a season or a trial. Suited to trying a new hobby before committing.

  • Skis & boards
  • Golf gear
  • Exercise equipment
  • Hobby kit

To checkProtective gear, safe condition and conditions of use must be explained.

Home, events & family

Things needed at particular moments: home repairs, events, children, pets and outfits. Safety and hygiene rules differ by item.

    Party & event supplies

    Baby & family gear

    • Pushchairs
    • Cots
    • Baby equipment
    • Family items

  • 09Pet Equipment

    Pet equipment

    • Carriers
    • Pens

    • Travel items

    Clothing & accessories

    Borrow suits, dresses, event outfits, bags and accessories for the day of an event.

    • Suits
    • Dresses

C · SPACES

Spaces

Here you borrow time rather than an object. Use, permits and facility rules must be checked first, so spaces come after item rental.

11Space Rental

Spaces & facilities

Book meeting rooms, filming spaces, rehearsal rooms and event spaces by the hour or day. Space operators may also list services they provide with the space; general staffing or labour matching is not part of SHAREON.

  • Meeting rooms
  • Filming spaces
  • Rehearsal rooms
  • Event spaces

To checkPermitted use, fire, safety and business rules, and the operator’s rights (including subletting) must be checked.

12Storage & Parking

Storage & parking sharing

Lend spare storage or an empty parking space for the period needed. Rules and running costs differ from item rental, so separate contract terms are being considered.

  • Storage space
  • Storerooms
  • Parking spaces

To checkLegal, insurance and permit review comes first, so this may be left out of the first launch.

D · BUSINESS ASSETS

Business assets

Lending company equipment to outsiders and sharing assets inside a company are designed with separate pricing and permissions.

  • 13

    Business Assets

    Business equipment & assets

    Companies lend filming, event and work equipment to individuals (B2C) or to other businesses (B2B). Bulk and long-term rentals need separate contract terms and business management tools. Trades involving a business open after business verification.

  • 14

    Internal Asset Sharing

    Internal asset management

    Employees and departments book and return laptops, equipment and meeting rooms inside a company. Kept apart from external paid trades and planned as a SaaS subscription without per-trade fees; the menu is designed to appear only to authorised users.

Trust & operations

The foundation every area shares: free lending, trust tools, business operating tools and partnerships.

  • 15Free Sharing

    • Dispute process

    Partners, insurance, delivery & global

    Areas that need outside companies: delivery and collection, insurance and guarantees, overseas expansion. Considered only once the rules and contract structure are confirmed; there are no contracted partners today.

RENTAL FLOW

From listing to settlement,
designed in seven steps.

This is the rental flow SHAREON has designed. The app has not launched, and each step is at a different stage of development.

  1. 01List

    List

    Add an item with photos, rental fee, deposit terms, rental unit and availability. Location data is removed from photos at upload.

  2. 02Discover & Chat

    Discover & chat

    Search by area, price, deposit, period and quantity, and ask about condition and hand-over in chat before booking. Exact addresses are not shown before a trade.

  3. 03Book

    Book

    Pick dates and send a request; the lender reviews and approves it. The first launch starts with approval-based booking; instant booking, confirmed automatically when conditions are met, comes later.

  4. 04Pay

    Pay — not operating

    Rental fees and deposits are to be handled through a payment provider (PG). NEWON does not hold funds itself, and payment stays off until a PG contract and legal review are complete.

  5. 05Pick up

    Pick up

    Meet, hand over and check condition and parts. Photo records at pick-up still need development; photos are kept with both parties’ consent for a set period and are never used to decide damage liability automatically.

  6. 06Return

    Return

    After a return reminder or an extension request, the item comes back and is compared with its pick-up condition. Handling no-shows and non-returns is at the design stage.

  7. 07Confirm & Settle

    Confirm & settle — not operating

    If all is well, the trade is confirmed and ends with the deposit returned, settlement and reviews. For disputes, a process of report → settlement on hold → operator review is being designed; settlement is off together with payment.

Steps with a dashed marker (Pay, Confirm & settle) will not operate until a payment-provider contract and legal review are complete. Rentals that need a deposit stay closed until the payment provider’s deposit method is confirmed.

USER SCENARIOS

One rental,
followed role by role.

Design scenarios showing how the app is meant to be used. Payment, deposit and settlement steps are not operating.

  • Borrower — camping gear for two days

    Find camping gear nearby in Explore and ask about the parts in chat. Pick dates and send a request; the lender approves, and you check condition when you meet. After a return reminder, hand it back and leave a review.

  • Lender — an unused tool set

    In List, add photos, parts, a fee and the days it is available. Approve or decline incoming requests, and check condition on return against the pick-up record.

  • Rental shop — listing part of its filming stock

    A verified rental shop lists some of its stock to reach neighbours and creators. Managing bookings across many items is meant for the business operating tools.

  • Company — sharing office laptops and meeting kit

    An administrator registers internal assets and only authorised employees book and return them. Kept apart from external trades with no per-trade fee; this is a future plan.

  • Neighbour — lending a ladder for free

    Post it as free lending; the return check and review follow the same flow as a paid rental.

PRIVACY, SAFETY & TRUST

Privacy, safety
and trust principles.

Because strangers hand things to each other, the rules come before new features.

  • 01

    Verified parties

    Identity and business verification status is shown, and trades involving a business open after business verification.

  • 02

    Right to lend & prohibited items

    Ownership or permission to lend is checked, and dangerous, illegal or regulated items are restricted. Vehicles and some medical and safety equipment are excluded until legal and insurance review.

  • 03

    Records as evidence, decisions by process

    Pick-up and return photos are kept with consent for a set period; damage liability is never fixed automatically but handled through evidence and appeal.

  • 04

    Money through the payment provider

    NEWON does not hold funds. Deposits are not revenue and are never deducted without legal or contractual grounds.

  • 05

    Only the personal data needed

    Exact addresses and contact details are used only as far as a trade needs, and photo location data is removed.

The ten parts of SHAREON Trust

  • Identity & business checks

    Verification status is shown.

  • Item checks

    Ownership or permission to lend is confirmed.

  • Prohibited items

    Dangerous and illegal items cannot be listed.

  • Deposits

    Terms set by category and risk. Not operating today.

  • Condition records

    Pick-up and return photos. Still needs development.

  • Reviews

    Left by users who completed a trade.

  • Report & block

    Users and items can be reported and blocked.

  • Disputes

    Evidence and appeals are taken in.

  • Settlement hold

    Handled in line with contract and legal requirements.

  • Personal data

    Contact and location details are protected.

Deposit holding, insurance and compensation are not offered today, and no protection or compensation is promised. Paid placements are always labelled as ads.

GLOBAL EXPANSION

Services to take abroad,
and how each country is localised.

The ‘Global expansion’ item in our future plans, broken down by service. The app has not launched and no feature is offered abroad; everything below is under review.

  • Neighbourhood first

    Local sharing and rental

    In each country, small-item rental between people is proven first within a city or local community, using the same single listing, booking and return flow as at home.

  • Local categories

    Categories that fit local life

    What people borrow most depends on housing, seasons and spending habits. From the 18 areas, the categories, rental units and hand-over methods that suit each country are chosen.

  • Trust, localised

    Trust tools fitted to local rules

    Identity checks, reviews, trade records, dispute handling and deposit conditions are adjusted to each country’s rules. Deposits are never counted as revenue in any country.

  • Businesses & spaces

    Extending to businesses and spaces

    Business equipment rental, space sharing and business asset management follow only in countries where rental between people has taken hold.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

Can I use the SHAREON app now?

Not yet. The app is in development and is not in the app stores. Once a launch is confirmed it will be announced on this page.

Is it a service for buying and selling things?

No. SHAREON is about borrowing for a set period and giving things back. Selling used or new goods is not its core business, and the first launch is being prepared around rental.

Does SHAREON keep the deposit?

No. Deposits are not platform revenue and are left out of fee calculations. Rentals that need a deposit will not open until a deposit method through the payment provider is confirmed.

How much is the fee?

It has not been decided. Category-based rate options are under review but are not approved, so they are not published. Free lending and moving assets inside a company are meant to carry no transaction fee.

What happens if something gets damaged?

A dispute process based on pick-up and return records, evidence, appeals and holding settlement is being designed, and the rules will be fixed in the terms after legal review. No insurance or compensation is offered today.

Are there things I cannot list?

Dangerous, illegal or regulated items are planned to be blocked. Vehicles, some medical and safety equipment and parking spaces may be excluded until legal and insurance review.

Can businesses lend or borrow too?

Trades involving a business (B2C, C2B, B2B) open only after business verification. Rental shops and space operators join directly without a separate partnership contract, and business users are designed to switch to a business role in the same app.

Can I lend something for free?

Yes. Free lending is one of the areas. It is meant to carry no platform fee, and return checks and reviews follow the same flow as paid rentals.

Do pick-up and return photos decide who is responsible?

No. Photos are records kept with both parties’ consent for a set period and are never used to decide damage liability automatically. Responsibility is handled through evidence and an appeal process. The photo record feature still needs development.

Can I book on the website?

No. The website introduces SHAREON and takes inquiries; listing and booking will be offered only in the app once it launches.
