PilotNotice: Building on physical-asset sharing and rental, SHAREON is preparing to expand into trades between people and businesses, business solutions and many kinds of asset use. Some of the businesses and features introduced here are part of future development or operating plans; before the app launches, real rentals, booking confirmation, payment, deposits and settlement are not operating.

INQUIRY TYPES

Choose
an inquiry type.

01–06 are usage and trade inquiries, such as using the app or joining and renting as a person or business; 07–10 are business and partnership inquiries, such as transaction infrastructure, business solutions, institutions and strategic alliances. Each type opens NEWON’s official inquiry form or official email; no sign-up is needed.

  • Usage & trade · 01

    Using the app

    Questions about using the app and technical topics. The app is not in the app stores yet.

    Inquiry form — Using the app → Email — Using the app
  • Usage & trade · 02

    Personal listing and renting

    How to lend or borrow assets as an individual. Basic listing is meant to work without a subscription.

    Inquiry form — Personal listing and renting → Email — Personal listing and renting
  • Usage & trade · 03

    Business joining and renting

    Asset listing and renting for rental companies and businesses, who sign up directly without a partnership contract.

    Inquiry form — Business joining and renting → Email — Business joining and renting
  • Usage & trade · 04

    Space rental

    Space operators, renting spaces and listing ancillary services offered with the use of a space. A focus area for the first launch.

    Inquiry form — Space rental → Email — Space rental
  • Usage & trade · 05

    Professional equipment

    Industrial, production and office equipment rental, with qualifications, safety training and insurance considered.

    Inquiry form — Professional equipment → Email — Professional equipment
  • Usage & trade · 06

    Returns and disputes

    Feedback on returns, inspection and dispute rules, which will be fixed with the terms of use after legal review.

    Inquiry form — Returns and disputes → Email — Returns and disputes
  • Business & partnership · 07

    Payment, settlement and deposit infrastructure

    Work with qualified operators and feedback on payment, settlement and deposits. Payments and deposits are not operating yet.

    Inquiry form — Payment, settlement and deposit infrastructure → Email — Payment, settlement and deposit infrastructure
  • Business & partnership · 08

    Business solutions and SaaS

    Considering SHAREON Business SaaS and business asset-use solutions. A long-term direction; no product has been released.

    Inquiry form — Business solutions and SaaS → Email — Business solutions and SaaS
  • Business & partnership · 09

    Public bodies

    Sharing projects with local governments and public bodies, reviewed under each body’s procurement and contract process.

    Inquiry form — Public bodies → Email — Public bodies
  • Business & partnership · 10

    Strategic, technology and global alliances

    Strategic business alliances, technology partnerships, expansion abroad and licensing, checked against local laws and licence terms.

    Inquiry form — Strategic, technology and global alliances → Email — Strategic, technology and global alliances

BEFORE YOU WRITE

Please check
before you write.

Privacy Policy →

  • Include only what we need to reply: your name, email and your question.

  • Never include card numbers, account passwords, ID numbers or other sensitive payment or personal information.

  • SHAREON inquiries are received only through NEWON’s official inquiry form and official email (newon@newon.app).

  • The app is in pilot development, so there is no customer support for real rentals, bookings, payments or refunds.

  • Fee rates, prices and launch timing are not decided yet, so we cannot give specific figures or dates in reply to an inquiry.

  • Response times depend on the inquiry; we cannot guarantee a reply time.
