PETLOG

Turning a pet's every day
into a lifelong record.

PETLOG is NEWON's app for recording and managing your pet's daily life and health. We are building it step by step, aiming for a premium app you can trust for years.

Today the PETLOG app centres on the pet profile, daily log, health records, vaccinations and medication, and family sharing. A market, booking and payments are not offered yet; this website is for information and inquiries only.

WHY PETLOG

Pets can't tell us when they feel unwell.
So their records have to speak for them.

“When did she start eating less?” Asked this at the vet, many owners search their memory. Records scattered across photo albums, notes apps, receipts and family chats rarely give a clear answer.

In many homes one person walks the dog, another gives the medicine and someone else orders the food. Care is shared, but the information never ends up in one place.

PETLOG starts from that gap: making small daily records easy to keep and easy to find the moment you need them. We believe that is where better care begins.

MISSION

Making records effortless,
so owners can care better.

  1. 01

    A record should take a few taps. Only then can it be kept up on busy days and build up over years.

  2. 02

    What builds up should be easy to read. Weight changes, vaccination dates and medication history come first, where owners need them.

  3. 03

    Everyone in the family should see the same information and share the care. A history of who did what closes the gaps.

  4. 04

    Records always belong to the owner. We don't diagnose, we don't sell records, and we never share them without consent.

A DAY WITH PETLOG

An ordinary day,
held together by records.

A day sketched with the record, health and family features available in the PETLOG app today.

  1. 07:30

    Morning walk

    A quick note on the walk and toilet. Who took the walk is recorded too.

  2. 08:10

    Breakfast

    Log what was served and what was left. A change in appetite is often the first sign of a health change.

  3. 13:00

    Medication check

    Anyone in the family can see whether today's medicine was given, so doses are not missed or doubled.

  4. 16:20

    Vet visit

    Record the visit and the next vaccination date, and show the vet the history straight away.

  5. 20:00

    Weight and condition

    Regular weight entries show the trend at a glance.

  6. 22:30

    Today's photo

    One photo and a short note a day slowly become a precious life record.

An illustrative example. Times and routines differ in every home.

OUR PRINCIPLES

What we keep first,
on the way to a premium app.

For PETLOG, premium means dependable basics rather than flashy features.

  • Records belong to owners

    Pet records and personal data are never shared automatically with sellers or businesses. Sharing happens only when the owner chooses it.

  • Essential health records stay affordable

    Essential health records such as vaccinations, vet visits and medication are not over-charged.

  • We don't diagnose

    PETLOG organises records; it does not diagnose illness or replace a vet's judgement. For health concerns, please contact a vet.

  • Stability and security first

    App stability, data security and backup come before new features.

  • Payments and booking after review

    Booking and payments open only after legal, payment-gateway (PG) and security review.

  • No trade in living animals

    Trading live animals is not allowed, and regulated items such as animal medicines stay off the platform until legal review.

  • We only say what is confirmed

    No unsigned partnership, undecided price or user number is shown.

LONG-TERM DIRECTION

From a record app
to an integrated pet platform.

PETLOG grows over the long term. First we make today's record app solid; then we widen it into a platform that connects individual owners and pet businesses.

01

Now — the basics of records and care

Profile, daily log, health records, vaccinations and medication, family sharing. Strengthening stability, security and backup, and improving the Home and Care screens.

02

Next — market and a booking pilot

A pet goods market, a grooming, hotel and training booking pilot, and seller checks. Payments come only with NEWON's shared payment infrastructure and legal and security review.

03

Long term — owners and businesses together

Care, social, market, booking, family and adoption, business, AI and global: an integrated pet platform where vets, groomers, hotels, sitters, trainers and sellers meet owners.

This is a long-term direction, not current operations. The order and scope of each stage may change after review.
