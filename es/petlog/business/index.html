ROADMAP

Step by step,
following real demand.

This is the plan, not current operations; no unconfirmed partnership, revenue or user number is shown.

01

Stage 1 — Complete PETLOG (top priority)

First backup and restore, security and app stability, then higher quality for records, health and family care. After that, user growth and validating subscriptions.

02

Stage 2 — Professional services

Vet information, demand research for grooming, hotel and daycare booking, and preparing a small booking pilot. Payment connects only after legal, payment-provider contract and security review.

03

Stage 3 — PETLOG Business

Validating booking, CRM, customer and pet management SaaS and enterprise solutions in a small pilot, and refining the business screens and tools.

04

Stage 4 — Commerce and community

Popular categories, a group-buy marketplace with seller checks, community and short video, and education content.

05

Stage 5 — Public and global

Lawful insurance partnerships, public bodies and local government, multilingual localisation and global licensing.

TRUST

Legal and trust principles

  1. 01PETLOG does not diagnose disease or replace a vet's judgment.
  2. 02Vet booking, advertising and brokerage models follow legal review.
  3. 03Insurance solicitation, brokerage and sales only with proper licences and contracts.
  4. 04No trading of live animals, no unverified person-to-person adoption brokering, and no illegal breeding or sales.
  5. 05Provider checks, liability, safety and insurance are built into care services.
  6. 06Only licensed pet funeral providers are listed.
  7. 07Showing official public information is not the same as being an official partner.
  8. 08Owners' records, personal and location data are never shared with businesses or sellers without consent.
  9. 09Sellers are checked before selling, and regulated items such as animal drugs are not sold pending legal review.
  10. 10Booking and payment do not run before legal, payment-provider contract and security review, and are never offered on the web.
