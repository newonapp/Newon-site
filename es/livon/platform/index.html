How LIVON works

From your situation
to your record.

Every LIVON area moves through the same five steps. Finishing one life event becomes the starting point for the next.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Understand

    Pick your life stage and the event ahead. What you choose matters more than your age.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Explore

    See guides and official schemes from every related area in one flow, each scheme with its source and date checked.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Plan

    Turn it into to-dos, dates and checklists — planned to be stored only on your device.

  4. 04

    Connect

    Connect

    Applications go to official channels; living services, later, to checked partners. Ads are always labelled.

  5. 05

    Record

    Record

    Keep what is done and what comes next, and carry it into the next life event.

How LIVON is meant to be used, across six events — with what the first app will include kept apart from what comes later.

Preparing for a first job

For · Someone in their 20s about to graduate

Open the job topics in Life Stage, read the CV, interview and contract guides, check youth employment schemes on the official portal and note application dates in My Life. After starting, move on to a first salary, year-end tax and social insurance.

App Coming Soon
Guides, official portal links and on-device dates
Later (planned)
Application reminders, AI interview practice, related certificates

Related areas 05 Career, 06 Education, 20 Certification, 08 Welfare, 03 Finance

Moving out

For · A new worker renting for the first time

Read how to find a home and what to check before signing, check deposit protection and youth housing support, list the move, utilities and registration as a checklist, and get pointed to SHAREON for things you only need briefly. Note the lease end date.

Housing and moving guides, official portals, on-device notes
Later (planned)
Moving and cleaning services (after provider checks), lease-end reminders

Related areas 04 Housing, 09 Legal, 08 Welfare, 03 Finance, 01 Life Dashboard

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is LIVON free right now?

LIVON is preparing its app launch. There is no paid product on sale today; paid plans are proposals with no price set.

When is the LIVON app coming?

The app is in preparation and no launch date is fixed. It will be announced on this site and NEWON's official channels.

Are all 21 areas available now?

No. The 21 areas are a long-term plan. Guides and basic record tools for some areas are being prepared for the app launch — see the status board above, area by area.

How do the 21 areas differ from the 40 categories?

The 21 areas group features and services as business units; the 40 categories organise the information guides. Each area card lists its related category numbers.

Will LIVON apply for benefits for me?

No. LIVON helps you find and understand schemes, and links to each agency's official channel for applications and eligibility. There is no fee on benefit information.

Can I get financial, legal or property advice?

LIVON gives general information and points to official advice services. It does not sell financial products, give investment advice, sell insurance, represent anyone legally or broker property.

Where are my notes stored?

LIVON is preparing its app launch. Records are planned to stay on your device and not be sent to NEWON's servers.

Can I buy and sell used things?

LIVON does not run personal used-goods trading. For sharing, rental and personal trading, please use NEWON's SHAREON.
