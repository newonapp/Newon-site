OVERVIEW

Plenty of information.
Not enough shared criteria.

When evaluating a new market, category, or audience, material is rarely the bottleneck. The harder part is aligning on structure, priority, and what counts as evidence.

Industry reports, news, competitor IR, app reviews, community signals, and public statistics each offer a different angle — but rarely in a form teams can decide from in the same meeting.

MARKET RESEARCH starts by defining what you need to know to decide. Then we collect, compare, and structure sources into a market map, opportunity areas, and implications in one brief.

PROJECT SCOPE

A guide to project complexity.

Not fixed packages — we scope and quote based on your goal and the depth you need.

01FOCUSED MARKET BRIEF

  • Single market or category
  • 3–5 core questions
  • Desk research focus
  • Summary brief (~10–15 pages)
  • Opportunity and risk points

02MARKET & COMPETITIVE MAP

  • Segment and player map
  • Compare 5–10 key competitors
  • Positioning, pricing, messaging summary
  • Opportunity areas and implications
  • Presentation summary optional

03CUSTOM RESEARCH

  • Multiple markets or categories
  • Primary research (interviews/surveys) optional
  • Deeper trend, regulation, and risk analysis
  • Custom presentation
  • Follow-up validation or BUILD linkage
