OVERVIEW

You have the story.
Not the experience.

App ideas rarely land through slides and wireframes alone. Investors, partners, and internal stakeholders ask to try it — and engineering teams struggle to start without defined screens, flows, and states.

Building a production app from day one costs too much time and money. Figma alone often lacks interaction, transitions, and mobile context. What you need is a prototype where core flows actually work.

APP PROTOTYPE starts by defining app goals and key scenarios. We design iOS and Android screens, user flows, and interactions — then deliver a clickable prototype for sharing, validation, and dev handoff.
