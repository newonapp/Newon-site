OVERVIEW

You know the names.
Not the comparison criteria.

Competitor names come up often in product, marketing, and investment conversations. But comparing features, pricing, messaging, and target on the same criteria is harder.

Each company's site, product pages, pricing, reviews, app stores, and IR offer different angles — but rarely in a structure teams can use immediately.

COMPETITOR ANALYSIS starts by defining who to compare and on what axes. Then we collect and organize material into a Competitor Matrix, positioning summary, and gap analysis in one deliverable.
